# S16 PART B — caption drafts for the paper figure set (written 2026-09-10)

All panels are built by `scripts/s16_paper_figures.py` from the results CSVs of S1-S16; the source
file and columns are named in each caption so every number can be traced. Figures are 300 dpi,
Okabe-Ito colourblind-safe (north = blue #0072B2, central = vermillion #D55E00, nulls/controls =
grey, Frank & Brodsky reference = pink #CC79A7), 7.4 in wide, consistent fonts (9 pt base) and
panel letters a-f. Wording follows RESULTS.md; numbers are the ones stated there.

Scope statement to repeat in the paper: 27 tremor-confirmed slip patches Mw >= 6.0 in north
(>= 46N) and central (44-46N) Cascadia, from the unbridged vbICA slip-patch catalogue (thrust slip
in the trailing 30 d > 5 mm); the southern segment is excluded (model at its noise floor).

## Figure 1 — Tremor and modeled slow slip are co-located, and the geometry does not drift

**(a)** Map of the 742-triangle (0.25 deg, ~28 km) plate-interface mesh, coloured by the log10 total
number of PNSN tremor detections assigned to each triangle over 2010-2026
(`s7_occupancy_triangles.csv`: `lon`, `lat`, `N_det_tot`). Circles: the slip-weighted centroids of
the 27 confirmed slip patches (`s1_events.csv`: `slip_lon`, `slip_lat`), with area proportional to
the event's accumulated potency (`potency_km3`). Dashed lines: the 44N and 46N segment boundaries;
the southern segment is shown for context but excluded from the analysis.
**(b)** Per-event offsets of the tremor centroid from the slip-weighted slip centroid, projected on
the local down-dip direction (Q1) and in Slab2 interface depth (Q3), for the 27 confirmed thrust
patches (blue; `s1_events.csv`) and for the 40 back-slip ("mirror") control patches with tremor in
their window (grey; `s1_controls.csv`). Bars: medians (annotated), whiskers: interquartile ranges.
Tremor sits a median 4.7 km down-dip and 0.9 km deeper than the modeled slip centroid — inside one
mesh cell — whereas the back-slip control gives 18.6 km / 5.3 km with about three times the scatter,
so the co-registration is not a property of the mesh. (S1)
**(c)** Q3 depth offset against event start date, by segment; dashed line: Theil-Sen slope through
the medians. The slope is -0.04 km/yr [-0.16, +0.09], p_perm 0.58 (`s1_trends.csv`, set =
confirmed, seg = combined, quantity = Q3_depth_km); the 80 %-power minimum detectable drift is
0.2 km/yr, i.e. 3.2 km over 16 years, which is inside the PNSN location error. (S1, S13 power)
**(d)** The control that any drift claim must beat: the tremor completeness proxy per year and
segment (`s2_yearly.csv`: `completeness`), which grows through the record; dashed line marks the
2019 network step. The back-slip control set does drift (Q1 +2.9 km/yr, p 0.013) for this reason.
**(e)** Depth occupancy per year in the north: tremor depth p10-p90 band and median
(`s2_yearly.csv`: `p10`, `p50`, `p90`), the slip-weighted interface depth (`slip_depth`), and the
central-segment tremor median; complete years only (`partial == False`). The north tremor median
depth trend is +0.003 km/yr [-0.074, +0.062] (`s2_trends.csv`). Depth axis inverted (shallow up).

## Figure 2 — Tremor is a sub-linear function of slow-slip rate, and the exponent has not changed

Regressor throughout: R7, the 7-day centred mean of the area-weighted positive modeled potency rate
over the 60-km neighbourhood of the event footprint. Fits are Theil-Sen slopes of log10(measure) on
log10(R7) over the event window [start-10 d, end+10 d], on days with R7 > 0 and >= 1 detection
(>= 8 such days required).

**(a)** Daily tracks for three example events (`s16_daily_tracks.csv`; regenerated by
`scripts/s16_daily_tracks.py` because S9/S11/S16 saved only the fitted exponents — the recomputed
per-event exponents reproduce the stored ones to 8e-17). Lines: each event's Theil-Sen slope through
its median point; exponents in the legend (`s9_rate_scaling.csv`: `exponent`).
**(b)** The per-event count exponent for all 27 events, sorted, coloured by segment
(`s9_rate_scaling.csv`). Vertical line and shaded band: median 0.40 and its 90 % bootstrap CI
[0.31, 0.45]; grey band: the >= 365-d shifted-tremor null mean +- 1 sd; dashed line: b = 1
(proportionality). 27/27 events fall below 1 and 26/27 lie above their own null (sign p 4e-07)
(`s11_exponents.csv`, measure = C, slip_var = R7, subset = all).
**(c)** Four aggregations of the same relation, medians with 90 % bootstrap CIs of the median:
tremor count on 27 and on the 18 amplitude-era events, summed tremor energy, summed amplitude
(daily sum of sqrt(energy)), median energy per detection, the Frank & Brodsky amplitude proxy
A1 = sqrt(median daily energy), and median magnitude (`s11_exponents.csv`,
`s16_fb_aggregation.csv`). Dashed line b = 1; pink dash-dot line and band: the exponent implied by
Frank & Brodsky (2019), 1/3.17 with 1/(3.17 +- 0.29).
**(d)** Model-free check (S11b): the same exponents with the vbICA regressor replaced by projected
raw GNSS displacement rates and by the ICA reconstruction, at matched (31-d) and unsmoothed (1-d)
bandwidth (`s11b_exponents.csv`, subset = all). Circles: tremor count; squares: tremor energy;
medians with 90 % CIs. b = 1 is excluded with the model-free regressor at matched bandwidth
(count 0.28 [0.10, 0.51]; energy 0.30 [0.04, 0.71]).
**(e)** Stationarity: the per-event count exponent by segment and by epoch (strips = events, bars =
medians, whiskers = IQR; `s9_rate_scaling.csv`, medians aggregated here). North 0.43 vs central
0.36; epochs 0.41 / 0.41 / 0.30 (Kruskal p 0.94, NOTES S11). Dashed line: the all-event median.
**(f)** Matching Frank & Brodsky's aggregation (S16). Forward exponent of the per-detection
amplitude A1 on R7 for 1-, 3- and 7-day bins and for a single point per event (n = 18, the
granularity closest to their 8 SSEs), plus two alternative estimators at daily resolution: the
direction-symmetric reduced-major-axis slope and the reciprocal of the slope in Frank & Brodsky's
own regression direction (rate on amplitude). Pink line and band: 1/3.17 with 1/(3.17 +- 0.29).
All values from `s16_fb_aggregation.csv`; the cross-event level has no bootstrap CI.

## Figure 3 — Within-event geometry: day-scale synchrony, compressed model speed, deeper waning tail

**(a)** Distribution of the slip-minus-tremor lag L from the cross-correlation of the daily
along-strike slip-rate and tremor tracks, 27 events (`s4_fronts_events.csv`: `L_days`). Green band:
|L| <= 5 d, which holds in 63 % of events against 18.5 % under the shifted null (binomial p 4e-07;
`s4_fronts_summary.csv`, group = combined).
**(b)** Slip-centroid propagation speed against tremor-front speed per event, by segment
(`s4_fronts_events.csv`: `v_t_km_d`, `v_s_km_d`); dashed 1:1 line. Directions agree in 78 % of
events, but the modeled speed is compressed by about 6x (median ratio 0.18) — a 13-fixed-pattern
mixture artefact, not a physical speed.
**(c)** Tremor depth anomaly against phase within the event, 10 bins (`s9b_phase10.csv`,
series = depth): observed median with 90 % bootstrap CI, grey band = the 90 % range of the shifted
null. The last tenth is deeper (+4.0 km across-strike, +2.4 km after latitude-detrending,
p 0.005); the early phase is not significantly up-dip (-1.6 km, p 0.22).
**(d)** Event-stacked residual depth and down-dip distance against days from the slip-patch start,
restricted to lags with >= 10 contributing events (`s9b_downdip_stack.csv`) — the Wech & Creager
(2011) style statistic computed from a slip-anchored reference.
**(e)** Control on (c)/(d): the last-tenth-minus-main-phase depth difference against the
tremor-selection radius, for two anchors (tremor onset and the catalogue slip start)
(`s9b_sensitivity.csv`); error bars are the shifted-null sd, numbers are the null p. The sign is
stable; the significance weakens at 100 km.
**(f)** The same statistic in S9's original 5-bin form (`s9_depth_migration_stack.csv`,
profile = tremor), for comparability with (c).
Caveat to state in the caption: detection geometry in the sparse waning tail cannot be excluded,
so this is a supporting observation, not a headline result.

## Figure 4 — Model checks: what the slip model can and cannot resolve, and central Oregon

**(a)** Representability map: r30, the fraction of a synthetic 30-km slip patch at each triangle
that the 13 fixed vbICA patterns can reproduce (`s12_representability_triangles.csv`: `r30`).
Orange squares outline the central-Oregon cluster (44.3-45.4N, 27-38 km; column `s7_cluster`).
**(b)** r30 and the GNSS fractional misfit for deep triangles per 1-deg latitude band, with the
median distance to the nearest GNSS station on the top axis
(`s12_misfit_by_latitude.csv`). Shaded band: the central-Oregon latitudes. Central Oregon is not
station-starved; its lower r30 is a pattern-span property, not a coverage gap.
**(c)** Spearman rank correlation between accumulated modeled slip and completeness-normalised
tremor-days over triangles, per segment and epoch (`s7_occupancy_summary.csv`, kind =
rank_diagnostic). Co-location is weakest in the central segment (0.40-0.46) and similar in north
and south (0.70-0.77).
**(d)** Data-space check at GNSS clusters (`s12b_data_vs_model.csv`, estimator = JOINT): the
composite tremor-synchronous transient amplitude seen in raw GNSS, in the 13-component ICA
reconstruction, and in the model slip, for the central cluster and a northern control. The model
captures 63 % of the central transient (77 % in the north); the residual is 1.2 sigma and the
unmodelled-slip bound is 6 mm / 30 d on a 30-km patch.
**(e)** Inter-ETS slip and tremor confirmation (`s6_interets_conf.csv`, group = north/central,
burst_kind = slip_burst): observed tremor-confirmed fraction versus chance for the three inter-ETS
definitions. Under the leaky definition A0 (which admits real ETS days) 10/11 slip bursts are
confirmed; under the strict definitions A1/A2 there are 0-1 bursts left north of 44N, so the
geodesy resolves no inter-ETS creep that tremor can confirm at the 1-deg-band scale.
**(f)** Slip per tremor day along strike, normalised for detection growth, by epoch
(`s3_profiles.csv`: `median_eff_norm` against `lat_bin_centre`). The along-strike pattern (peaks
near 42.8-44.3N and 46.3N, trough 47.5-49N) is the same in all three epochs; the shaded band marks
the central-Oregon latitudes. Log x-axis.

## Table 1 — the 27 tremor-confirmed slip patches (`results/s16_table1.csv`)

One row per event, ordered by start date. Columns and their sources:
`no`, `id`, `start`, `end`, `seg`, `Mw`, `dur_d`, `n_tri_F` (footprint triangles),
`potency_km3`, `N` (PNSN detections during the event within 40 km of the footprint),
`Q1_downdip_km`, `Q3_depth_km`, `log10Q5` (potency per detection), `z1`, `z3` (offsets in units of
the shifted-tremor null sd) — all `s1_events.csv` (S1);
`PASS`, `overlap`, `jaccard`, `peak_mm_per30d` — the amplitude-snapshot re-derivation check
(`s15_events_check.csv`, S15A: every event is recovered with 0-day start/end offsets and footprint
overlap 1.000);
`n_days_fit`, `b_count`, `b_count_null_p` — the per-event count exponent and its shifted-null p
(`s9_rate_scaling.csv`, S9/S11);
`b_energy_sum` — summed-energy exponent, 2017+ events only (`s11_energy_events.csv`, S11);
`b_energy_median`, `b_amplitude_FB`, `beta_FB_direction` — the Frank & Brodsky aggregation: median
per-detection energy, the amplitude proxy A1, and the slope in F&B's own direction, 2017+ events
only (`s16_fb_events.csv`, S16);
`L_days`, `v_t_km_d`, `v_s_km_d`, `dir_agree`, `corr_at_L` — front lead/lag, speeds and direction
agreement (`s4_fronts_events.csv`, S4).
Values are rounded to paper precision; full precision remains in the source CSVs. NOTE the two
different tremor selections: `N` uses the S1 40-km/event-duration definition, the exponents use the
S9/S11/S16 60-km/padded-window definition.

## Table 2 — every exponent with its CI, null and provenance (`results/s16_table2.csv`)

57 rows x 23 columns, one row per (measure, aggregation, regressor). Blocks:

`bandwidth ladder (S11)` (count against R1 / R3 / cumulative slip — the errors-in-variables

`F&B direction (S16)` (the slope in F&B's regression direction and the reduced-major-axis slope);
`stationarity (S9/S11/S16)` (segment and epoch medians of the saved per-event exponents — marked

Columns: `n_events`, `b_median`, `b_q25`, `b_q75`, `b_ci90_lo/hi` (90 % bootstrap CI of the

`null_mean`, `null_sd`, `z_median`, `n_z_positive`, `n_events_z`, `sign_p`, `n_events_null_p05`
(all against the >= 365-d shifted-tremor null), `source_step`, `source_csv`, `note`.

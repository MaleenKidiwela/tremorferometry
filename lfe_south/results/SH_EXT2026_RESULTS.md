## 6. Deviations

- The download and continuous detection start on 2023-12-01, not 2024-12-01, because the join comparison needs 12 months of continuous overlap with his catalogue (v3b only has build and test days cached).
- Prep (burst muting neighbours) uses the 17 S1 stations only. v3b used the 23-station v2 set for its old stations. The continuity check measures the effect on the same days.
- The KMR re-templates keep the it0 S offset (no new S pick), as in the v3 'it1' stage.
- Everything else is the v3b it0 recipe unchanged (t2_fetch, t3_cache, sh_v2_common.prep_day, t11_mf_v2, sh_v3_common.apply_rules).

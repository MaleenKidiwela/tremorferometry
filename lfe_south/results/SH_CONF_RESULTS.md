## 1. Design

- Candidates: every ≥ 8×MAD 4-s-bin maximum of the v3b it0 run on the 385 Shelly-2025 test days 2018–2024 and of the forward run 2023-12-01..2026-09-28 (all segments: cont/A it0, NK = no KMR on the two KMR switch days, B/C = KMR re-templated). Scrambled control 'c1': the same detector (t11_mf_v2 functions, same prep, same templates) with each ≤ 3-km site's per-channel cc series rolled by its own random shift of the same day (10 min..1 day−10 min, ≥ 2 min apart; NC.KBU + PB.B049 are one site), ≥ 8×MAD, 4-s bins.
- Check: the re-run reproduces every stored candidate: max |net_mad error| 0.00e+00, max |ccsum error| 0.00e+00 (332662 of 332662 stored candidates found).
- Features (no truth-based feature): MF net_mad, mean cc, summed cc, live channels / fraction, nchan, nsta; per ≤ 3-km site (live-channel-weighted mean of the station cc at the aligned position): site_cc_max/2nd, site_z_max/2nd/min (z vs that site's cc at 1000 random origins of the same family-day), n sites z ≥ 2/3/5, frac z ≥ 3, top-site share of the sum, median site noise; hfr_rel = log10 E(10–19 Hz)/E(2–8 Hz) over the 12-s window at the family's HF station (highest pick_q with raw data) minus the median of 300 random windows of the same station-day; candidate density of the family and of its ≤ 3-km group within ±1 h and ±10 min (same day), other-family candidates within ±4 s; S1 flags (9×MAD, mean cc ≥ 0.12, summed cc ≥ 2.75, 4-s cross-family winner, in the S1 catalogue); PNET (off-the-shelf SVI `pnet_w05`, suffix _w; Mendocino fine-tune, suffix _f): max / 2nd site value, sites > 0.3 / 0.5, max site value above its random-origin median, at each station's expected S (template pick, ±0.5 s); family id (categorical). Per-station cc, z and PNET values are kept as columns.
- Labels: nearest of his events within ±2 s, same family (`m_same`) and any family (`m_any`); one model per label.
- Model: HistGradientBoosting(max_leaf_nodes=15,min_samples_leaf=200,l2=2,lr=0.05,<=300 it,early stop) + isotonic CalibratedClassifierCV(GroupKFold(3) by day). Feature sets: FULL (42); NO_PNET (32); NO_FAM (41); MF_S1 (25); PNET_W_ONLY (37).
- PNET input: the cache is the model's 20 Hz, 1–8 Hz chain; horizontals rotated to N/E with the FDSN azimuths of the day (PB EH1/EH2, BK.BRIC 7°/97° before 2024-05-21); NC.KPR / NC.KBU are vertical-only → (Z, 0, 0). Fine-tune (WA recipe): SVI start, windows on v3b BUILD days only at 14 stations (held out BK.JEWT, NC.KBU, PB.B933), positives = his events at stations whose template pick_q ≥ 3 (weight 1 if ≥ 10 else 0.5), negatives = random windows with ±3 s masks around every catalogued S, best internal-AUC checkpoint (every 10th build date).

## 7. Deviations

- One scrambled control series (c1) per day, as specified; WA used four.
- Density features are counted within the same calendar day for both runs (the v3b test days are isolated days, the forward days are contiguous), so they mean the same thing in fit and application.
- 'wsum' (WA: sum of template weights) is represented by the live channel count and live fraction at the peak (all Shelly channels carry equal weight in his sum).
- Family groups use the families_south.csv locations, ≤ 3 km epicentral (as WA); most families have 14–18 others within 3 km.
- HF stations come from the raw miniSEED day files (traces/), as WA; a family whose ranked stations lack raw data that day gets NaN.

### Adding stations made the reproduction WORSE — and why

Bed B differs from bed A only by the extra modern stations, each given a template from its own stack at his detection times.  Recall falls 0.836 → 0.621 and precision 0.708 → 0.400; families at the 30 % line 63 → 43, while the raw detection count RISES 10,841 → 13,923.  More detections, fewer of them real.
* channels averaged per family-block: median **10 → 21**; median network mean-cc at a detection **0.154 → 0.105**; median block MAD **0.0150 → 0.0113**.
* the detector's bar is 8×MAD of that same averaged trace.  Averaging ~2.1× more channels shrinks the mean-cc SIGNAL in proportion to the fraction of channels that actually carry the LFE, while the MAD of the averaged trace shrinks only as ~1/sqrt(n) — so the bar moves down more slowly than the signal does for every event the added stations do not see well.
* on the 4,206 detections the two beds share, the network mean cc falls to **×0.672** of its bed-A value while the block MAD falls to **×0.628** (pure-independent-noise prediction for the MAD: ×0.615).  The noise behaves as independent between the added channels; the signal does not fall as far as ×0.378, so the added stations DO carry LFE energy — about 0.47 of a his-station channel each.
* the extra detections are not mis-timed copies of his events: the fraction of our detections within ±2 s of one of his is 0.679 (bed A) vs 0.307 (bed B), and widening the window to ±10 s changes nothing (0.703 vs 0.312).  Bed B's surplus detections sit nowhere near his catalogue.
* **Reading:** the extra stations dilute the per-family network average.  Their channels carry roughly half the per-channel cc of his, so the averaged signal drops faster than the averaged noise for the quiet events, and the 8×MAD bar — a bar set on the same averaged trace — is then crossed by network-scale noise excursions instead.  The number of detections rises while the number of REAL ones falls.  The conclusion is not 'more stations is worse' in general but 'a per-family station set must be chosen, and a template that is not carrying that family's energy must not be averaged in with unit weight'.  No station was discarded on this result: both beds are reported in full, and the cure to test next is weighting channels by their own measured cc, not deleting them.

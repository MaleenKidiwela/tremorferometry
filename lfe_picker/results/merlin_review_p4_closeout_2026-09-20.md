# Merlin close-out review of P4 (P4-0a + P4-0b) — 2026-09-20 (verbatim; Fable 5.1 as Merlin)

**Model check**: Fable 5.1 (claude-fable-5-1), running as Merlin. Not downgraded.

## Verdict

STOP for Puget discovery is the right decision and it is over-determined — but not for the reasons the two write-ups give, and two of the failures trace to *my own* specification. The load-bearing facts that survive audit are: (1) Puget has 6–10 quality sites per sub-region at 50–65 km from the tremor field, covering ≤22 % of active cells at ≥3 sites; (2) on the north, single-event seeds starve at pass 0 (13/18 families never produce a template with ≥20 hits in 203 days) at a growth threshold the false-alarm rule never actually tested; (3) the template-free seed rule selects catalogued LFEs at 2–7 % regardless of z-band, so Puget — which has no given seeds — has no working entry point. The "purity collapse is a property of the construction" narrative in `results/P4_0B_RESULTS.md` §3/§6 must be **retracted**: two-thirds of the "impure" hits are the same family's frozen-catalogue detections below the p_real cut, and the eligible families sit 0.12–1.6 km from their neighbours, so a per-family one-to-one purity is not a measure of noise admission. No experiment on the table reverses the Puget decision; close the discovery line with one CPU-minutes re-score for the record.

## Flaws (severity-ordered)

**F1 — MAJOR (scoring truth; my spec, inherited from q_rediscover).** Arm-(i) purity counts a hit as correct only if it one-to-one matches a *p_real ≥ 0.8* detection labelled to *that one family*. I checked the frozen catalogue (`../lfe_extension/results/trackE/realness_v1_1.parquet`) on the usable episode days: **97.5 % of hp events have `n_other5 > 0`** (co-detected by another family's template within 5 s), and among the 18 eligible families the nearest-neighbour hypocentral spacing is 0.12 km (52), 0.17 km (75), 0.45 km (21), 0.92 km (36), 1.37 km (32), 1.5 km (56), 1.63 km (114/116); family 44's co-located neighbour 43 (0.79 km) is *hidden*. Consequences measured from `run_given_it2_indep.parquet`: G44_0's 114 hits match family-44 hp events at 0.298 but **family-44 frozen detections at any p_real at 0.667** (chance 0.005); REF44 gives 0.426 → 0.782, so the relative purity is 0.70 under hp-only but **0.85 under any-p — G44_0 assembles**. G75_1's 147 hits match family 75 (70), family 73 (63, 0.7 km away) and family 72 (60, 1.5 km) almost equally; under a 3-km cluster truth at any p it also assembles (0.81). G110_1 (0.70), G61_0 (0.55) and G32_1 (grew onto the 35/38/65/68 cluster) still fail. The A3 anti-correlation (purity 1.0 at 0–4 hits → 0.36 at ≥20) is exactly what an incomplete truth produces as a template becomes more sensitive; it carries no information about noise. **Verdict unchanged (≤3/18 under any truth I can construct), mechanism statement wrong.**

**F2 — MAJOR (spec, mine).** The P4-0a sub-region clause "≥4 admitted sites within 45 km of ≥50 % of active cells" is unattainable: `results/p4/shortlist_cell_geometry.csv`, set `all_candidates`, gives 0.545 (R1) / 0.34 (R3) / 0.60 (R2) with **every one of the 118 sites admitted**. R3 could never pass; R1/R2 pass only if essentially nothing is rejected. It was a bound-coverage clause misapplied as a discovery-feasibility gate. The geometry finding that stands is the admitted-set coverage itself (`subregion_summary_6day_margin.csv`: 0.024/0.083/0.000 at ≥4 sites; 0.218/0.122/0.014 at ≥3).

**F3 — MAJOR (interpretation of arm ii).** `seeds_free.parquet` vs the frozen catalogue: only **2.2 %** of the 1,500 template-free seeds fall within ±2 s of a kept-family hp event (≈0.3 % chance → ~7× enrichment, but 98 % of seeds are not catalogued LFEs), **9.7 %** within ±2 s of any frozen detection (chance ≈7 %), and the fraction is **flat across z_med bands (6.0 / 9.1 / 6.7 / 6.2 % for 4–6 / 6–8 / 8–12 / 12–25)**. The seed statistic does not select LFEs at any level, so the §5 implication ("lower the floor, raise the budget") points the wrong way. Arm (ii)'s one rediscovery (F187 → 44) is consistent with arm (i)'s per-true-seed yield (~9 % pass-0 survival) times a ~5 % LFE fraction. This is the decisive negative for Puget, where only template-free seeds exist.

**F4 — MAJOR (spec, mine; untested).** The growth threshold floor 8.0 was never exercised by the FA rule: `run_given0_quiethits.parquet` holds 13 hits ≥ 8.0 over 54 templates × 20 quiet days = **0.012 per template-day against an allowance of 0.1**, and `v_engine.run_day` (line 199) applies `max(thr, floor)` so nothing below 8.0 was ever recorded. The pass-0 starvation (median 6 independent hits, `run_given0_survival.csv`) that removed 13/18 families before iteration is therefore partly a floor convention, not a measured noise limit. The ablation (A1/A2) varied only the *survival* floor, not this one.

**F5 — MAJOR (circular reference).** `v_bank.build_ref` stacks each family's template from `eligible_families()`'s hp events; `v_score._hp_by_family` scores against the same events on the same days. The reference is trained on the test set, inflating `recall_ref`/`purity_ref` by an unknown amount and making the relative bar harder than an independent (transferred) reference would. Deviation 3 calls this "conservative"; it is, but it is not fair. The cluster re-score above suggests it is not what decides the outcome.

**F6 — MINOR.** The N-moveout null (`v_bank.build_nmoveout`) is a *window-exclusion* null, not a moveout scramble: a pass-0 template cut from a real event carries the true inter-station timing regardless of the assumed cell, so the network sum coheres at the true origin unless the shift (median 8 s, range 4–15 s in `bank_given_nm_cells.csv`) pushes the arrival outside the 20-s window at enough stations. It worked operationally (17 of 19 ≥20-hit free templates failed it; all 5 given survivors passed) but should be documented as such.

**F7 — MINOR.** Withheld-by-ρ-rank put PAN4H (57 R1 cells, third-best geometry) into the withheld pair; withheld should be chosen to minimise coverage loss. Moot.

**F8 — MINOR.** "The proxy calibrates" overstates: `rho_calibration.json` shows distance alone LOO 0.73 vs prescribed model 0.69, contrast β ≈ 0. The shortlist is a geometry ranking with a small noise correction; acceptable as used (the contrast bar is a separate hard filter), but the report headline should say so first.

**F9 — MINOR.** `hp_seed_z` is measured at the *region* moveout and in the 1–8 Hz cache, so "64.6 % of true LFEs below z_med 4" is the operational number for the free-arm rule, not the LFE amplitude distribution. Immaterial given F3.

**F10 — SPECULATIVE.** `DayData.station_z` (v_engine.py 91–102) computes cc over all samples including gaps (cc = 0 there); on gappy station-days this deflates the MAD and inflates z. Not verified; the quiet-day FA rule would not catch a day-specific effect.

What was done right and should be credited: freeze/hash discipline before every pass; per-day availability not intersected; seed cap works (max 3/hour, top-10-hours 2 %); burst guard reported; documented deviations honest; ablation isolated with hashes verified; and **F187 → family 44 from a seed 184 s from any catalogued hp event** is a genuine positive datum — growth from a non-catalogued window converged on a real family (34 matches vs null max 2, 100 % of hits tremor-locked at median 21 PNSN epicentres within 60 km).

## Q1 — P4-0a

The 50 % clause was the wrong bar for a discovery attempt (F2). A cell-restricted attempt is legitimate *in principle* — the natural unit is a contiguous cluster of tremor-active cells with ≥4 admitted sites ≤45 km plus 2 withheld ≤60 km — and R1 would offer 4 cells at ≥4 sites (36 at ≥3), R3 13 (19). But it is worth nothing now: P4-0b shows the construction has no template-free entry point (F3) and starves on a record three times longer than Puget's BUILD (~67 tremor days). Legitimate, moot.

Far-tremor bar: **accept the intent substitution** (local − far ≥ 3 dB). The absolute ≤+1 dB clause sits inside the 3.4 dB quiet-day scatter of the same statistic and flipped six unambiguous local responders (B005/B006/LRIV/SKOKO/HDW/HERD, margins 6.5–10.4 dB) when two quiet days were added; the differential reading is what the clause was for. Both readings were carried; neither changes the outcome.

## Q2 — P4-0b

**(a)** `v_engine`/`v_run` implement the statistic I specified (lowhalf-MAD z per station-day, site mean, clip 8, equal-weight sum, ≥2 sites z≥3 coincidence, ≥3 sites observable, 30/60 s decluster/independence, FA-only T). Deviations: (1) depth 35.3 km — correct and necessary; (2) net_mad conversion — reasonable, keeps one threshold comparable across 5/6/7-site days, saturation ~20 irrelevant at T = 8; (3) truth-stack reference — circular (F5), harder not unsound; (4) one-offset matching — harmless (33 vs 34). None makes the bar unreachable; F1 and F4 do more damage than all four together, and both are mine.

**(b)** Mostly the **purity definition** (F1): 67 % of G44_0's hits are frozen family-44 detections; matched vs unmatched hits differ only modestly (net_mad 12.1 vs 10.4; 5 vs 4 coincident sites), i.e. the template is picking up weaker events of the same source as it improves. The residual ~27 % unexplained (vs 3 % chance) is unresolved — events the frozen chain missed or noise. The 203-day record and the untested 8.0 floor (F4) are what starved pass 0; the two-iteration cap and the equal-weight/z-clip statistic are not implicated by anything measured.

**(c)** The ablation's mechanics are sound (pre-registered, hashes verified, one parameter moved). It settles that the *survival* floor alone does not rescue the hp-only scoring. It does not settle "the floor was not the cause": it kept the hp-only single-label truth and the 8.0 growth floor, and its inference "the 0/18 is a purity result" is the F1 artefact.

**(d)** Arm (ii) failed at the **seed stage**, not at growth (F3). Lowering the seed floor would not have helped — the LFE fraction is flat in z. The correct reading of §5 is "there is no energy-based seed rule for LFEs on this network", which for Puget is terminal.

**None changes the decision.** Both STOPs are independent of each other and of the flaws above, and Puget cannot supply what the north lacked (years on admitted stations, given seeds).

## Q4 — standing conclusion for the user (plain language)

"We tested whether new LFE families can be found without a pre-existing catalogue — first in the north, where Bostock's families are truth, then as a plan for Puget. They cannot, with the tools we have. Pairwise linkage (P3D) found no clusters. The real seed-and-grow construction, run on the north with 7 good sites over 203 episode days, assembled at most 2–3 of 18 known families from single true events (13 never produced a template that survived the first pass), and a template-free seed rule picked windows that were catalogued LFEs only 2–7 % of the time, rediscovering one family. Puget has 6–10 usable sites per sub-region at 50–65 km from the tremor field, covering at most a fifth of the active cells with three sites, about 67 build-period tremor days, and no seeds. The Puget discovery line is closed. The north negative is a power-and-seed result, not proof that the construction is intrinsically incapable — the earlier 'purity collapse' reading was a scoring artefact and is withdrawn. The only route to LFE families that has worked in this project remains growth from known events or stacks (the transferred-template catalogue)."

Implications for the parked items:
- **Front estimator v2** (`lfe_extension/results/trackD/RESULTS.md` §3b-2): independent of P4; resume it. One P4-0b by-product matters to it: 97.5 % of hp events are detected by ≥2 family templates and neighbouring families sit 0.1–1.6 km apart, so per-family "first activation" is not independent across families — the estimator must de-duplicate events (`n_other5`) or cluster families at ≤3 km before fitting a front.
- **Far-south existence test** (`results/o4_deferred.md`): P4-0b makes it *more* valuable, not less — stacking at known detection times is the regime that worked (truth-stack recall 0.45; F187 → 44), and Shelly's 27 families (~2,300 detections each, on disk) are stack-grade truth with an external denominator. The download (B045/B935/RVIT/PETY, Shelly-active windows 2018–2024, ~50–60 GB, ~1 h FDSN) is justified as the only remaining route to a non-northern family set; it is an existence test, not discovery. My prior that ≥10/27 register at ≥2 stations is ~40 % — unverified, I have not checked station-family distances. Requires the user's download authorisation.

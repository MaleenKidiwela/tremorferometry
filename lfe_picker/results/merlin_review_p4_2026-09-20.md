# Merlin review of the P4 (Puget/Olympic) design — 2026-09-20 (verbatim; Fable 5.1 as Merlin)

**Model check**: Fable 5.1 (claude-fable-5-1), running as Merlin. Not downgraded.

**Verdict.** P4 is viable as a **bounded, injection-calibrated** discovery attempt, but the premise the P4 plan inherits from P3D — "seed-and-grow has demonstrated linkage power (14.5×); pairwise kNN percolates" — is false as stated: what P3D ran under the name ARM B was a *seed-restricted pairwise graph*, not Bostock's construction (matched-filter growth + iterated stacks + Gate-1 acceptance). The pre-registered Arm B (`results/p3d/arm_b_preregistration.md` steps 2–5) has **never been executed**, and its seed set as built was earthquakes and bursts, not LFEs. The single decision that matters: **run the true construction on the north first (data on disk, ~1 GPU-day), with kept-family truth, before a single Puget byte is spent on discovery.** If it cannot assemble families where linked pairs are known to exist at 14.5× chance, Puget is not a discovery test — it is a power-limited bound, and should be run and reported as such.

---

## Flaws (severity-ordered)

**F1 — MAJOR (premise). "Seed-and-grow" in P3D was not seed-and-grow.** `scripts/s_discover_pairs.py:186–190, 227` show `--seed-rows` merely restricts the i-side of the *pairwise* correlation to seed windows; `s_discover_chain.py:27–46` then builds kNN components — the same graph construction as ARM A. No template was ever run over the record, no stack was iterated, no per-template threshold existed. Consequences: (a) the 14.5× enrichment (`disc_Mseed_true_killtest.json`: 196/476 both-labelled edges) is a *pair-linkage* number and tells nothing about whether growth assembles a family; (b) "percolation at 1e-4" (`P3D_DISCOVERY_RESULTS.md` E2) is a graph property the real construction cannot have; (c) the P4 plan text (PLAN.md lines 191–193) is conflating the two and would carry a false confidence into Puget. `disc_Mseed_true_chain.json`: 0 components — the construction as run produced nothing.

**F2 — MAJOR. The seed statistic selected earthquakes and bursts, not LFEs.** `disc_M_seeds.parquet`: seed_z ranges 10.3 → **882,226**; 456 of 600 possible windows in one hour were seeds; the top 10 hours hold 2,097 of 9,874 seeds; the top 5 days hold 2,856. `disc_B_seeds.parquet`: max seed_z 2.28e6, 471 seeds in one hour. A 12-s window with median-over-sites energy z ≥ 30 is a regional earthquake, teleseism, or glitch — an LFE is a ~2σ event at the best permanent site (PLAN.md line 8). The "top 1 % by median z" rule (`s_discover_seeds.py`, `--top-frac 0.01`) is the wrong tail. Also `s_discover_seeds.py:53–58` computes the day background median/MAD over *all* windows two-sided — the loud-day collapse flagged in `merlin_review_p1p2_2026-09-19.md` flaw 3 (lowhalf exists and was not used).

**F3 — MAJOR. The S-excess clause on detection-network sites is not evidence, whether at 3 or N sites.** Hits are *selected* by correlation with the template, so their stack necessarily shows the template's arrival at detection sites (selection bias) — for real sources and for burst templates alike; and `s_discover_chain.py:79–82` computed the excess *after* `align_stack`, which is how NET-D 51599 passed at three sites with no S at PGC alignment-free (E3). Fixing "exactly 3 sites → ≥3 of N" does not repair this. The clause must become a cheap **screen**; evidence lives only at withheld sites (Gate 1), in the N-shift count, and in a per-template moveout-scramble null (below).

**F4 — MAJOR. The engine is hard-wired to the north.** `s_discover_pairs.py:51–59` defines tremor hours by distance to `S.families()` (Bostock positions); `:92–99` uses a *single* grid centre from `p_common.GRID_LAT/GRID_LON` (48.0–49.0 / −124.5..−123.0, `p_common.py:210–211`) for station constants; `g_gates.py:79–84` `GateConfig` has a north-centred 124-km location grid and a fixed `sites` tuple; `g_common.station_corrections()` (`g_common.py:69–77`) builds terms only for the 15 stations in `a5_station_corrections.csv`. Puget spans ~145 km along strike (S-time variation at one station across the region ≈ 10–15 s > half the 24-s window), so one grid centre mis-cuts. Everything must be re-pointed by override tables, not edits (the P3D pattern).

**F5 — MAJOR. The site-noise/registration proxy is unvalidated.** The P3D class+distance model missed SYMB by 3.1 dB — the full 6.5 dB site-term spread (`P3D_RESULTS.md` §1.4, §3.2). P4-0 proposes PSD-vs-PGC and tremor-contrast proxies (PLAN.md line 191) but there is no evidence either predicts ρ. The north has **14 stations with measured single-event ρ** (§3.2, §A3.2: LHLYB 1.09, DAOB 0.83, SYMB 0.78, CLRS 0.77, GOBB 0.57, TURTL 0.56, RNWY 0.49, LOPEZ 0.40, LRIV 0.37, BUCKS 0.30, PTRF 0.28, OSQM 0.17, ODUC 0.16, plus PGC/VGZ). The proxy must be calibrated there before it is allowed to select Puget stations.

**F6 — MAJOR. Gate 1 cannot return FAIL in a region with no control table.** `g_gates.py:305–316`: with `ctx.controls` None, `_gate1_control` returns `available=False`, so `ctl.get("any_pass", False)` is False and every non-pass becomes UNTESTABLE. `GATES_RESULTS.md` §6 item 7 says the control table is regional. Without a Puget control table the gate has a pass side and no fail side. Injection-built controls fix this (prescription).

**F7 — MINOR (premise correction). "No published families" is true for event times, false for locations.** `codex-LFE/results/olympic_reference_discovery_v1/report.json`: Chestler & Creager 2017 `LFEsAll.mat` "identified, not acquired; available upon request" — so no truth, correct. But 69 derived Chestler coordinate rows (no family IDs, "not a catalog") are on disk (`derived_coordinate_rows_not_a_catalog.csv`: 47.7–48.3 N, −123.4..−122.63, Slab2 depth median 40.4 km). I computed modern sites within 45 km hypocentral of those rows: OSQM+B001 44 rows, CHIMA 37, B013+B943 27, EQUIL 27, ODEER 25, NATEM 16, LRIV cluster 11, DOSE 7, HDW 5. Usable as a *descriptive* spatial prior and a pre-registered prediction ("if real families exist, some will fall within 5 km of a Chestler row"), never as a bar. Separately, `P3_RESULTS.md` §6.3 item 5 ("Ducellier 2004–2011 covers the Olympic segment") is wrong: `catalogs/ducellier_catalog/README.md` says Southern Cascadia.

**F8 — MINOR. Puget geometry is not uniform; 47.5–48.0 N is weak.** From `cell_geometry_by_year.csv` (all-network, tremor-active cells): 47.0–47.5 N in 2023/2025 median 4 sites ≤45 km, p90 8–8.6, 67–68 % of cells ≥3 sites, gap 68°, n60 median 16; 48.0–48.3 N in 2025 median 4, 77 % ≥3, gap 69°; **47.5–48.0 N in 2025 median 2, 45 % ≥3, gap 105°**. In 2021 all three bands were at median 1–2. So the discovery window is effectively 2022/2023 onward, and 47.5–48.0 N should be a secondary sub-region.

**F9 — SPECULATIVE (not read). Behaviour of Gate machinery for stations absent from the a5 file.** I did not read the consumers of `station_corrections()`; a Puget station may raise or silently default. The executor must verify the fallback is 0.0 with a logged flag before any Gate 1/2 run.

## Prescription

Governance throughout: agents write a freeze file with hashes *before* each pass, launch a shell queue, and exit; one scoring pass per stage. 2013 stays sealed; the 30 hidden northern families stay hidden; Puget TEST period (below) is sealed until P4-2 step 7.

### P4-0a — Station shortlist by a *calibrated* site proxy (CPU, ≤1 day)

**What.** (1) Calibrate first, on the north: for the 14 stations with measured ρ, compute on data already in `cache20hz`/`traces` two source-agnostic statistics over the same S18/S21/S22 days: (i) **quiet-night noise**: median 2–8 Hz power per component over local 00–05 h on days with no PNSN tremor within 150 km, divided by the StationXML overall sensitivity (scalar; broadband only — flag EH/short-period for full response removal or exclusion), expressed in dB relative to PGC on the same nights, horizontals and vertical separately; (ii) **tremor contrast**: median 2–8 Hz 3-C energy in 5-min windows co-timed with PNSN detections within 40 km hypocentral of the station, divided by the median over matched quiet 5-min windows (same hour-of-day, ±30 days, no tremor within 150 km), in dB; plus the same contrast for tremor located 100–200 km away (must be ≈0 dB) and in 10–19 Hz (must be lower than in 2–8 Hz). Fit ρ against hypocentral distance, noise term, contrast; report Spearman(proxy, ρ) with n=14 and the leave-one-out prediction error in ρ. (2) Then probe Puget: every 3-C seismometer site in 46.8–48.5 N / −124.6..−122.0 alive through 2026 (`results/p3d_puget_geometry/sites.csv`; 127 sites in the box, ~70 alive; accelerometers excluded, `Z2`/`X4` temporaries excluded), 12 station-days each: 6 tremor days (2 from each of 2023-03/04, 2024-08, 2025-09/10, chosen with ≥100 epicentres within 40 km of the station where possible) + 6 matched quiet days. Read dip/azimuth from StationXML for every channel (the PQ +90 lesson), verify delivery, compute (i) and (ii). Rank by *predicted ρ* from the north fit, applied per station to the nearest tremor-active cells.

**Why.** Site noise, not distance, decided P3D; the proxy is only worth using if it ranks the 14 known stations.

**Accept.** Calibration: Spearman ≥ 0.6 with the expected signs (noise negative, contrast positive), LOO error in ρ/ρ_PGC ≤ 0.25 median. Shortlist = sites with predicted ρ/ρ_PGC ≥ 0.5, tremor contrast ≥ +3 dB on ≥4 of 6 tremor days, far-tremor contrast ≤ +1 dB, delivery ≥ 90 %, metadata parsed. Add every long-record PB borehole in the box regardless of rank (B001, B003, B004, B005–B007, B013/B943, B014, B017, B018, B941) but flag them "backfill only" if they fail the bars. Sub-regions (≤50 km, one station-constant each): R1 = 47.0–47.5 N (Shelton–Hood Canal), R2 = 48.0–48.3 N (north Olympics), R3 = 47.5–48.0 N (secondary). A sub-region proceeds only if ≥4 admitted sites lie within 45 km hypocentral of ≥50 % of its tremor-active cells and ≥2 further admitted sites exist to withhold.

**Fail signature.** Calibration Spearman < 0.4 → the proxy is not predictive; fall back to *measured* geometry + tremor contrast only, admit the top 8 per sub-region, and state in the report that station selection was uncalibrated. All three sub-regions failing the ≥4+2 rule → STOP P4.

### P4-0b — The true Arm B kill test on the north (GPU ≈ 1 day; data on disk) — **the gate for P4-2**

**What.** Implement the construction as pre-registered in `arm_b_preregistration.md` steps 2–5, with the corrections below, and run it on the PQ-era north (sites PGCq, VGZ, SYMB, BUCKS, LHLYB, ALBHB, DAOB with per-day availability, ≥3 sites observable — do **not** intersect days across stations, which is what starved NET-B), pooled S18+S21+S22, kept families only. Two seed arms: (i) *given* seeds — one window at each of 3 hp events (`p_real ≥ 0.8`) per kept family with ≥50 hp events in the pooled episodes; (ii) *template-free* seeds by the P4 seed rule (P4-2 step 2). Growth, iteration, dedupe, screen, nulls exactly as in P4-2 steps 3–6. Score (i) with the relative clauses of `q_rediscover.py` (recall ≥0.5× and purity ≥0.8× the family's transferred template on the same days, withheld sites excluded from detection), score (ii) by one-to-one ±2 s matches to kept-family hp events (≥30 % of the family's hp events, ≥20 matches).

**Why.** Linkage at 14.5× chance is known to exist here; if growth from a *true* seed cannot assemble the family, nothing in Puget will, and the P4 look would be spent on a broken tool.

**Accept.** (i) ≥2/3 of eligible kept families assembled; (ii) ≥3 kept families rediscovered template-free with ≤1 alias (a grown template matching two families) per rediscovery. Both → P4-2 prior ≈ 30 %. (i) pass, (ii) fail → seeds are the bottleneck; P4-2 runs as an injection-calibrated bound, prior ≤10 %; also report which seed-z band the true hp events occupy (this fixes the P4 seed band empirically). (i) fail → STOP; do not run P4-2 until the construction is repaired and re-passes.

**Fail signature to report immediately.** Given-seed growth returns >50 % of hits within ±10 min of the seed (burst convergence) for the majority of families — the decluster/independence guards are not working.

### P4-1 — Download for the shortlist (batch; ≤1 day wall, ~250 GB)

**What.** Episodes = runs of Puget-band (47.0–48.3 N) PNSN days with ≥50 epicentres, merged across gaps ≤7 d (I computed 50 runs, 213 tremor days, 37,842 epicentres; 13 runs with ≥7 tremor days: 2020-05-22..28, 2020-10-05..18, 2020-10-31..11-10, 2021-09-26..10-10, 2021-10-20..26, 2022-05-21..06-05, 2023-03-29..04-30 (24 d), 2023-07-18..27, 2024-01-02..17, 2024-08-09..24 (16 d), 2025-05-10..21, 2025-07-27..08-08, 2025-09-17..10-08 (21 d)). Pull all 213 tremor days ±2 d pads, 60 quiet days (no tremor within 150 km ±2 d; ≥8 per year 2022–2026, matched by season), and the days of ~30 USGS intraslab earthquakes M≥2.5, depth 25–60 km, inside the box (for a5 corrections). Shortlist (~18–22 stations incl. withheld and boreholes) × ~330 days ≈ 7,000 station-days ≈ 145 GB raw + 145 GB 20-Hz cache (P3 §6.2 rates: 20.5 + 20.7 MB/station-day; ~4 station-days/s FDSN). Frozen chain (`o_cache_month.cache_one` via `s_cache.py`), dip sign honoured, per-station-day coverage_frac mask.

**Split, hashed before download completes.** BUILD = 2022-01-01..2024-12-31 (5 major episodes, ~67 tremor days); TEST = 2025-01-01..2026-05-27 (4 episodes, ~41 tremor days, sealed); RESERVE = 2020–2021 (4 major episodes, ~51 tremor days) for P4-3 backfill only. The network in 2020–2021 was median 1–2 sites (F8), so pooling it into BUILD buys little and costs a clean second holdout.

**Accept.** ≥90 % of BUILD tremor station-days delivered for ≥4 admitted sites per sub-region. **Fail.** A sub-region's admitted sites deliver <70 % → drop the sub-region before P4-2.

### P4-2 — Seed-and-grow with pre-registered nulls, injections, and gates (GPU ≈ 1.5–2 days as a queue; one scoring pass)

Order the steps so the nulls and injections are in the queue *before* the true run's scoring.

**Step 1 — Freeze.** Hash: shortlist and site merge (1-km rule), withheld sites (2 per sub-region, chosen as ranks 3–4 by predicted ρ so the best 2 remain in the sum; never in any sum, never used for seeds), sub-region centres, BUILD/TEST/RESERVE, seed rule, growth threshold rule, iteration count (2), dedupe rule, screen, null definitions and realisation counts, claim rule, Gate settings, injection design, the Chestler descriptive prediction.

**Step 2 — Seeds (the corrected rule).** Candidate windows: 12-s at 6-s hop on PNSN tremor hours with ≥5 epicentres within 50 km of the sub-region centre (I count 2,746 such hours band-wide: R1 843, R3 1,269, R2 963), BUILD days only. Per-site 3-C 2–8 Hz energy z at the sub-region moveout, MAD-normalised per station-day on the **lower half** of the day's window distribution. Seed = window with ≥3 admitted sites individually z ≥ 3, median over sites in the band **4 ≤ z_med ≤ 25** (above 25 is earthquake/glitch class — P4-0b reports where real hp events fall and may tighten this), no USGS/PNSN catalogued earthquake within ±60 s, HF ratio E(10–19)/E(2–8) < 3.5 at the two nearest stations (native-rate read). Cap: ≤3 seeds per hour per sub-region, ≥120 s apart; budget ≤1,500 seeds region-wide, ordered by z_med within the band. Report the seed time histogram (max seeds/hour, top-10-hours fraction) — if the top 10 hours hold >20 % of seeds the cap is not working.

**Step 3 — Growth pass 0 (BUILD days, all hours).** Template = per-station 20-s window from predicted S−6 s at the sub-region moveout (longer than 12 s because the true cell may be ~25 km from the centre), all admitted sites of the sub-region except withheld. Matched filter with the frozen runner's statistic: per-station 3-C cc series, MAD-normalised per station-day (lowhalf), per-site mean, **per-site z clipped at 8** (anti-dominance), summed over sites with equal weights. Hit = network sum ≥ T_seed **and** ≥2 sites with per-site z ≥ 3 at the hit time (coincidence). T_seed per template by the frozen FA-only rule (`c_tiers_by_epoch.py` docstring): smallest T on a 0.25 grid ≥ 8.0 with ≤0.1 false hits per template-day on the 60 quiet days. Decluster hits at 30 s; define **independent hits** as ≥60 s apart. Compute at ~110 template-station-days/s (`logs/s5b_dense.log`: 351 s for a 31-day month × 1,165–1,378 (fam,sta) on 20–24 stations): 1,500 × 8 × ~200 days ≈ 2.4e6 → ~6 h.

**Step 4 — Survival to iteration.** Keep a template only if: ≥20 independent hits; hits on ≥3 distinct days and ≥2 episodes; ≤20 % of hits within ±10 min of the seed; **N-moveout per template**: re-cut the seed with per-station timing from a wrong cell (predicted S differing ≥5 s at ≥ half the stations), grow identically, require true independent hits ≥ 3× scrambled. This is the direct burst guard — a tremor-character template is insensitive to inter-station offsets, a source is not.

**Step 5 — Iterate exactly twice.** `align_stack` (2 iterations, ±1.5 s, the frozen recipe) on the hits at each detection site → re-pick S on the aligned stack at the highest-S-SNR site → re-cut 12-s templates from S−2 s → re-grow with the same threshold rule. No third iteration; convergence is a forking path. Then dedupe: two templates are one family if declustered hit sets overlap ≥50 % (±2 s) or stacks correlate above the per-station cross-family p95 at ≥ half of the registering sites (recompute the p95 table for Puget stations from the grown bank, as `g_crossfam.py` did); keep the larger.

**Step 6 — Screen, then evidence.** Screen (cheap, not evidence): alignment-free stack of independent hits at each detection site at the hit-implied time shows ≥+3 dB S excess at ≥3 sites *or* ≥50 % of registering sites. Evidence: (a) **Gate 1** at the sub-region's 2 withheld sites, alignment-free, ±1.5 s constant-offset search charged to 200 random-time and 100 moveout-shuffled nulls (the `s_discover_score.withheld_excess` path), on independent hits, PASS at ≥2 withheld sites (Bostock-construction prereg (a)); (b) **N-shift**: whole chain (seeds, growth, iterations, dedupe, gates) on data with station k shifted by k×1,800 s, **≥3 realisations** (the §A5 protocol correction: report mean, max, and per-realisation counts); (c) **N-quiet**: whole chain with seeds drawn from quiet hours; (d) Gate 0 in Puget = HF class veto (median per-member, native rate) + earthquake-catalogue veto (reject if >10 % of independent hits fall within ±10 s of a catalogued event) + distinctness against P4's own accepted list, re-screened as it grows; (e) Gate 3 over the evaluated BUILD calendar (span >61 d, so the circular-shift null is valid); soft, as designed.

**Step 7 — Injections = chain power + Gate-1 control table (in the same queue, scored in the same pass).** Transplant *distinct* real hp events (never the stack — an injected stack correlates perfectly anywhere, Q2(ii)) of kept northern families with ≥100 hp events into BUILD station-days of the shortlist at randomly drawn tremor-active Puget cells, using the `p2_train.Transplanter` mechanism (`scripts/p2_train.py:33–43`) generalised to arbitrary cells and recipient stations: donor station class matched to recipient class (borehole→borehole, broadband→broadband), amplitude scaled so each event's 3-C S-window energy excess at the recipient equals the donor event's own excess at the donor station (natural per-event class), with variants −3 dB and −6 dB. 12 synthetic families (4 per sub-region) × N ∈ {25, 50, 100} × 3 amplitudes, spread over ≥4 tremor days and ≥2 episodes. Recovery curve = fraction assembled and Gate-1-passed vs (N, amplitude). Separately inject at the withheld sites at matched N and distance to build `g_gate1_controls.csv`'s Puget analogue (columns as in the existing file) so Gate 1 can return FAIL honestly (F6). Label everything "chain power calibration — not a completeness statement" (the E4/E5 injection-anchor lesson).

**Step 8 — Claim rule (pre-registered).** *Discovery*: Gate 1 PASS at ≥2 withheld sites; independent-hit count ≥ N-shift max + 3 **and** N-shift mean ≤10 % of the true count; N-quiet yields no cluster at that cell; Gate 0 vetoes silent; N-moveout ratio ≥3; **and** the final template, run on the sealed TEST period with withheld sites still excluded, produces independent hits ≥ its own TEST N-shift max + 3 and Gate 1 passes on TEST hits alone at ≥1 withheld site; Gate 2 PASS if N ≥ 150 (location), else the family is a *located-provisionally* discovery with the cell from Gate 2's energy back-projection and LOSO spread reported. *Candidate*: passes Gate 1 + N-shift in BUILD but fails or is untestable on TEST re-fire, or Gate 2 untestable with N < 150. Everything else is unlisted. TEST is opened once, after BUILD scoring is written and hashed.

**Gate 2 specifics.** Corrections: run the `a5_eq_corrections.py` recipe on the ~30 Puget intraslab events with the shortlist; accept a station's term at n ≥5 picks and MAD ≤0.5 s, else 0.0 with σ_S inflated to 0.6 s and the flag "uncorrected". Geometry is not the limit (n60 median 11–16); N is. Pre-register an ablation on the injections: pick on native-rate 2–15 Hz stacks (the a5 recipe) versus the 20 Hz cache, to see whether pick yield at N=50–100 improves — unverified.

**Accept P4-2.** Injection recovery ≥50 % at natural amplitude, N=50 → the run has power and any null result is a bound. **Fail signatures.** Recovery <50 % at natural amplitude N=100 → power-limited; report the bound and stop before opening TEST unless a BUILD candidate exists. N-shift realisations producing gate-passing clusters → the chain is manufacturing sources; fix before any claim.

## Compute and data (Q5)

Downloads ≈ 290 GB, ~1 h FDSN. GPU: P4-0b ≈ 18–24 h; P4-2 growth pass 0 ≈ 6 h, N-shift ×3 ≈ 18 h, N-quiet ≈ 2 h, N-moveout ≈ 6 h, iterations on survivors (expect ≤10 %) ≈ 2 h, injections ≈ 6 h, TEST scan of final templates ≈ 1 h → ≈ 40 GPU-h as one queue. Disk is not a constraint (71 TB free).

## Honest prior and STOP criteria (Q6)

P(≥1 *discovery* under the full claim rule) ≈ 25–35 % if P4-0b passes both arms; ≤10 % if only the given-seed arm passes; 0 by decision if it fails. P(≥1 *candidate*) ≈ 50 %. P(a publishable, injection-calibrated bound of the form "no repeating source with ≥N events at ≥X dB in sub-region R during 2022–2024") ≈ 90 %. Grounds: Bostock's families were built with typically five sites ≤45 km (`p3d_puget_geometry/SUMMARY.md` §1b) — R1 and R2 now have that geometry — but on surface UW broadbands in a populated lowland whose site terms are unknown, with single-event registration ρ ≈ 0.3–0.4 on this station class in the north, and with a construction that has not yet assembled a family anywhere.

STOP: P4-0a leaves no sub-region with ≥4 admitted + 2 withheld sites; P4-0b given-seed arm fails; P4-2 injections show <50 % recovery at natural amplitude N=100; P4-2 true run yields no candidate — report the bound and stop.

Report to the user: the P3D premise correction (ARM B was pairwise; seeds were earthquakes); the north kill-test result in both arms; the calibrated proxy table with Spearman; the shortlist with predicted ρ; the injection power curve per sub-region; candidates and discoveries with every null count; what data were spent (BUILD/TEST) and what remains sealed.

## Experiments I could not run

- **North kill test (P4-0b)** — decides everything. Pass/pass → proceed; pass/fail → seeds are the problem, use the hp-event z band it reveals; fail → construction broken.
- **Proxy calibration on the 14 known stations** — Spearman ≥0.6 licenses proxy-based selection; <0.4 means select by geometry+contrast and say so.
- **Seed z band of real hp events** (from P4-0b): if median-z of true LFE windows on 3+ sites is typically <4, the seed rule must lower its floor and the seed budget must rise — report before freezing P4-2.
- **Gate-2 native-rate pick ablation on injections** — whether location becomes reachable at N≈50–100.

## What would change my mind

If P4-0b assembles ≥2/3 of eligible kept families from given seeds *and* rediscovers ≥3 template-free with the corrected seed band, I would raise the Puget prior to ~40 % and drop the "bound-first" framing. If the injection recovery at natural amplitude exceeds 80 % at N=50 in R1, I would accept a null Puget result as strong evidence that Puget LFE families at that amplitude class do not exist or are far smaller than the north's. Conversely, if the corrected seeds in the north still concentrate in a few hours despite the caps, the seed concept itself — not its parameters — is wrong for a permanent network, and P4 should pivot to growth from *transferred* northern-family-class templates placed at Puget cells (moveout-only seeds), which I have not analysed here.

# Merlin review of lfe_picker/PLAN.md — 2026-09-17 (verbatim; Fable 5.1 as Merlin)

**Verdict.** The plan is salvageable but two of its load-bearing premises are false, and both are checkable from disk: (1) the "√N coherent gain left on the table" is already realised by the template network sum (single-station registration ~2.1 MAD → network 4.4–6.2 MAD ≈ √6 × 2.1; E5, `lfe_catalog_repair/RESULTS.md` lines 855–861); (2) "Lin's ~90 %" is a window-classification accuracy on his own labelled test windows, not completeness — his published catalog recovers, chance-free, only ~10–15 % of Bostock's events at ±10 s (computed here from the two on-disk catalogs, all four years), i.e. LESS than the template catalog's ~20 %. The single decision that matters: re-scope the learned model as a source-agnostic SEED/DISCOVERY engine (per-station learned characteristic function + explicit physics back-projection on the Slab2 interface, never a learned location head), keep the frozen template machinery as the per-family production detector everywhere, and gate the whole phase on a CPU-hours oracle (P0-O below) that bounds the achievable per-station gain before any GPU-day is spent.

## Flaws (severity-ordered)

**F1 — FATAL as a target: "toward Lin's ~90 %".** Matched `lfe_catalog_repair/results/external/lin_catalog/EQloc_001_0.1_3_S.csv` (OT) against Bostock DB time + the 2012 family offsets with a ±120 s pair histogram and background subtraction. Chance-free recall of Bostock events within ±10 s: 2010 0.11 (N≥3), 0.13 (N≥4), 0.14 (N≥5); 2011 0.14/0.15/0.08; 2013 0.11/0.10/0.06; ±20 s adds ≤0.05. Peak/background 1.2–1.8. Lin's 90 %/95 % is accuracy on 15-s test windows centred on catalogued picks, split by event ID within the same families and days. Every P1/P2 bar phrased as "toward 90 %" is unreachable on any station class. Caveat (SPECULATIVE): a per-family alignment fitted on 2012 and applied to 2010/2011/2013 could raise these numbers somewhat, not to 0.9.

**F2 — FATAL as a premise: the coherent-array gain is not unexploited.** The matched filter sums 3-C cc across sites with the true moveout; that IS a matched-field beamformer for known sources. Per-event coherent amplitude at a withheld station (Track A 3b): +7.57 dB with N≈62 ⇒ a/σ≈0.28 (−11 dB); incoherent 6-s S-window power excess at PGC 1.57–2.46× (E4 step 3). Headroom for ANY learned detector on known families: (i) template-mismatch loss (single event vs stack), (ii) the false-alarm floor (quiet-day FA at 5–6 MAD is 70–700/template/day; HF veto already cut 60 %). To reach 90 % the typical event (5–6 MAD) must clear threshold: +6 dB per station or ~1000× FA reduction at 4–5 MAD. A learnable transient discriminator plausibly buys ~10× FA ⇒ threshold 8.25 → ~7–7.5 MAD ⇒ recall 0.20 → ~0.30–0.35. Geometry confirms the ceiling: nearest roster site to a Bostock family median 24 km (p10 12, p90 38); 71/130 families have ≤1 site within 30 km. Honest completeness ceiling on the north permanent network ~0.30–0.40 of Bostock; the plan's payoff is discovery, not completeness.

**F3 — MAJOR: idea 1 (learned source-grid output) rebuilds the E3 location pathology in learned form.** Labels exist at 130 positions only (family NN distance median 2.5 km, 54 % <3 km). A cell output supervised only there learns the position prior and collapses discovered events onto known families — E3's failure, now unfalsifiable because learned. P5 discovery locations would be uninterpretable.

**F4 — MAJOR: the cross-station-prediction pretext selects for the wrong signal.** Most predictable 1–8 Hz energy: (a) intra-site coherence in the PGC/B009/B010/B011 and LRIV/B005/B006/B007 clusters (the site-coherent noise of E5 step 5), (b) regional earthquakes/blasts. LFEs are a negligible fraction of predictable energy; the encoder has no incentive to represent them. Pretraining sets must exclude 2011/2013 traces.

**F5 — MAJOR: label inventory overstated/mis-specified.** (a) 150k of 269k Bostock events are 2003–2005, before B926/NLLB/LRIV existed, none on disk; usable 2007–2013 ≈120k; on disk 2010–2013 ≈80k (+2008 14k, 2009 22k downloadable). (b) SeisBench stack picks exist for PGC/VGZ/SNB/LZB/YOUB/PFB/SOKB only; our 2012 stacks (`a5_picks.csv`) give S picks PGC 102, B009 99, B010 101, B011 94, VGZ 79, SNB 78, B926 73, NLLB 63 but B001 8, B003 10, B004 0, B005 11, B006 18, B007 18, LRIV 14 ⇒ far-borehole S labels are PREDICTED times (Crosson + station corrections up to +1.13 s) with ≥0.5 s uncertainty. (c) P labels unusable (Lin σ 4.2 s; our P residuals +0.5..+2.5 s beyond 60 km). (d) Time-shifted controls are network-level nulls, not per-station negatives. (e) dv/v families are not Bostock families, not proven non-LFEs (R10: 3,616/4,875 B011 repeaters unknown origin) — do not use as negatives. (f) Catalog v1 p_real ≥ 0.8 positives are the template's selection function at ~60 % precision — training on them locks in template biases and false alarms.

**F6 — MAJOR: negatives/PU.** ETS-day windows absent from Bostock are not negatives (69 % of unmatched ETS-day detections are within 60 s of a neighbouring family's event; tremor near-continuous). Lin's noise class was contaminated the same way. A model trained with ETS-day "negatives" learns to suppress sub-threshold LFEs.

**F7 — MAJOR: P3 bar nearly unfalsifiable** ("≥50 members within 5 km" with 2.5 km family spacing and cross-family cc up to 0.67). Needs one-to-one member purity against the hidden family's Bostock events and an alias test against kept families.

**F8 — MAJOR: the P1 baseline is missing.** The frozen template detector has never been run on 2011/2013. All comparisons must be at matched NETWORK-WIDE quiet-day false events/day (T_core ≈ 12/day, T_ext ≈ 116/day) AND a moveout-shuffle null ratio.

**F9 — MAJOR: injection curriculum as completeness repeats E4/E5.** Injected-stack recall was 4–6× above real. Completeness must be calibrated on natural Bostock events by magnitude (dev years) and validated on the held-out year; injection only extends the mapping to families/regions without truth, frozen on the north.

**F10 — MAJOR: "elsewhere: picker only".** The science needs per-family per-day completeness; a source-agnostic picker gives neither family identity nor Mc/λ. Once a family passes the gates, production = the template machinery. The learned model's job is to find ≥50 events per family per SSE so a stack can be built.

**F11 — MINOR: compute/data.** cache20hz 1.6 TB ≈ 77k station-days ≈ 210 station-years; traces 1.8 TB. NFS I/O-bound for pretraining. Bostock training windows ≈14 GB — fits in RAM.

**F12 — MINOR:** calibrate against withheld-station realness, report reliability vs Bostock as a lower bound.

**F13 — MINOR: Gate 3 passes any activity-modulated FA process** — needs local-vs-distant tremor contrast.

**F14 — MINOR: southern truth.** Ducellier's templates live on FAME temporaries + KHBB/YBH/WDC/KRMB/KSXB/B039 — none of B045/B935/RVIT/PETY; Shelly's array has no roster overlap. P4-south has no denominator until a stack test shows those families register on roster stations.

## Prescription (ordered so the cheapest step can kill the approach)

**P0-O — Oracle and referee freeze (CPU, 1–2 days, before any training).**
1. Seal 2013 (Bostock 2013-09-13..10-13, 14,873 events, 123 families ≥30, 60 station-days × 15 stations on disk) as the single final referee; 2011 gets exactly one evaluation per stage gate; 2010 + 2012 are dev. Per-family offsets for 2011/2013 = the frozen 2012 offsets (never refit on the held-out year). Order 2008 (2008-05-01..06-10) and 2009 (2009-05-01..06-15) ETS windows (~600 station-days ≈ 12–15 GB) for training breadth.
2. Run the frozen template configuration on 2011 and 2013 → P1a baseline: own recall (±2 s, one-to-one), quiet-day FA, network-wide false events/day. Acceptance: 2011 own recall within 0.7–1.3× the 2012 S15 ablation (0.146/0.184). Failure (<0.5×): time-base/moveout problem in the referee setup.
3. Per-station headroom oracle on 2012 test-half events: at every station, (i) E4 incoherent 3-C S-window energy excess (S−1..S+5 over the window 20 s earlier, 1–8 Hz), (ii) 3-C cc to the family template; single-station ROCs vs (a) quiet-day windows, (b) ETS-day windows ≥120 s from any Bostock event; combine across sites with true moveout (co-located merged) and compare recall at matched network-wide quiet-day FA (12/day and 116/day). Decision: energy-network recall <1.25× template ⇒ P2 re-scoped to discovery only; ≥1.5× ⇒ P2 carries a sensitivity claim. Report single-station recall at 100 peaks/station-day as the target a learned per-station model must beat.
4. Southern truth-existence test (when 2019+ southern data lands): for each of Shelly's 27 families, stack B045/B935/RVIT/PETY at his detection times with a per-family offset searched in ±30 s (same search on 200 random-time null stacks); pass = ≥10 families with S-window excess above null p99 at ≥2 stations. Failure ⇒ P4-south = injection sensitivity + activity series only.

**P0 — Labels, negatives, grid, infrastructure.** Positives: Bostock 2010–2013 (+2008/2009), per station S = DB time + frozen family offset + site S pick from `a5_picks.csv` where present, else Crosson ttS + a5 station correction; label σ = 0.5 s; provenance (picked/predicted) per label. P auxiliary only. Certain negatives: PNSN-quiet station-days excluding ±120 s around any Bostock event; hard negatives: catalogued EQs/blasts, HF-veto quiet-day transients, template quiet-day T_ext detections. ETS-day windows not near a Bostock event: UNLABELLED (excluded from loss, or nnPU with class prior from Track A 3b). Time-shifted controls = evaluation nulls only. dv/v families and catalog v1 events: excluded from training in P1–P3. Rotate boreholes to ZNE for the learned model's inputs (template path stays on the frozen chain); peak-normalise per window; train across BH/HH epochs at 20 Hz / 1–8 Hz. Grid: Slab2 interface, 2-D cells 3 km, depth fixed on Slab2; S-VI box ~1,600 cells; 0.25 s; Crosson + a5 S corrections; moveout tolerance ±0.5 s. Family holdout fixed now and hashed: 30 families (10 with NN ≥5 km, 10 at 2.5–5 km, 10 at <2.5 km), hidden from ALL label uses. Station holdout: B926, NLLB, LRIV excluded from training, evaluated as unseen. Acceptance: label table with provenance counts.

**P1 — Baselines (≤2 GPU-days).** P1a = template on 2011. P1b = Lin's S-model recipe from scratch on our 15 stations AND a PhaseNet-style model at 20 Hz with 60-s windows; pick by dev single-station S-peak recall at 100 peaks/station-day. Association: physics back-projection on the P0 grid (primary), PyOcto (secondary). Evaluate once on 2011 at matched network-wide FA; report per-station S recall at ±1 s vs peaks/day (pretrained LFEDetect was 0.12–0.13 at 720/day). Acceptance: single-station recall ≥0.30 at ≤100 peaks/station-day at PGC. Failure (<0.2): labels/time base wrong — check offset sign and S placement against `a5_picks.csv`.

**P2 — Array model.** Core = per-station learned characteristic function (3-C, 60 s, station-agnostic weights) + explicit back-projection over the Slab2 grid (site-merged log-odds at the predicted S, per-cell per-day MAD normalisation, threshold by quiet-day FA ONLY). Station dropout (3–15-station subsets; whole-site drops ≥20 %). Learned beamformer demoted to an ablation after the core passes; if run, supervise cell outputs with real-waveform transplants at random cells and evaluate location on hidden families only. Pretraining optional, must pass a cheap probe: cross-station predictor on 2014–2016 with targets ≥15 km from every input station and EQ windows excluded; linear probe on 2012 Bostock windows vs in-tremor non-event windows — pass only if AUC ≥0.85 (R9 baseline 0.81); else drop. Budget ≤2 GPU-days. Injection for training: transplant REAL single-event 3-C windows of loud Bostock events into quiet days at the moveout of a random Slab2 cell, scale 0.2–1, annealed, ≤30 % of samples. Acceptance on 2011 (one shot): own recall at matched network FA ≥1.25× P1a if the oracle licensed a sensitivity claim; moveout-shuffle null ≤10 % of ETS-day detections; recall by label provenance, n_sites, magnitude, family; calibration vs withheld-station realness within ±0.1. Failure: <1.0× P1a ⇒ no completeness claim, proceed to P3 only.

**P3 — Family discovery on truth.** Cluster detections by back-projected cell and multi-site waveform similarity; a hidden family is rediscovered if a new cluster has centroid within 5 km, matches ≥30 % of its Bostock events one-to-one (±2 s), member purity ≥50 %, and is not >50 % explained by a kept family. Bar: ≥2/3 of the 10 isolated hidden families; neighbour strata reported. Also the spurious-split rate for kept families. Failure (<1/3 isolated): stop before P5.

**P4 — Transfer.** (a) Unseen-station recall (B926/NLLB/LRIV) on 2011 — degradation ≤30 %. (b) Along-strike family split (train west/test east and reverse). (c) South only if P0-O step 4 passed; else injection sensitivity with the north-frozen mapping, labelled "uncalibrated".

**P5 — Margin-wide discovery.** Regions by geometry: Puget/Olympic 2019+ (2023+: 26 stations within 60 km per cell, nearest 9.8 km, gap 52°), N Oregon 2023+, then C/S Oregon and MTJ. Proposals → clusters → Gates 0–3 → per-station stacks → template production with the Track C/D machinery → catalog v2. Report the rejected fraction per gate.

**P6 — Production**: template machinery as the per-family detector; learned model re-run per SSE as the proposal generator.

## The gates, quantified
**Gate 0 (identity/class):** ≥5 km from every accepted family, or multi-site stack cc below the per-station cross-family p95 (PGC 0.67, others 0.32–0.52) at ≥2 sites; stack HF ratio within the LFE core distribution (median ~0.25; veto ≥3.5).
**Gate 1 (withheld-station stack):** two stations at two distinct sites excluded from detection for the whole run; N≥20 members; statistic = 10·log10 S-window (S−1..S+5) over pre-arrival (S−12..S−6) energy. Nulls: 200 random-time stacks (excess > p99 and ≥+3 dB); 100 moveout-shuffled stacks (true-moveout excess > p95); positive control: a known Bostock family at matched N must pass, else "untestable". Not caught: intraslab multiplets with interface-consistent moveout; aliases; families whose withheld stations are all >60 km.
**Gate 2 (location with controls):** S picks on stacks at ≥4 sites within 60 km, gap ≤180°, a5 machinery on the 2-D interface grid; shuffled-pick null (<0.5×), synthetic floor (≤3 km expected), leave-one-site-out spread ≤5 km, free depth within ±10 km of Slab2 and not at a bound.
**Gate 3 (tremor timing, soft):** E_local (members on days with PNSN tremor within 25 km / calendar fraction) ≥3, E_local/E_far(75–150 km) ≥1.5, p<0.01 from 1,000 circular shifts ≥30 d. Genuinely inter-ETS families: report, do not reject, if Gates 1–2 pass strongly.

**Verdict**: The three datasets support real science now, but two premises in the question are artefacts and must be corrected before any test: (i) "only C0/C1 host strong VLFEs" is template/sensitivity-limited — from 2013 on the events locate 20–30 km NW of C0/C1 (year-median latitude 48.43–48.52 N vs 48.25 in 2011–12; in 2024-08, 64 of 136 events have a Washington family as nearest), so the productive zone is the Strait segment 48.0–48.7 N at ±16 km; (ii) "2024 is so big" is a raw count: completeness at Mw 3.4 is 0.79 in 2024-08 vs 0.31 (2023-04), 0.42 (2018-06), 0.14 (2015-12) (`/home/jovyan/tremorferometry/svi_science/results/s13_vlfe/catalogue/tables/prod*_episodes_v2.csv`), and the median event (Mw 3.26) is below Mw_c, so cross-year counts need per-magnitude completeness. The decision that matters: anchor every VLFE to its own concurrent LFEs (time and location) rather than to the C0/C1 patch or the VLFE grid, and run the day-scale "front vs behind" test first.

**Not testable at our resolution (columns only)**: b-value — 527/540 ETS events lie in Mw 3.08–3.6, `Mw_scan` good to ×2, 13 events ≥ 3.6, 2 ≥ 3.8: the distribution is the detection roll-off (substitute: largest Mw per episode, count ≥ 3.6 with injected completeness). **Ranked questions (value ÷ cost)**

1. **Do VLFEs fire at the local LFE front, behind it, or in re-activations?** New: Ghosh 2015 / H&G 2016 compared VLFEs with tremor migration in one ETS each; Gomberg 2016 "VLF = sum of LFEs" predicts VLFE times are a random draw of LFE times; nobody has tested timing against each family's own onset over 12 ETS. Data: 540 ETS rows of `catalogue/tables/prod*/prodr*/prodf*/prodfy*_events_v2.csv` (`time_pt`, `epoch`, `episode`, `lat_best`, `lon_best`, `n_lfe_10m`, `lfe_status`); LFE times from `svi_science/results/s0/analysis_table.parquet` (2014+, series data, P_same ≥ 0.75, keep_xfam8, `fam`, `time_utc`) and `data/catalogue/svi_catalogue_v1_4_backward.parquet` (2003–13, `t`, `fam`, `P_same`), WA families via the lists `cat_e2.lfe_status()` uses (`hg_r3.lfe_lists()`); family positions `svi_science/results/s0/families.csv`. Test: for each VLFE with ≥ 1 P ≥ 0.75 LFE within ±10 min (431 events), Δt = VLFE time − first LFE of those families in that episode (count-weighted mean; min/median as columns). Control: 1,000 draws of a random LFE of the same families and episode, same Δt. Three scales: day (above); minutes — LFE counts in [−10,−2], [−2,0], [0,+2], [+2,+10] min, null symmetric; hours — fraction of 2010–13 VLFEs inside the 30 Bostock-density RTR windows of 2010-08/2011-08/2012-08/2013-09 (`svi_science/results/q8_rtr/tables/rtr_list_frozen_d2.csv`: `start`, `end`, `x_start`, `x_end`; within ±20 km along strike) vs the LFE-drawn null; plus S3 day-scale returns (`svi_science/results/s3/bursts.csv`, `return_streaks.csv`; 2023-04 Apr 18–19, 2018-06 Jul 8–10). Limits: VLFE location ±16 km with an 11.5 km NW bias at 5 km/day = ±3 days per event with a direction-dependent sign — hence the per-VLFE LFE anchor; for the 109 events without LFEs require the same sign in SE-ward (2015-12, 2018-06, 2019-11, 2021-01, 2022-03) and NW-ward (2019-08, 2020-11, 2023-04, 2024-08) episodes (`svi_science/results/s3/episodes_kinematics.csv` `direction`; 2010–13 from `svi_science/results/q6_front_arrest/episodes_pnsn.csv`). My first look confirms the confound: 2018-06 VLFEs (NW-located, SE-ward front) start ~2 d before the first C0/C1 LFE; 2024-08 (NW-ward front) VLFEs lag the patch-LFE median by ~3 d. Detection control: injection recovery vs injection day in the episode from `data/<run>/inj_list.parquet` (`epoch`) × `inj_v2.parquet` (`battery`); MF thresholds are per template per run (`scripts/cat_e2.py` lines 35–36), so no daily self-suppression is expected — verify. Outcomes: late = VLFEs are a post-peak phenomenon of the slipped zone (same regime as rising tidal sensitivity and RTRs) — new; early = VLFEs mark front arrival — new; indistinguishable = sum-of-LFEs holds at the day scale, minutes/hours decide.

2. **Are VLFEs tidally modulated, and more or less than the LFEs around them?** New: Royer 2015/S5 are LFEs, Houston 2015 is tremor; no Cascadia VLFE tidal result exists. Data: hourly stresses at all SVI families 2014–26 (`svi_science/results/s5_setup/tidal_stress_svi_hourly.parquet`: `tau_updip_Pa`, `sigma_n_Pa`, `coulomb_0p4`) and 2003–2014-01 (`svi_science/results/s5/tidal_stress_bostock_hourly.parquet`); interpolate to the second or synthesise from `s5_setup/tidal_constituents_per_family.csv`. Location error irrelevant: across the 10 families nearest C0/C1 the up-dip shear differs by 14 % of its temporal std (checked 2024-08-20). Test: M2/K1 phase of 540 VLFEs, Rayleigh + modulation amplitude; control = LFE-time draws from the same families/episode matched on days-since-local-onset (S5 sensitivity rises 3–4 d after onset; without matching, "VLFEs more tidal" would be a timing artefact). Power: at ±35 % modulation N = 540 gives Rayleigh z ≈ 16; a 0.2 difference in modulation is ~3σ. Limits: 2016–17 (LRIV fault) flagged. Outcomes: same = VLFEs are LFE-like slip; stronger = large slow events more tide-sensitive (friction/fluid statement); none = distinct, loosely coupled process.

3. **Does discrete VLFE productivity per LFE vary with episode size while the continuous slow moment per LFE does not?** New: S13 gives a constant ~12× continuous accompaniment 2008–26; the discrete counterpart was never tabulated across ETS. Data: VLFE counts per Mw bin with `compl_3.4/3.6/3.8` + CIs (episode tables); patch LFEs = P ≥ 0.75 dedup LFEs of families within 20 km of each episode's VLFE centroid (C0/C1-20-km counts: 2015-12 441, 2018-06 424, 2019-08 356, 2020-11 356, 2021-10 188, 2023-04 416, 2024-08 408, 2025-09b1 61); Tier A `D` (clean 2015-12 5.95 mm, 2018-06 5.51, 2023-04 5.05, 2024-08 5.08, 2025-09b1 5.13; south-only 2019-08 1.59; near-miss 2020-11 2.91; WA-leak 2021-10 1.91); S13 totals `m0_total` in `s13_vlfe/tables/scale_svi_episodes.csv`. Raw ratios span 0.5 (2021-10) to 33 (2024-08) per 100 patch LFEs; roughly corrected, 2018/2023/2024 cluster ~40, 2019-08/2020-11 ~10–15, 2021-10 ~2, 2015-12 5–16 (LRIV broken, `tables/qc_exclusions.csv`). Test: Spearman of corrected productivity vs `D` over 8–9 Tier-A episodes, permutation p; subtract 2018's 17 relay self-detections; LFE-side `n_sta_day` column (2025: 11 stations). Also tabulate discrete/continuous moment per episode (0.08–2.4, not "about half" as finding 10 says). Limits: n ≤ 9, completeness corrections up to ×3 → only a factor-3 effect is claimable; 2014-11 (0 VLFEs at 0.80, 48 patch LFEs, 141 SVI LFEs for 4.3 mm) is an LFE-catalogue suspect to audit first. Outcomes: ρ > 0.7 = whole-island ETS make VLFE-rich patches (super-linear discrete share) — new; flat = productivity a patch property; 2015-12-dominated = unresolved.

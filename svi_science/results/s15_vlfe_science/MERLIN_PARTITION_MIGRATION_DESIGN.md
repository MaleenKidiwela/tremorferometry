**Q2 — dependence on migration rate (test design)**
- Speed measures: (i) per-episode main front speed `lfe_speed` (Theil–Sen km/d, CI columns) in `/home/jovyan/tremorferometry/svi_science/results/s3/episodes_kinematics.csv` for 10 SVI episodes 2015–2024 (2.7–6.7 km/d; 2021-10 "no single front"); for 2010-08b1, 2011-08, 2012-08, 2013-09, 2025-09b1 compute the same from the pool with S3's onset rule (`/home/jovyan/tremorferometry/svi_science/scripts/s3_kinematics.py` lines 142–148: family onset = 10 % quantile of the family's times, families with ≥ 5 LFEs; Theil–Sen of onset vs `along_strike_km`). (ii) Per-VLFE local passage speed: Theil–Sen slope of onset vs along-strike over families within ±10 km of the VLFE's `anchor_along` (≥ 4 families), folded to the episode direction (`q1_episode_directions.csv`). Assign the same quantity to every pool LFE of the same families. (iii) Daily front speed: front position per day = median along-strike of families whose onset falls that day; Theil–Sen over ±1.5 d; attach to each hour of `q2c_hourly.parquet` (9,000 h; columns `episode, t_mid, n_vlfe, n_zone, n_all, days_since_onset, th_m2, th_o1, onset_bin, day_block`). (iv) Fast reversals: the 30 Bostock-density windows (cat `bostock`, ep 2010-08/2011-08/2012-08/2013-09) in `/home/jovyan/tremorferometry/svi_science/results/q8_rtr/tables/rtr_list_frozen_d2.csv` (`v_ev` is km/h, 1.1–21; `days_after_onset` 4–30); `in_rtr` already in `q1_events.csv`.
- Main test (immune to completeness): compare the local-passage-speed distribution of the 422 anchored VLFEs with that of re-anchored pseudo-VLFEs drawn from the same families and episode within ±24 h (the S15 matched control, `s15_q1b.py` machinery), 1,000 draws; statistic = VLFE-per-LFE ratio in slow/medium/fast terciles (edges from the pooled LFE distribution). Per-episode sign for the 9 big episodes; both front directions. Claimable: a ≥ 1.5× productivity contrast between outer terciles above the 99th percentile of draws.
- VLFE moment vs speed: median `Mw_scan` by tercile (count, not size, is expected; a 0.05-Mw shift is resolvable but inside the ×2 scan error — column only). Discrete/continuous vs speed: S13 cannot split the continuous term within an episode without re-stacking; optional step — rows split by local passage speed tercile for 2012-08, 2018-06, 2024-08, acceptance ±20 % per tercile (≥ 300 LFEs each). Otherwise disc/cont ∝ VLFE per LFE and the main test answers it.
- RTRs: recompute the 24 % vs 15 % against the ±24 h-matched draws (not whole-episode draws): RTRs run at days 8–20 and VLFEs are late, so the whole-episode control conflates reversal with maturity. Add a fast-forward class (top tercile of daily speed, or the q8 forward surges — 31 in 2003–13 per `q8_rtr/RESULTS.md` line 93; their per-burst list is not in a table I found, `runs_d2.csv` is only an index and `members_data_d2.parquet` maps uid→row) to separate "fast" from "backward". 2014–26 has only 8 SVI RTRs: not testable there.
- Tidal gating × speed: event-level M2 `a` (declustered) by local-passage-speed tercile with matched LFE draws, and the S15 Poisson model (`s15_q2c.py`) with speed tercile × (cos θ, sin θ) interaction, days-since-onset bins kept in. Power: all-event a 1.05 (cluster bootstrap ±0.17); per tercile n ≈ 140 → ±0.15, so a difference ≥ 0.3 between outer terciles is the claimable size; the regression ratio (CI 0.74–1.31 on 495 events) resolves only a 2× amplitude difference between terciles.
- Control: detection vs speed. `/home/jovyan/tremorferometry/svi_science/results/s15_vlfe_science/tables/detect_injections.csv` (54,400 C0/C1 injections: `run, source, Mw, epoch, rec, episode, onset, days_since_local_onset, bin`) — add the local passage/daily speed at the source and tabulate recovery by tercile; the 30 % relative-difference line is a column, per the user's rule.
- Limits: per-episode main speed spans only 2.5× over ≤ 11 episodes (little leverage; report, do not lean on it); local passage speed ±20–30 % per VLFE; no-LFE events excluded; RTR test 2010–13 only (129 VLFEs, 31 inside, carried by 2012-08).
- Outcomes: more VLFEs per LFE where the front passed slowly → VLFEs need local dwell/maturity (consistent with the late-phase finding) — new; more where fast → VLFEs mark rapid front loading — new; flat → productivity independent of speed, and if the RTR excess vanishes against matched draws it was a maturity effect. Tidal: if a rises with slower passage with days-since-onset held fixed, speed is a separate control on the gate; if the onset bins absorb it, it is not.

2. MAJOR — f's "z_loso ≥ 0.5 z_best" clause assumes a balanced network. C0's centroid (48.14 N, 123.53 W, `tables/e3_events.csv`) is 9 km from UW.LRIV, so LRIV legitimately dominates. C1 (ratio 0.50 in Oct 2021) sits exactly on the clause. The scan is Z-only in these years: `data/scan_2021/runs/real_v2/daycfg.parquet` has w_on = 0 for every N/E channel; SNB Z off from April, VGZ Z off from July; LRIV, PGC, NLLB, OTR Z on all year. The coordinator's "f not testable with < 3 near stations" is worse than the status quo: it turns a measurable z_loso = 12 into a missing check (level ≤ 4 in the ladder) and is an invented gate.

# MERLIN — review 4: 2016–17 controls after W2b (2026-10-16 ~11:40 am PT; saved by the main session)

**Model check**: Fable 5.1 (claude-fable-5-1), not downgraded.

**Verdict**: Do not adopt the per-year control-tuned threshold; it cannot be validated at this N and it is aimed at the wrong number. Report 2016–17 at measured sensitivity with the 2011 bank as their final bank (the 2018 relay bank fails the pre-registered relay acceptance rule in those years), and fix the one real non-uniformity: 2018 was never re-run under the adopted W2b although the exclusion table drops LRIV for all 12 months of 2018. The decision that matters is to stop treating the per-year "≤ 1/yr" gate as informative: it has no power at 16 catalogues × 0.52–0.69 live yr.

**Flaws** (severity-ordered)
1. MAJOR — 2018 not under W2b. `tables/qc_exclusions.csv` lists UW.LRIV for 2018 months 1–12 and 2015-12 (LRIV Z 410–495 nm/s every 2018 month vs the 2011–2018 reference 3.6 nm/s; `diag_noise_2018.csv`), but `scripts/w_queue.sh` re-ran only 2013/2016/2017; `data/prodr2018` (10:23–11:15 PT) predates it. The 2018 relay templates RC0–RC7 were cut with a broken LRIV channel and are now carried into 2019–26 with PROD_QC=1. "2018 passes, 2016–17 fail" compares different rules. W2(ii) says every production year.
2. MAJOR — the gate is a coin flip, not a discriminator. From `wdiag_*.csv` (events deduped per draw): 2013 = 8 control events, all A2 (polarity) draws, A1 = 0 → A2 rate 1.45/yr, mean 0.72; 2016 A1 0.72 / A2 1.20; 2017 A1 0.66 / A2 1.76. 2013 "passes" only because its A1 count is zero. Events sit on 5–9 days/yr (clustered), so 8 vs 11 events is not a difference. The §18 p ≈ 0.3 is right but post hoc; the honest statement is that the pre-registered gate had σ ≈ 0.35/yr at 1/yr by design.
3. MAJOR — the controls do not reproduce the catalogue's own between-ETS noise, so tuning to them is pointless. Real raw between-ETS tier events: 2013 5, 2014 11, 2016 8, 2017 4 (`prod_years_final.csv`, `prodq*_R_between_v2.csv`) vs 0.5–0.7 expected per scrambled catalogue — ~8× over 2010–18 (39–48 real vs ≈ 5 expected). The between-ETS bound therefore rests entirely on Q2/Tier A′, and `cat_r.py` lines 172–190 compute Q2/Tier A′ only for real events (`btw`), never for control tier events: the Tier A′ false rate is unmeasured. §18 never mentions this.
4. MAJOR — proposed fix is unsound at this N. Calibration half ≈ 4–6 control events per year; the threshold would be the 2nd–3rd largest z among them; the check half is Poisson(≈4.6) against a pass line of 4.6 → ~50 % failure with a correct threshold. It also jitters completeness year to year by control noise, breaking the uniform Mw_c the catalogue needs. The design's "threshold from controls" (`PLAN_CAT.md` §2) used NOISE templates at 0.5/template-day; that z* exists (6.46–6.71, `prod*_noise_threshold.csv`) but costs 18–28 % of real 2011–13 ETS tier events (zmad < 6.5) and 70 % in 2017, without separating 2016–17 from 2013 (W2 result).
5. MINOR — relay bank in 2016–17: Amendment R/T2 accepts a relay bank only with scrambled ≤ 1/yr; U applied the 2018 bank to 2016–17 without that test. Its 2016–17 rates (3.48/2.54) fail it, so by the existing rule the 2011-bank runs (prodq2016/2017, W2b) are the final 2016–17 numbers; relay runs become columns. Bank-size growth of false pairs is real; for the forward chain cap the bank (2011 ∪ latest accepted relay) rather than re-tune.
6. MINOR — "LRIV fully masked where excluded" holds for real and A2 only; A1 rolls the masked months (`cat_pilot.py` 328–330; wdiag rows 2017-05-23 A1_1 carry LRIV shares 2.7/3.4). By construction, not a bug.
7. SPECULATIVE — T2 "fails" in 2017 may be an LFE-catalogue gap: the 2017-03 major has 42 SVI P ≥ 0.75 LFEs total vs 1,138–2,864 in other majors (`prod_episodes_all_final.csv`). Check PNSN tremor within 30 km of C0/C1 in 2017-03; if abundant, call T2 untestable, not failed.

**Prescription**
1. Re-run 2018 under W2b (2011 bank and a relay bank re-cut without LRIV; LOO ≥ 85 % must be re-passed), then re-start the forward chain from the new bank. Accept: 2018 T1/T3 unchanged in verdict; report new control rate whatever it is. Failure signature: relay LOO < 85 % → forward chain uses the 2011 bank only until a new relay year passes.
2. Replace the per-year gate by the pooled 2011-bank rate 2010–18 with a Poisson interval and an A1/A2 split as columns; per-year rates stay as columns. Accept: dispersion test of per-year control counts vs common rate × live time; p > 0.05 → per-year pass/fail is noise, 2016–17 are plain low-completeness years (Mw_c > 4.0, stated). Over-dispersed → 2016–17 flagged "elevated noise", still reported.
3. Compute Q2/Tier A′ on every scrambled-control tier event (scan columns already in wdiag) → Tier A′ control rate per year; this is the number behind the between-ETS bound. Accept: control Tier A′ ≤ 0.3/yr pooled. Failure: > 0.3/yr → bound must be stated at the raw-tier false rate.
4. Do not apply the proposed per-year threshold anywhere; if a Tier A with lower false rate is wanted, use the §2 noise-template z* uniformly for all years as a second tier with its own completeness.

**Experiments I could not run**: (a) LRIV PSD shape in broken vs normal months (per-component factors differ: Z ×185, N ×49, E ×27) — flat ratio → gain/metadata error, CC-harmless; red/LP-dominated → sensor fault, W2b essential. (b) The dispersion test in step 2. (c) Tier A′ control rate in step 3.

**What would change my mind**: a Tier A′ control rate well above 0.3/yr in 2016–17 but not in 2013 after step 3 — then 2016–17 really are worse and need a year flag; or the 2018 W2b re-run losing T1/T3, which would mean the LRIV channel was carrying the 2018 result.

The decision that matters: Tier A′/Q2 (vr ≥ 0.12 AND far/near2 < 0.5 AND GNW ≤ 0.551 north of 48.1 N) passes **3 of the 26 known VLFEs** (the Y8c positives) and 38/146 ETS battery′ events (0/29 in 2013–2017) — computed from `/home/jovyan/tremorferometry/svi_science/results/s13_vlfe/catalogue/data/ranked/r3_all_<Y>.parquet` columns `q2`, `vr_thrust`, `far_over_near2`, `ratio_GNW`. "0 Tier A′ between ETS" is therefore what you get even if every tier event were a VLFE. That rule was my own review-2 triage class for three 2014 events (`MERLIN_CAT_REVIEW2.md` line 18); it was never validated on known events, contradicts `MERLIN_CAT_DESIGN.md` line 9 ("GNW a column"), and §15 (`RESULTS.md` line 428) already saw it rejecting real-looking 2017 events. Retire it as a gate; keep the three quantities as columns.

**Flaws**
1. FATAL (for the score as ordering) — positives are battery-selected (18 of 26 are 2013 tier events; 21/26 pass battery′ by construction), the fit is separable (`tables/ranked3_cv_l2.csv`: CV loss falls monotonically to the weakest L2), and the additive logit lets one heavy-tailed feature override vetoes: the #1 between-ETS row (2017-12-03 19:53 PST, logit 15.4, `tables/ranked3_top10_between.csv`) has an M4.8 in the window, pass_e2 False, grid edge, unlocated; 45 % of the top-20 are in_head2; 190 of the 211 between rows with scan z > 100 are earthquake windows. A learned score at N = 26 cannot be defended; keep `score_logit` as a column labelled in-sample.
2. MAJOR — §20 explains the wrong set. The real between-ETS excess is the battery′-pass rows: on the Y8b footing 39 real vs noise 4 raw (1.8 bank-equivalent), A1 2.5/draw, A2 4.0/draw (`K.apply_rules` on `data/ranked/r2bat_<Y>.parquet`, all classes); production says 45 (`RESULTS.md` §20(b), different dedup). The 80 Q2 rows that §20(c)–(d) eyeballed fail battery′ and are disjoint from these 39. Of the 39, only the 8 on 2014-01-23 and 2 on 2013-01-18 have been looked at (wavefield days); 29 isolated events on 28 other days (e.g. 2012-07-03 17:08 PDT, 2015-03-18 05:23 PDT, 2014-07-26 20:41 PDT: located 1–9 km from families, ratio_far 0.19–0.22, loso 0.6, failing Q2 only on vr) are uncharacterised.
3. MAJOR — vr_thrust is an SNR proxy (Spearman vs scan z 0.5 on ETS tier rows; known VLFEs with zmad < 8 have vr 0.03–0.11), so "vr ≥ 0.12" silently removes weak events — exactly the gate the user forbade. `data/prod<Y>/inj_v2.parquet` carries vr_thrust, so Q2 completeness is computable now (it never was: `RESULTS.md` line 489).
4. MINOR — "missing → worst" is false for f: `scripts/cat_ranked2.py` line 124 fills loso with 0 while 984 measured rows are negative (min −0.905). Fill with the clip floor (−1).
5. MINOR numbers — §20(c) "z_loso weight 0.28" is the Y8b coefficient (`data/ranked/score2_model.json`); Y8c is 0.056 (`tables/ranked3_sanity.json`). `tables/r20_footing_raw.csv` lists 24 noise templates for 2017; the parquet and `tables/y3_controls.csv` have 25. 2014-01-23 is not a storm day only because pct 0.818 < 0.85 while 01-22/01-24 are 0.93/0.94 (`tables/prod2014_R_stormdays_v2.csv`). A2 does not "keep the wavefield": it is a random per-station sign on the CC sum (`scripts/cat_pilot.py` line 332), so it cancels coherent wavefield sums and halves single-station spikes — not a substitute for proposal (i). Other §19–§20 numbers (curve table, 136.3 template-years, even/odd split, 790–1,400 vs 880–1,490, 330–1,120 vs 80–450) reproduce.

**On the proposals**: (i) sound and needed, but build it as similarity-matched noise banks: measure the real bank's 11×11 inter-template CC (per station, 0.02–0.05 Hz), then for each base noise template make 10 siblings by mixing with other quiet windows at the amplitude that reproduces that mean CC; run agreement + battery′ on these banks; A2 stays a column. (ii) right direction, wrong form — no weights at all: count checks passed. (iii) make it per-detection **emergence** (window peak / median envelope of the surrounding ±30 min, per station; report median over near stations and count of near stations ≥ 2) plus the amplitude–distance Spearman already used in §12 (`RESULTS.md` line 297); an hour-RMS column penalises real events on noisy days, emergence does not. (iv) is subsumed by the per-station emergence vector (max / second-max station). Learned score: not worth keeping as the ordering; a transparent ladder is.

**Prescription** (cheapest-invalidating first)
1. Q2 completeness from `data/prod<Y>/inj_v2.parquet`: fraction of battery′-recovered injections at Mw 3.6/3.8/4.0 with vr ≥ 0.12, per year. Accept < 85 % → Tier A′ retired as bound-carrier; columns kept. Failure signature: ≥ 85 % everywhere → re-examine why known VLFEs fail (then it is the GNW clause alone).
2. Replace the score ordering with a transparent ladder: level = number of battery′ checks passed (a, b, c, e′, f) among agreement rows, `n_checks_missing` shown, ties by zmad; earthquake-in-window, grid edge, storm pct (max within ±1 day) as flag columns. Mechanical check: no in_head2 or grid-edge row in the top 50 between ETS; all 26 known VLFEs at the top level their n_missing allows.
3. Ladder table per year and pooled, between ETS: real events, distinct days, matched-noise-bank mean (×11 placeholder until step 4), A1, A2, completeness at each level from inj_v2 (it has pass_a…pass_f), excess rate with Poisson interval. This is the honest answer to "how many and how sure": at level 5, 39 real vs ≤ 4 control, divided by completeness 0.3–0.7.
4. Build the similarity-matched noise banks (2011–2013 first). Accept: their between-ETS agreement rate is within ×1.5 of the real bank's 790–1,120/yr. Outcome A: level-5 control rises to ≥ 20 → the excess is bank similarity; state real ≤ control. Outcome B: stays ≤ 5 → the excess stands.
5. Compute emergence + amplitude–distance columns for every agreement row (bp arrays per `cat_ranked2.amp_fn`), calibrate on the 26 known VLFEs (acceptance: ≥ 85 % have median near emergence ≥ the threshold chosen) and show that ≤ 20 % of the 2014-01-23 rows do; then figure every one of the 39 level-5 rows (r20_wave style) and label each wavefield / spike / candidate in a table. The between-ETS statement becomes: N pass every check, controls predict M, K are wavefield days by eye, the remainder are candidates with these columns.

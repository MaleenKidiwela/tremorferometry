## Verdict
The SVI result is sound and reportable as provisional with its resolution stated (±15–20 % per big episode, PGC-Z-dominated, F4 lower bound). The WA section is wrong at the root and must not be reported: `s13_units.wa_shift` moves every WA origin ~16 s too early (shift quantiles −18.7/−16.0/−14.0 s vs SVI −0.1/+0.5/+1.5 s), and in a 20–50 s band with a ~30 s kernel period that puts the ±8 s lag scan on the kernel's zero-crossing/side-lobe. A refit of the stored WA stacks at the right lag already shows all zones positive at SVI-like values.

## Short answers
**Q1 SVI constancy.** Not forced: linear projection with fixed template, weights do not pull to a common value, ±1-day stacks calibrate σ (0.90–0.94), 38/38 positive is not an estimator property. Independent channels agree within episodes (single-channel Z, 1e12 N m, from `data/stacks/scale_svi_*.pkl`: 2012-08 PGC 3.9/VGZ 4.5/SNB 5.3/LRIV 5.7; 2018-06 PGC 5.2/SNB 6.8/PFB 7.5; 2024-08 PGC 5.2/VGZ 7.1/LRIV 5.3/ODUC 4.6/OTR 4.7). Caveats: (a) pre-registered A2 verdict is "changes"; "constant 2008–2026" is post hoc and must be labelled so; (b) resolution ±15–20 % for the ~12 episodes with σ ≤ 1e12, nothing from the small ones; (c) PGC Z holds 46–78 % of fit weight in 2003–05, 2008-05, 2018-06, 2024-08 → PGC-out column needed; (d) control σ mildly inflated by the lag search (control p99/SD ≈ 1.9 vs 2.33 Gaussian) → χ²/dof 1.03 could hide ~20 % real scatter (MINOR). The 2003–05 drop is NOT instrument: PGC BHZ is one sensor epoch 2000–2015 (CMG3T_NSN, `data/resp/CN_bb.xml`); PGC reads 2.1 in 2004-07 and the independent C8 POLARIS stations agree (SHDB 1.2±0.5, KHVB 2.9±1.0, TWBB 4.7±1.1, weighted ≈ 2e12); only VGZ (CMG-3ESP era, σ_M0 1.4e12) reads 4.5. NOT a family-mix effect: top-10 families by count 9.0 (2012-08) → 4.8 (2004-07), other families 5.4 → 2.8, a uniform 0.5×. It coincides exactly with a catalogue regime change: 336–628 LFEs/day in 2003–05 vs 40–120/day in 2007–2013 inside the same backward catalogue. Discriminating tests = P-bin and rate-matched thinning stacks (step 4).

**Q2 between-ETS (688, z 3.7).** Plausible, not yet robust. Set = "outside episodes ± 2 d with local tremor" (`s7/PLAN_S7.md` l.20) → say "LFEs in inter-ETS tremor bursts", not "LFEs without slip". 36 % of events on 5 days; 2020-09-13/14 (109) + 2015-10-08/10 (106) = 31 %; the 2020-09-13 burst precedes the WA 2020-09-25 ETS by 12 d. tau_next ≥ 4 d, tau_prev ≥ 9 d: no overlap with episode windows. Teleseisms: none M ≥ 5.8 on the four biggest burst days (M6.3 on 2014-03-23, 33 events) → negligible with RMS QC. Timing jitter irrelevant at 20–50 s (unit lag 0). Missing: leave-burst-out, year-half split, P-weighted column, ratio uncertainty (0.79 ± 0.22 → 0.4–1.2 at 2σ). Step 5.

**Q3 WA.** Root cause: WA catalogue `time` is already origin-like (`lfe_extension_wa/scripts/wa_prod_cut.py` docstring l.13–14: "Deduplicated on (family, 20-Hz origin sample)"; "S = origin + template offset + 0.5 s"), `s_off` is a within-template offset (3.65 s at PGC for fam 1, not a travel time), so `wa_shift` (`s13_units.py` l.34–47) subtracts r/Vs that was never added → −16 s. Wide-lag refit (−60…+60 s, SP2 E and σ=0 excluded) of stored WA stacks: zone-1 best lags +8…+14 with M0 3.1–6.0e12 (2009-04 5.95, 2011-07 4.0, 2021-09 4.4, 2017-02 3.8, 2023-03 3.4, 2018-05 3.1); zones 2–3 positive at +12…+16 in most episodes (2009-04 z3 +6.6, 2020-09 z3 +4.4, 2025-09 z3 +3.8, 2021-09 z2 +4.4); a few units pick a negative side lobe at +24–30 (the band's sign ambiguity). Secondary: (i) cross-family duplicates — 46 % of WA pooled rows within 3 s of another row (median 44 %, max 76 %), nb±2 s 0.30–0.37 vs SVI 0.00–0.01; SVI uses `counted_once`, WA nothing though `post_v2/dedup` exists; inflates n ~1.8×, z ~1.35× = the ±1-day/scrambled ratio 1.3–1.5; (ii) 31 of 132 NW families used have depth 0–18 km (21–31 % of rows per zone), 5 filled at 35 km; (iii) SP2 E (known). Station geometry and P threshold are not needed. Control for WA z: scrambled, after dedupe, calibrated by ±k-day shifts (k = 1…5 both signs); if the ratio is outside 0.85–1.15, scale σ by it.

**Q4 physics of 0.15 %.** Measured quantity = kernel-weighted 20–50 s thrust moment within ~±15 s of each catalogued P ≥ 0.75 LFE. State in this order: (1) band-limited VLF-to-LFE moment ratio ≈ 12 (5e12 vs 4.5e11), first-order independent of catalogue completeness (nb±10 0.12 in 2012-08 → ≤10 % double count; 0.38 in 2004-07); (2) moment rate ≈ 5e12 / ~30 s ≈ 2e11 N m/s at LFE times vs episode-mean geodetic rate 1.3–1.9e12 N m/s (2024-08: 4.7e18/41 d; 2018-06: 5.6e18/34 d) → LFE-locked 20–50 s moment-rate fluctuation ≈ 10 % of the mean SSE rate: most slip at LFE sites is released smoothly at these periods, or in discrete VLFEs excised by F4, or outside the band; (3) summed, 0.1–0.3 % of GNSS moment, a floor (F4; uncatalogued tremor unsampled; band-limited), ×2 absolute. Never "0.15 % of the slip is VLF". Per-catalogued-LFE totals depend on catalogue density (the 2003–05 era would read 2–3× higher for the same slip). Ide-type comparison SPECULATIVE (papers not read).

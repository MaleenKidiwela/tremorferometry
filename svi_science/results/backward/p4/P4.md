# P4 — thresholds and quiet-day flag for the SVI backward extension (2003–2013)

outputs in this folder. Rules below were written **Thu 2026-10-08, 5:53 am PT, before anything was computed**.
"Scrambled control" = the same detector on the same day with each station site's data shifted to another time of that day.

### The forward rule, as used here (= `lfe_extension/scripts/c_tiers_by_epoch.py`, re-implemented read-only)

- Candidates: `series == "data"` rows of the detector's feature files (every ≥ 8 MAD network peak; identical to the
  Track C detections), with `hf_pass` computed exactly as `c_run_month.py` does (the feature files do not carry it):
  `hf_cut = c_common.hf_cut_for(c_common.hf_cuts(), "PGC", date, hf_fs, frozen 3.6384)`,
  `hf_pass = not (hfr finite and hfr > hf_cut)`. No cross-family assignment.
- Family-days: `series == "data"` rows of the `_fd` files with `observable == True`.
- Quiet day (PNSN) = the Track C / `svi_ref_fit.quiet_table` rule: no PNSN tremor in the family box that day and no
  tremor day within ±3 d. Days before 2010-01-10 are not eligible (the catalogue starts 2010-01-06).
- Post-SSE day = 1..10 days after the last tremor day of an episode (episode ends after ≥ 3 tremor-free days).
- FA(T) = candidates with net_mad ≥ T and hf_pass on those family-days / number of those family-days.
- Grid 8.00 … 15.00 step 0.25. T_core = first T with quiet FA ≤ 0.1. T_ext = first T with quiet FA ≤ 1.0 and
  post-SSE FA ≤ 1.5 (post bar ignored when there are no post-SSE family-days). Needs ≥ 200 quiet family-days.
- Note (known before computing): 8.00 is the recording floor, and in every forward epoch the quiet FA at 8.00 is
  already < 1.0, so T_ext usually lands on the floor (8.00); the T_ext part of the anchor is then 8.00 vs 8.25 = one step.
- Self-check, reported, not a pass rule: the same code on the forward production feature files (`refine/feat/prod`,
  E0 2014-01..2017-02) next to Track C's derived E0 value (9.00 / 8.00, quiet FA 0.1046 at 8.75). The two runs differ
  by the R2 station exclusions (SVI production has them, Track C v1.1 has not), so they need not agree exactly.

### P4-1 — anchor

- Runs `mf/feat/p5f_2010 … p5f_2013` (frozen network S15 as alive that day, no R2 exclusions = B4 production).
- Pass: |T_core − 8.75| ≤ 0.25 AND |T_ext − 8.25| ≤ 0.25. Fail → stop and report.
- Provisional on the years whose P5F marker exists when this is first run (2010–11); the final verdict is the same
  computation on 2010–13 (queued on `P5F_2013_DONE`).

### P4-2 — reduced station sets on E0 quiet days

- Days: every E0 (2014-01-01..2017-02-28) PNSN-quiet day (same rule as above). Caches/prep: the forward production
  defaults. One GPU process at a time.
- Runs (forward `svi_ref_mf.py` through `bw_gate_mf.py`, unchanged): CN4, B1, B2, B3, each with the forward R2 station
  exclusions applied — done by running `--sset eff` with an exclusion file = R2 exclusions ∪ (every station outside the
  set, every month), so the only difference from the forward production is the station set. Reference = the forward
  production run itself (`refine/feat/prod`, S15 with R2 exclusions). Extra, reported only: S15 fixed set without
  exclusions (the exact B4 production configuration) on the same days.
- Per set: T_core / T_ext by the forward rule above on these days (quiet bar only; no post-SSE days in this run).
- Control-matched T beside it (the G1 procedure, `bw_gate_g1_score.matched_T`): the smallest T on a 0.01 grid ≥ 8.00
  whose scrambled-control candidates per observable control family-day (no HF veto, as in G1) are ≤ the reference's
  rate at 8.75 (core) and at 8.25 (ext) on the same days; also rounded up to the 0.25 grid. If it is at 8.00 it is
  reported as "≤ 8.00 (floor)".
- If the forward-rule T and the control-matched T (0.25 grid) differ by more than one step (0.25), both are reported.

### P4-3 / P4-4 — era detector 2003-02..2006-06

- Era runs `p5e_<Y>` / `p5e2_<Y>` and frozen runs `p5f_<Y>`, 2003–2006 (P5 production), on the option-3 quiet days
  of 2003-02-01..2006-06-30. Forward rule (quiet bar; post-SSE bar not usable without a catalogue) → T_core / T_ext of
  the era detector (pooled 2003-02..2006-06; per year reported).
- Cross-check: the frozen network in the same days (p5f; CN4 before 2005-07, B1 2005-07..10, B2 from 2005-11)
  derived the same way, against its P4-2 value. Within one grid step (0.25) → the era thresholds stand. Otherwise
  bracket: era T as derived, and era T + (P4-2 T − option-3 T of the frozen set), both carried forward.
- Needs ≥ 200 quiet family-days per detector; queued on `P5E_<Y>_DONE` and on the quiet flag of those years.

### Quiet flag (option 3)

- Stations: CN4 only (PGC, VGZ, NLLB, SNB), every era. 20 Hz 1–8 Hz caches (backward first, then forward chain).
- Per station-hour: median over the hour of the 3-C summed squared amplitude (`w_energy_hours.hour_energy`, ≥ 1000
  finite samples).
- Normalisation per station instrument epoch: divided by the median of that station-epoch's hourly values over its days
  that are > 3 d from every Bostock window. Added 6:05 am PT, still before any flag was computed: when the epoch overlaps
  the calibration years 2010–11, only its days inside 2010–11 enter that median (so the calibrated threshold cannot
  move when pre-2010 years arrive); an epoch that is not yet fully cached is marked `ref_provisional`. Epochs: VGZ 2003-02-27 / 2007-02-28 / 2009-07-31 (→ 2003/07/09 as in
  PLAN), SNB 2000-05-25 / 2006-04-20 / 2008-12-11 / 2011-03-17 (→ 2006/08/11), PGC and NLLB one epoch each.
- Network hour ratio = median over the CN4 stations with a value (≥ 2 needed). Day statistic Z(d) = median over its
  hours with a ratio (≥ 12 hours needed; otherwise Z unknown and the day is never quiet).
- Active day: Z(d) ≥ thr_q, thr_q = the q-quantile of Z over the 2010-01-10..2011-12-31 PNSN-quiet days.
- Energy-quiet day: Z known AND no active day within ±3 d (unknown neighbours ignored).
- **Option-3 quiet day = energy-quiet AND not within ±3 d of a Bostock window** (`backward_scope/truth_episodes.csv`).
- Calibration (2010-01-10..2011-12-31): q ∈ {0.50, 0.51, …, 0.99} maximising sensitivity + specificity of the
  option-3 flag against the PNSN quiet flag; ties → larger q. Positive class = PNSN not-quiet day;
  sensitivity = P(option-3 not quiet | PNSN not quiet), specificity = P(option-3 quiet | PNSN quiet).
- Validation (2012-01-01..2013-12-31, q frozen), pass = all three:
  1. specificity ≥ 0.9; 2. sensitivity ≥ 0.8;
  3. control-subtracted quiet ΣP: P = the frozen forward WAVE confidence model (`refine/conf/models.joblib`, label
     "same") applied unchanged to every data and scrambled-control candidate of `p5f_2012`, `p5f_2013`; rate =
     (ΣP data − ΣP control) / quiet observable family-days. Option-3 rate / PNSN rate within 0.90–1.10 pooled over
     2012–13, AND within 0.90–1.10 in ≥ 80 % of the calendar months that have ≥ 5 quiet days under both flags.
     (P = `WAVE_same` of the frozen models, all candidates ≥ 8 MAD, no P cut.)
- Fail → both flags are written and reported side by side (no re-tuning on 2012–13).
- The flag is written for every day 2003–2013 that has CN4 caches; pre-2010 years are added by a queue step that waits
  for `P2_<Y>_DONE` (the P2 marker; P1 markers alone do not mean the cache exists).

### Quiet flag — calibration 2010–11 (`tables/qflag_calibration*.json|csv`, `quiet_flag_cn4.csv`)
- Frozen q = 0.72 (threshold Z ≥ 1.36): sensitivity 0.81, **specificity 0.28** (Youden J 0.09). The validation bar
  (specificity ≥ 0.9) cannot be met by this flag; the 2012–13 validation still runs once, as registered.
- Why: the CN4 daily energy statistic barely tracks PNSN tremor. AUC of Z for "PNSN not quiet" = 0.51, for "PNSN tremor
  that day" = 0.55, for big days (≥ 50 PNSN windows) vs quiet days = 0.63. Z is dominated by season (monthly median
  0.59 in Oct to 1.30 in Mar). Diagnostic only, nothing re-tuned.
- So, as prescribed, both flags are written and reported: `quiet_opt3`, and the Bostock-only flags `quiet_bostock3`
  (> 3 d from every window) and `quiet_bostock30` (option 1, > 30 d).
- What the weak flag costs, measured on B4 2010–11 (`tables/p4_flagdiag_B4.csv`; same detector, same days, only the
  quiet-day choice changes):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PNSN (truth) | 330 | 0 | 8.75 | 8.00 | 0.092 |

  threshold rule, and P4-3 is the cross-check.

# S11 — deep vs shallow dv/v with sensitivity kernels: Merlin design (2026-10-12)

Model: Claude Fable 5.1 (read-only review; file saved by the executor). "Scrambled control" = station sites shifted to another time of the same day. ε = −dv/v. All numbers cited are from files read on 2026-10-12.

## Verdict
Viable as a bound-plus-attribution study, not as a detection: deep = volumetric 25–45 km layer per region per episode, shallow = per-site term, data = raw per-episode 3C stacks, separation measured by injection through the whole chain. Expected precision per big SVI episode ~0.1 % on the deep term (per-cell σ ~0.1–0.15 % at n ≈ 100, deep coefficient ~0.3, ~100–250 cells), ~0.03 % pooled over the 12 episodes with ≥ 50 cells (`svi_science/results/s10_dvv/multi/tables/episode_stacks.parquet`: PGC/SNB/VGZ ~430 cells each with n ≥ 50, boreholes ~190, B926 164; 22 episodes with ≥ 20 cells).

Geometry: SVI families 48.12–48.93 N, 28.8–46.6 km deep; PGC-site hypocentral distance 44–63 km (t_S ≈ 12–17 s), VGZ 39–77 km, B926/SNB ~45 km epicentral; window excess ≈ 3 s → S-pinned amplification 4–7 (fleet: 18).

## Decisions
D1 Kernel. S-pinned differential time-residence kernel by MC radiative transfer, new script adapted from `fault_tomography/kernels/residence_kernel_mc.py` (original untouched). Reasons: the recipe anchors at the picked S, so the observable is the coda's excess delay over the direct ray (`notes/2026-09-02_merlin_3to5_ruling.md` Q2); `fault_tomography/kernels/kernel.py` diffusion is origin-anchored and needs lapse ≫ mean-free-time (here 1–3); `fault_tomography/inversion/build_G_captured.py` is a row-normalised single-scatter kink density with windows 2–4 s past the direct arrival — the unit-sum/capture-fraction class of error. Required changes: windows past the picked S (2–5 headline; 3–6, 2–3.5, 3.5–6 columns; `BIN_EDGES` to 6.5 s; the fleet's "w24" = 1–3 s past S must not be inherited); receiver at sensor depth with the surface-reflected (image) contribution; time residence = length / β(z) from the regional USGS CVM v1.7 S profile (`data/velocity_models/usgs_cascadia_v1.7/profiles/{south_VI,royer_NW_WA,royer_SW_WA}.csv`; SVI β 2.24 km/s at 0–1 km rising to 4.25 at 34.5–42 km) with exact segment–layer intersections, not 1-km sampling; outputs per layer (0–0.1, 0.1–0.3, 0.3–1, 1–3, 3–5, 5–10, 10–25, 25–35, 35–45, > 45 km) × zone (near-station cylinder r ≤ 10 km, near-source sphere r ≤ 10 km, rest); lapse weights from each series' own grand-stack envelope (`grandstacks.npz` G1), not the fleet profile `data/window_snr_profile.csv`; ell 20/40/80 km plus a two-layer variant (5 km in the top 3 km, 40 km below); 8,000 particles, second seed. Unit test per pair: Σ over all layer×zone differential coefficients = 1.00 ± 0.02 (homogeneous change → ε = δ). Report the ell-spread of a_s as the kernel-model uncertainty (expect ×2) and the family-depth spread of a_d at each site (expect ≥ ×2 across 29–46 km).
D2 Deep unknown = 25–45 km layer inside the region's family footprint. Not the ±1.5 km slab: fleet residence diagnostic (`res_catalog_kernels/residence_diag.csv`) gives slab net +0.21 with 33 % of pairs negative; top-5-km net +0.57, rising with offset 0.36 → 0.82. The slab sign problem is geometric and stays at SVI distances.
D3 Data unit. Raw P-weighted (P ≥ 0.5) episode stack per (family, station, response-epoch, episode); per-event 3C realignment (`s10_measure.realign`); leave-episode-out reference within the epoch; joint 3C S-anchored stretch 2–5 s (`s10_fam_3c_26.stretch` logic) with 200-draw bootstrap σ (`s10_measure.boot_stacks`); columns: 3–6, 2–3.5, 3.5–6 s; Z-only, H-only; 2-parameter (shift ±50 ms, stretch) fit; cc; rail (|ε| ≥ 0.99 %); c2 scrambled-control stacks through the identical path. Headline cells n ≥ 50 and cc3c ≥ 0.8; n 20–49 as a column. The user's SVD-Wiener day path (CC > 0.8 days, CC-weighted episode mean) is kept as a QC column, with its recovery measured by injection per series; it is not inversion data (pilot: recovery 0.10–0.70, pre-step leakage 0.33–0.50, 0/21 pass; the 0.064–0.075 % episode scatter is shrunken).
D4 Unknowns per region R: offset per (family, station, epoch) series (absorbs the epoch zero and any instrument step; never reference across epochs; epochs from `s10_common.EPOCHS`); S_site(e) per site (`s10_common.SITE`: B009/B010/B011→PGC, B005/B006/B007→LRIV) per episode; D_R(e) per episode; mid-crust not free — report the factor by which a uniform 5–25 km change projects onto D. Variant: S_site = two annual harmonics + per-year step. Weighted least squares, weights 1/σ² inflated by the series' scrambled-control χ²; weak Gaussian prior σ = 1 % on S and D for conditioning only; report formal covariance, resolution-matrix diagonal, and the eigenvectors of the normal matrix with eigenvalues below the noise level (the expected one is D ≈ −mean(S)); doubling/halving the prior must move D by < 10 %, else D is unresolved and that is the reported answer. No λ scans, no oracle, nothing transplanted.
D5 Time bins. None shared across SVI and WA: S is per site, D per region; each region is sampled at its own episodes (SVI `svi_science/results/r23/tables/episodes_2003_2026.csv`; NW `lfe_extension_wa/results/episode_rates/episodes_nw_final.csv`, 69 major 2007–2026). Compare regions afterwards on calendar-year means with errors.
D6 Inclusion. Families: SVI 115 (Bostock hypocentres, `lfe_catalog_repair/results/e5r_families_2012.csv`); WA NW location_quality A/B (113 of 141) and SW A/B (69 of 81) from `data/catalogue/families_v1_3.csv`; C-quality and approximate-only families measured but excluded from the inversion (no kernel); depths outside 25–45 km replaced by Slab2 at the epicentre and flagged (NW depths span 0.0–49.6 km, SW to 103 km). Stations: every station with a template S for the family, hypocentral distance ≤ 80 km, grand-stack causality coda/pre-P RMS > 1.5, realign cc median ≥ 0.5; failures kept as columns. POLARIS 2003–06 + LZB + NLLB included (raw reads from `lfe_extension/traces_backward`), expected to matter only for 2003–2005. SW only at P ≥ 0.75 on tremor days as a column (21.7 % scrambled share at P ≥ 0.5, `wa_prod/post_v2/POST_V2_RESULTS.md`). Olympic A/armB: column only — position-group stacks mix sources, which is itself a stretch systematic. NVI: excluded, no extended repeats (`notes/CATALOGUE_SUMMARY.md` l.57 "Not attempted").
D7 Gates (pass rules fixed before numbers). (a) Co-located test, kernel-free, first: PGC site quartet (later LRIV quartet) — the deep lobe is identical for co-located sensors, so any instrument difference is shallow or instrumental; current `multi/RESULTS.md` amplitudes B010 0.162 ± 0.016 vs PGC 0.085 ± 0.020 % differ at ~3σ. Needs borehole sensor depths from FDSN metadata (not on disk). (b) Injection suite, differential, waveform-level (`s10_measure.stretch_trace`, each window stretched by the pair's coefficient × amplitude), full chain re-run: A deep step +0.3 % from 2015 → recovery 0.7–1.3, leak into S < 30 %; B common-mode shallow annual 0.1 % at all sites → leak into D < 25 %; C one-site step → leak < 20 %; D kernel error (a_s ×/÷1.5 random per pair) → leak < 30 %. B > 50 % → D is "deep + regional shallow", reported as such. (c) Scrambled control: D from control stacks within 2σ of zero in ≥ 90 % of measurable episodes. (d) Split network: {PGC site} vs {B926, VGZ, SNB}, families odd vs even → D slope 0.7–1.3, r > 0.5. (e) Station-specific systematic: per-site D sign agreement ≥ 70 %; ε vs pre-arrival RMS change and vs (1 − cc) across cells ρ < 0.2. (f) Lapse: D(3.5–6) vs D(2–3.5) slope within the kernel-predicted ratio ± 0.3. (g) Seasonal: annual amplitude of D < 2σ while S carries 0.07–0.08 %; otherwise adjudicate with (a).
D8 Staging and cost. Pilot = SVI 76 families × 7 core stations on the existing windows (`svi_science/results/s10_dvv/multi/win/<NET.STA>.npz`, 3C, 40 Hz, −14..+10 s about S): kernels < 1 h (8 workers, ~600 pairs); measurement ~2 h (12 workers); inversion minutes; injections ~4× measurement. Then POLARIS/LZB/NLLB (~800 station-days). WA: headline rows `selected_v2 == True` in `lfe_extension_wa/results/wa_prod/post_v2/catalogue_v2.parquet` (NW ≈ 25 k repeats 2012–26 plus E1 2007–11), station S offsets from the WA templates, raw day files via `lfe_extension_wa/scripts/wa_common.py` TRACE_ROOTS with the `svi_science/scripts/s10_windows.py` Raw reader (same `<NET.STA>/<YYYY>/<DOY>.mseed` layout; 2–8 Hz, 40 Hz): ~15 k station-day reads, ~1–2 h wall at 12 workers, ~3 GB of windows.

## Ordered steps (cheapest invalidation first)
1. Write the S11 PLAN section with D1–D8 and the pass rules before any number.
2. Gate (a) on existing tables + FDSN sensor depths. Acceptance: amplitudes with errors for all four PGC-site instruments, per component. Failure signature: none — any outcome is informative.
3. Kernels (D1) with the Σ = 1 unit test. Failure: Σ off by > 2 % or a_d negative for > 10 % of pairs → fix or re-define the deep layer before continuing.
4. Measurement (D3) on the pilot windows, with the SVD column and its injection recovery. Failure: 3C 2–5 s raw per-cell σ at n ≥ 100 > 0.25 % at the boreholes → the study is a bound only; say so and continue.
5. Inversion (D4) + injection suite (D7 b). Failure: B > 50 % → change the headline quantity to "deep + regional shallow".
6. Gates (c)–(g). Failure of (d) or (e) → the D series is a station systematic; report the bound from the scrambled control and injection A only.
7. Scale to POLARIS and WA NW; repeat steps 3–6 per region; the LRIV quartet replicates step 2.

## What would overturn this design
Injection B < 25 % with D < 30 % and gate (d) passing → a per-episode deep term is a measurement; co-located instruments agreeing within errors with H/Z and lapse behaving as the deep kernel predicts → the annual cycle is not near-sensor.

## Executor note (main session)
The user fixed the measurement recipe as SVD-Wiener day stacks. This design demotes that to a QC column (D3) because of the pilot's measured shrinkage. Resolution adopted by the main session: run BOTH data paths through identical kernels and inversion; the injection recovery of the user's SVD 3C 2–5 s recipe (step 4) decides which is the headline. Recovery 0.9–1.1 → SVD path is headline; < 0.8 → raw episode stacks are headline and the SVD path is reported beside them. Logged in NOTES.md.

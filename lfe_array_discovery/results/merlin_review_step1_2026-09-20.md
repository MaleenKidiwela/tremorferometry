# Merlin audit of Step 1 (1a/1b/1c) and the repair decision — 2026-09-20 (verbatim; Fable 5.1 as Merlin)

**Model check**: Fable 5.1 (claude-fable-5-1), running as Merlin. Not downgraded.

## Verdict

The 1c FAIL is real, but it is a failure of the frozen *seed construction*, not of array discovery, and not of the data chain. I measured on the 2005 cache that the catalogued events are present at the predicted S times (envelope stacks of 95–97 same-family events peak within ±2 s of prediction at 4–14 MAD on every near site) but that a typical catalogued event carries S energy only 1.0–1.85× its own surrounding tremor (p90 2–5×), which caps single-window pair cc at ~0.1–0.2 — and the frozen 20 Hz 1–8 Hz cache gives numbers identical to two decimals with raw 100 Hz at 1–8, 2–8 and 3–8 Hz. So the per-pair statistic can only ever see the loud tail (which the engine does find: D3's 6× enrichment), and the "≥5-member component" seed rule throws that tail away. The single decision that matters: spend the one permitted repair on a pre-registered **pair-seeded growth** construction (Bostock's own bootstrap — few loud pairs → stack → matched-filter growth → iterate) with the FA controls moved to the growth/survival stage, together with four defect fixes — not on a T-only re-run, which I predict fails.

## Flaws (severity-ordered)

**F1 — MAJOR: a broken station sat in every sum, undetected.** C8.SHVB's horizontals carry a pure 4.76 Hz tone (raw `traces/bed2005_polaris/C8.SHVB/2005/259.mseed`, HHE PSD peak/median 7×10^8; horizontal/vertical rms ratio 400–2400 on 16 of the 24 ETS-window days per my scan of `cache20hz/C8.SHVB/2005/*.npz`; only doy 252–254 and 262–264 are sane). Any two SHVB windows correlate at cc≈0.98–1.00 at the right phase lag. In `results/step1diag_D5_true_pairs.csv` z_SHVB is the only site hitting the ±8 clip. Neither the engine (`scripts/w_autocorr.py`, only the flat-channel `VAL` mask at line 99) nor the 1c bed assembly (`results/step1c_bed.json`, delivery-based) has a station-health screen. Consequence: 1 of 12 sites injected structured garbage into every hour, quiet and tremor alike, and the "≥2 sites z≥3" clause could be half-satisfied by a tone. The WA bed has the same gap (the P4-0 contrast probe does not test for this failure mode).

**F2 — MAJOR: the quiet set is not the null population of the tremor run.** `scripts/w_common.py` lines 519–537: with 20 quiet days and a 40-hour target, `linspace(0, 23, 2)` yields hours {0, 23} only (`results/step1c_quiet_hours.csv`: 20×00h, 20×23h), while the 299 candidate hours span all 24 clock hours (5–18 per hour). Daytime transients (blasts, cultural sources) are never sampled by the null. On top of that, no earthquake screen: one regional event at 2005-07-09 23:48 sets T=12.50 alone (`results/step1diag_D4_leave_one_hour_out_T.csv` — verified: only that hour moves T, to 7.50). `results/freeze_wa_v1.json` inherits both (quiet_hours_per_day 2 → the same {0, 23}).

**F3 — MAJOR: statistic/regime mismatch — the accumulator exists but is unreachable.** Physics (my measurements below) says only ~1–5 % of same-family pairs are individually significant; D3 confirms the engine finds them (544/2000 vs 91 null). But `scripts/w_cluster.py` lines 76–87 require ≥5 members in an hour spanning ≥2 ten-minute bins before anything reaches growth, and `scripts/w_grow.py` line 90 refuses a station with <3 segments, so a 2-member seed cannot be handed off at all. The growth/survival/scramble stage — the only place SNR accumulates — never received the real pairs. Fixing T alone leaves this in place.

**F4 — MAJOR: the 2005 scoring is uncalibrated.** (a) The relative clauses use the 2012 template reference transferred unchanged (`results/step1c_score.json` "reference_note"); no oracle ceiling exists for this bed, so neither 0/10 nor a future 3/10 is interpretable against what the chain *could* do here. (b) The 2005 catalog times are quantised to whole seconds (100 % of 2005 rows in `lfe_catalog_repair/results/external/bostock/bostock2015_sloweq_FULL_2003-2013.csv` have integer `sec`; later years do not), so D5's "exact lag" column is meaningless for 2005 and its ±0.5 s search misses ~25 % of true lags — the write-up's per-site z medians are underestimates (direction of the verdict unchanged). (c) Six of the ten isolated hidden families have ≤2 events at M≥2.0 in the window and 130–277 events total (113, 119, 87) — the isolated stratum may be intrinsically thin on this bed; without a ceiling that is invisible.

**F5 — MAJOR (WA, parked): the 11-site spread subset also governs growth and location.** `scripts/x_common.py` lines 255–260 install the 11-site (mem, of, order, det) as `W.sites` for every stage, and `w_grow.handoff` takes its station list from `W.sites()`. The spread rule is needed only where windows are cut at a common absolute time; growth embeds moveout per station. GNW, PL01, B014, W060, B017, B018 are dropped from stages that could use them for nothing.

**F6 — MINOR: D4b NaN void** (`w_autocorr.py` line 100 clamp, line 127 `NaN×0`): 26/299 hours voided; confirmed exact correspondence in `results/step1diag_D4b.json`. Real bug, cannot explain 0/10.

**F7 — MINOR: D6-native never ran.** `scripts/y_step1diag.py` line 928 calls `t0.timestamp()`; on an obspy UTCDateTime `timestamp` is a property. My own chain test (below) supersedes it.

**F8 — MINOR: a freeze was edited in place.** `results/freeze_wa_v1.json` carries an `amendments` block re-hashing `w_diag.py`. Harmless here (the WA queue never executes w_diag) but freezes must be versioned (v2), never mutated.

**F9 — MINOR: D5's true-pair statistic is biased upward relative to its null.** `y_step1diag.py` lines 537–558: the row is chosen by argmax over ~24 alignments at the true lag, the null is drawn from that one chosen row. The 2.11-vs-1.78 gap is therefore optimistic; the true signal is even smaller.

**F10 — MINOR / SPECULATIVE: catalog magnitudes do not stratify anything.** Joining the 934 D5 pairs to `mag` (no GPU) gives Spearman ρ=0.03 (p=0.40) between the fainter event's magnitude and net_mad; M1.9–2.05 pairs read the same z≈2.1 as M<1.5. Either the within-family magnitude spread (~0.2) is inside its own error, or loud events occur in proportionally louder tremor. Do not use `mag` as an amplitude proxy on this bed.

## Q1 — Is the design a sound positive control, and what limits D5?

Design: the *array-then-permanent* control on 2005 POLARIS is the right bed and the orchestrator was correct to move there; my own Step-1 spec (2012 permanents, 46–91 km, 13.8 s spread against a 10-s common window) was geometrically incapable of the test, so 1a and 1b were not engine tests and do not count as failures. Per-hour vs per-day normalisation is irrelevant: the lower-half MAD of the cc distribution (~0.07, hence z_self≈14) is a property of window-vs-window cc noise, not of block length. Station count matters only as √N in the network sum; 12 sites versus Bostock's 20+ is a ×1.3–2 loss, which enlarges the visible loud tail but cannot bridge a factor 5–10 for typical events.

What limits D5, measured (recipes reproducible from the paths cited):

1. **Events are present at the predicted times.** Envelope stacks of family 104 (97 events, 2005-09-16 13h) and family 45 (95 events, 09-05 03h) cut at `t_origin + fam_tt` on the frozen cache peak at +0.2…+1.6 s (family 104) and −2…−6 s (family 45) relative to prediction, heights 4–15 MAD at GLBC/TWKB/MGCB/TSJB/TWBB/SILB/PGC. The time base (`p_common.bostock` offsets 3–21 s, median 12 s, fitted on 2012) transfers to 2005 to within a few seconds.
2. **Per-event SNR is the limit.** Each event's 3-s S-window energy over the median 3-s energy of its own ±60 s neighbourhood: median 1.21/1.72/1.85/1.16/1.05/1.45 at GLBC/TWKB/MGCB/TSJB/TWBB/SILB for family 104 (p90 1.9–5.5), 1.0–1.35 for family 45; a +30 s shifted control reads 0.95–1.05. The background is tremor made of other LFEs with the same spectrum, so a typical event is 0–45 % of its window's energy at the *best* station. Pair cc ≈ product of the two energy fractions → 0.05–0.2 typical, ~0.5–0.6 only when both events are in the top decile. That is exactly D2/D5's z≈2 and D3's enriched top-2000.
3. **The chain is not the limit.** Same family-hour, same windows, five stations, seven chain variants (frozen 20 Hz 1–8 Hz; raw 100 Hz at 1–8, 2–8, 3–8 Hz; 2–8 Hz decimated to 20 Hz; S-focused 6-s and 10-s windows): linear-stack coherence 0.86–1.09 (1 = incoherent), leave-one-out event-vs-stack cc median 0.11–0.23, pairwise cc median 0.11–0.25 / max 0.22–0.47 over 300 pairs — identical across variants to ±0.01. Band and rate buy nothing because signal and background share the spectrum.
4. **The "true pair" choice is the reason D5 looks hopeless**: all catalogued events, median energy excess ~1.2–1.8×, are exactly the population a per-pair statistic cannot see. Bostock's seeds came from the tail this engine already ranks at the top.

The one test that separates these — already done above on one family-hour — should be run as a frozen diagnostic (D8) over the 50 busiest kept family-hours: envelope-stack peak (time base), per-event energy excess distribution (physics), and the chain comparison. Outcome reading: excess distribution p90 ≥2 at ≥3 sites with chain-invariance → physics, proceed with the pair-seeded redesign; chain variants differing by >×1.5 in stack coherence → chain, stop and re-cache before anything else; envelope peaks absent or displaced >5 s → time base, fix `fam_tt`/offsets for 2005 before scoring anything.

## Q2 — Does the repair count, and what statistic?

The earthquake-in-quiet-set defect, the NaN bug, the SHVB station, the {0,23} quiet hours and the 2012 reference are *defects*; fixing them is owed regardless and does not consume the repair. The **repair** is the seed-rule change (F3), and it must be spent once, pre-registered, with the defects fixed in the same freeze. A T-only re-run is predicted to fail: at T=7.5 the bed has ~3,800 pairs of which ~30 % are real (D3), spread over ~4,700 kept family-hours — ~0.1–0.3 real edges per family-hour — so ≥5-member same-family components are rare, and 1a already showed 3 such components grow into 0 survivors. Do not burn the repair on it.

The repaired construction (prose; FA rule, seed rule, bars, cost) is the Prescription below. Its logic: per-pair evidence selects hypotheses, not claims; the accumulator is the existing growth stage (template-vs-continuous network matched filter, per-template quiet-day T_seed at ≤0.1 false hits per template-day, survival ≥20 independent hits on ≥3 days with ≤20 % near the seed, template-scramble ≥3×), and the family-level FA control is the existing whole-chain site-shift null — now also counted at the *survivor* level, which with ~2,000 seeds per realisation is the sensitive readout. Pair-graph density clustering across hours is a second-order improvement on top of this and is not required; a Brown-style whole-day sum then 8×MAD is the same per-pair statistic with a larger N² and no gain.

## Q3 — WA_GO

WA_GO may be created only when all of the following hold: (i) 1c′ passes as specified below with the *same* repaired engine hashes the WA queue will run; (ii) a **freeze_wa_v2** is written (v1 is superseded, not edited) that adopts hour-matched quiet hours, the earthquake and window-transient vetoes, the station-health veto census on the WA cache, the NaN fix, the pair-seed rule, and restricts the 11-site spread subset to the autocorrelation stage only (growth/iteration/location use all admitted non-withheld stations); (iii) `x_freeze.check_constants` passes against the 1c′ freeze; (iv) the WA quiet-hour T is re-derived under the new rules (the smoke T=6.50 is void).

Legitimate WA work that does not wait on the north control: (a) the Step-5b **injection mechanics** run on WA station-days — no claim, no constant tuned from its result, its outputs never enter a freeze; (b) the WA **station-health census** and hour-matched quiet set (CPU, prepares v2); (c) **Arm B** — but with a caveat the plan missed: the installed `seisbench` LFEDetect weights `cascadia`, `cms`, `jcms`, `jcs` are trained on Bostock et al. 2015 stacks (model card printed from the installed package), so a north control with them is a training-set test and proves nothing for WA. Arm B's north control on the 2005 bed is legitimate only with `jms` (Nankai + Guerrero + San Andreas, no Cascadia), and WA must then run the same weights; run LFEDetect(jms) → PyOcto → space-time clustering → stacks on the 880 2005 station-days and score with `w_score_step1.py` against the same 10 isolated families (≈1 GPU-h). This can run now, in parallel, and is the only path that survives a STOP of Arm A.

## Prescription — Step 1c′, the one repair (total ≈ 8–10 GPU-h; the 1c autocorrelation took 369 s for 299 hours per `results/step1c/ac_meta.json`, so autocorrelation cost is negligible here)

Order is chosen so the cheapest steps can invalidate the approach first, and so the ceiling is frozen before any discovery result is seen.

**1. D8 diagnostic (GPU/CPU, ≤1 h).** Over the 50 busiest kept family-hours: envelope-stack peak offset and height, per-event S-energy excess distribution per site (3-s window at predicted S over the median 3-s energy of ±60 s; +30 s control), and the chain comparison (frozen vs raw 2–8 Hz) on ≥5 of them. *Why*: converts my single-hour measurements into the frozen record and rules on chain vs physics before any code is changed. *Accept*: median envelope-peak offset within ±3 s at ≥4 sites; control excess 0.9–1.1; chain variants within ×1.2 in stack coherence. *Abandon-and-report*: chain variants differ by >×1.5 → re-cache at 2–8 Hz before anything; envelope peaks absent → time base wrong, fix `s_common.fam_tt`/offsets for 2005 first.

**2. Station-hour health veto (CPU, 30 min).** For every station-hour of the bed, quiet and tremor alike: veto if any component is flat (this replaces the NaN path — the hour still runs on the remaining stations), if horizontal/vertical rms ratio is outside [1/30, 30], or if the 1–8 Hz Welch PSD peak/median exceeds 10^4 (tone). Write `results/step1c_prime/station_hour_veto.csv` with reason codes. *Accept*: SHVB flagged on doy 241–251 and 255–261, GLBC/TWKB/MGCB/TSJB/TWBB unflagged; <30 % of any dense site's hours vetoed. *Abandon-and-report*: a dense site loses >30 % → the bed shrinks; recompute the spread rule and report.

**3. Quiet set (CPU, 30 min).** 40 hours on the 20 literature-quiet days with clock hours matched to the candidate-hour histogram (cycle 2 hours/day through 0–23 so every clock hour appears at least once). Exclude hours containing a catalogued earthquake (ComCat/NRCan: M≥2.5 within 300 km, or M≥5.5 anywhere with predicted arrival in the hour; a catalog query is not a waveform download). *Accept*: leave-one-hour-out T range ≤1.0 after the rule below. *Abandon*: LOO range >1.0 → another transient dominates; inspect that hour, do not proceed until it is explained.

**4. Engine fixes and window-transient veto (code, then autocorrelation ≈10 min GPU).** In `w_autocorr.autocorr_hour`: a station whose `ew.median()` is 0 is dropped from the hour (line 100), and NaN can never reach `sbuf` (line 127). Add a window-level veto applied identically to quiet and tremor hours: a 10-s window whose network-median energy (each site normalised by its own hour median) exceeds 10× is excluded both as row i and as lag position j. Re-run quiet and tremor hours, recording at ≥5.0 as before. *Accept*: 0 hours with zero pairs for non-veto reasons; the 2005-07-09 23h burst produces 0 pairs above 8 if that hour survives the earthquake screen.

**5. T_pair by the FA rule, robustified (CPU).** Keep the frozen rule (pooled ≤1 false pair per quiet hour AND zero quiet components ≥5, grid 0.25 from 5.0) on the new quiet set, and write the leave-one-hour-out T for every hour as a mandatory column. *Accept*: T binds by the FA rule between 6.5 and 8.5 with LOO range ≤1.0.

**6. Oracle ceiling and 2005 reference (GPU ≈30 min) — before Step 7 is read.** For each of the 30 hidden families: seed the frozen growth chain (handoff → quiet-day T_seed → growth → survival → 2 iterations → dedupe → locate) with the family's own catalogued events from its busiest hour (≤40, aligned by `align_stack` ±1.5 s because the times are 1-s quantised), score with `w_score_step1.py`. Write C_iso (of 10), C_30 (of 30) and per-family recall/purity as `results/step1c_prime/oracle_reference_2005.csv`; the scorer's relative clauses use this file instead of the 2012 `truth_template_reference.csv`. *Accept*: C_iso ≥4 → the isolated stratum is informative. *Fallback (pre-registered now)*: C_iso <4 → the pass bar becomes ≥0.5·C_30 (minimum 5) on all 30 hidden families.

**7. Seeds (CPU) and handoff.** Components (node merge 2 s, per day) of pairs ≥T_pair; every component with ≥2 nodes is a seed, ranked by its maximum pair net_mad then size; keep the top 2,000 (SEED_BUDGET raised from 600, recorded). Remove the ≥5-member and ≥2-bin clauses for this run (the window-transient veto and the survival stage replace the burst guard). In `w_grow.handoff` lower the per-station minimum from 3 to 2 segments (line 90); `align_stack`, onset search, 20-s pass-0 template and the ≥4-site requirement stay as frozen. *Accept*: ≥500 seeds handed off with ≥4 sites.

**8. Growth, survival, iterations, dedupe, locate, gates (GPU ≈1.5 h).** Exactly as frozen: per-template quiet-day T_seed (FA ≤0.1 false hits per template-day, engineering floor 5.0 reported), growth over the 24 bed days, survival (≥20 independent 60-s hits, ≥3 distinct days, ≤20 % within ±10 min of *either* seed window, scramble ≥3×), 2 iterations, dedupe, locate, Gates 0–2 with withheld JRBC/VGZ/SNB (Gate 1 low-power, noted).

**9. Whole-chain site-shift null ×3 (GPU ≈5 h).** As frozen (Steps 4–8 under each site-day shift), reporting per realisation both gated families and **survivors**.

**10. Score and rule.** PASS = ≥3/10 isolated (centroid ≤5 km, recall ≥0.5× and purity ≥0.8× the 2005 oracle reference) — or the Step-6 fallback — AND spurious-split ≤0.10 AND null gated ≤1 per realisation AND null survivors ≤10 % of real survivors. FAIL = <2/10 (or <0.4·C_iso) with clean nulls → STOP Arm A. FA-broken = null survivors ≥50 % of real → STOP Arm A. Freeze everything above in `results/freeze_step1c_prime.json` (new file, hashes of all repaired `w_` scripts, the veto table, the quiet-hour list, the oracle reference, the seed rule and bars) before Step 4 runs.

## Experiments I could not run

- **D8 over 50 family-hours** (Step 1): if the per-event energy-excess p90 is <1.5 at every site, even the loud tail is too thin for pair seeds and the pair-seeded design will fail — report before spending Steps 7–9.
- **Oracle ceiling** (Step 6): C_iso ≤2 means the isolated stratum cannot be passed by any engine on this bed and the fallback governs; C_iso ≥7 means a 3/10 result is a weak engine, not a strong one.
- **Arm B(jms) north control**: ≥3/10 → a WA path independent of Arm A exists; <2/10 → Arm B is corroboration only.
- **WA station-health census**: if any XG array has >20 % toned/degenerate station-hours, the site-probe admission was blind to it and the WA coverage numbers in `results/P_STEP2_SITES.md` are overstated.

## What would change my mind

Toward "wrong at the root": D8 showing chain variants differing by >×1.5 (then the cache, not the physics, is the ceiling and every 20-Hz result in this study is suspect); or the oracle ceiling itself <3 on the 2005 bed (then no seed rule can pass and the control bed is unusable). Toward "better than I think": Step 7 producing ≥50 real survivors before iteration, or 1c′ at ≥5/10 with null survivors ≤2 % — then WA should open immediately with freeze_wa_v2 and the Puget 2008 pass should be queued behind it rather than after Step 7.

# Merlin review of lfe_array_discovery PLAN v0 — 2026-09-20 (verbatim; Fable 5.1 as Merlin)

**Model check**: Fable 5.1 (claude-fable-5-1), running as Merlin. Not downgraded.

## Verdict

The idea is sound and, unlike P3D/P4, it has geometry on its side — but the draft measures that geometry in the wrong unit. After the project's own 1-km co-location merge, XG's 124 stations are **8 sites**, ZK's 97 are **1 site**, Z3's 47 are **4**, and CAFE is 44 sites in 2007–08 but only 7 in 2010 and 5 in 2011. Recomputed against PNSN-defined tremor-active cells, the corrected picture still supports the attempt, and it is the best network this project has ever had: **N-Olympic in Aug 2010 has a median of 12 quality sites within 60 km hypocentral of every active cell (100 % of cells ≥5 sites; 75 % have ≥5 sites within 30 km epicentral)**, versus 7 sites at 46–80 km on the P4-0b north bed. Puget's window is **2008-05 (CAFE)**, not 2010/2011 (where it has 0–3 sites). The single decision that matters most: **run the new autocorrelation engine first on the 2012 S-Vancouver-Island data already on disk, where Bostock's 130 families are truth**, before a WA byte is scored — it is the only positive control that can distinguish "engine cannot" from "WA data cannot", it needs no download, and it is the thing that must run while the downloads stream.

## Flaws in the draft (severity-ordered)

**F1 — MAJOR (unit of geometry).** `PLAN.md` §1 table and §7 ("densest: XG 115 + XU 19 + PB 19") count stations. The project's standing rule (memory `check-geometry-before-density-claims`; P4-0 §4.2 site merge) is that co-located sensors are one site for FA and for location. From `/tmp/casc_sta_service.earthscope.org.txt`: XG 124 stations → 8 clusters at 2 km (sizes 28, 27, 18, 11, 10, 10, 10, 10; lat 47.93–48.07, lon −123.47..−122.93); ZK 97 → **1** cluster (47.95–47.96 N / −122.93..−122.91); Z3 47 → 4 (one 33-station array at the ZK location, three 4–5-station mini-arrays at 47.15/47.46/47.72 N); XU 59 → 44 clusters. Consequence: the window ranking, the "2009-05 has XU 21 + Z3 23" question, and the compute model all inherit the wrong unit. Corrected per-window *site* counts in the box (1-km merge): 2007-01 XU 43 + PB 6; 2008-05 XU 44 + ZK 1 + YW 6 + PB 16; 2009-05 XU 7 + Z3 4 + PB 16; 2010-08 XG 8 + XU 7 + PB 16 + TA 5; 2011-08 XG 3 + XU 5 + PB 16.

My recomputation (sites = temporaries + PB + CN + the four probe-admitted UW stations alive then; cells = Slab2 28–45 km points with ≥30 PNSN 2010–11 epicentres within 8 km, median depth 40 km):

|---|---|---|---|

Puget is a CAFE-era (2007/2008) problem; N-Olympic is an XG-era (2010, and 2011 thinner) problem; S-Olympic is workable in 2008 and 2010. The PNSN daily latitude track also shows the 2010 Puget tremor passage was only 07-25 and 09-03..09-07 — the draft's 2010 window would capture almost no Puget tremor even with a network.

**F3 — MAJOR (the F4 defect is carried forward unfixed).** §3 says "growth-threshold floors must be exercised by the FA rule", but `lfe_picker/scripts/v_common.py` line 84 still sets `T_FLOOR = 8.0` and `v_engine.run_day` applies `max(thr, floor)` (the `tb = ... np.maximum(thr[bs], floor)` line); the close-out's C-2 measured 0.012 false hits/template-day against the 0.1 allowance, i.e. the FA rule never bound. The same file hard-codes `SITE_MEMBERS` as a 7-site dict (lines 63–71); with 160 stations the merge must be data-driven. Also C-8's `station_z` gap-MAD bug (median/MAD over all samples including gaps) is bounded at +6 % on the north but temporaries are gappier — fix before reuse. The draft names none of these as prerequisites.

**F4 — MAJOR (claim without truth is under-specified).** §5 lists Gate 1 "at withheld stations", but `GATES_RESULTS.md` §6 blind spot 7 says the Gate-1 control table is regional: without a WA control, a non-PASS is UNTESTABLE, not FAIL, and the plan has no way to say "the withheld sites could have seen it". The "transplanted northern hp events" idea is right but must be specified as an *injection* (north per-station templates scaled and inserted into withheld-station days at a WA cell's moveout) and it can test only mechanics and location accuracy — `notes/2026-09-11_Notes.md` records E3 step 3: injected stacks recover 4–11× off real at matched s/n, so injection cannot set a sensitivity ceiling.

**F5 — MAJOR (withheld design is a count, not a rule, and is infeasible as written).** "3 PB + 2 UW per sub-region": in 2007–2011 the only P4-admitted UW stations that exist are HDW (1979–), GNW (1995–), LRIV (2009-08–, co-located with B005/6/7 → one site) and DOSE (2010-07–). The close-out's F7 lesson (choose withheld to minimise coverage loss, and they must *register*) is not written down, and the conflict with the extension (the same permanents are the whole post-2011 network) is not addressed.

**F6 — MAJOR (Gate 3 cannot run in discovery).** `GATES_RESULTS.md` §2(c): the circular-shift null needs ≥61 d and every in-window day is a local-tremor day, so Gate 3 is UNTESTABLE by construction on any single ETS window — regardless of which tremor catalog exists pre-2010. §5 and Q3 treat it as a discovery gate. Tremor-timing evidence in discovery is only the weaker local-vs-far *day* contrast; Gate 3 proper comes from the extension record ≥2010.

**F7 — MAJOR (location bar not certifiable).** §4 "≤3 km horizontal, ≤5 km depth" — `GATES_RESULTS.md` §4 and blind spot 5: controls certify that a location is data-driven and stable, not accurate (median 5.6 km proposal-to-solution at N=200 in the north while all controls passed). Without truth, accuracy can only be *estimated* from the injection control.

**F8 — MINOR (ETS dates).** PNSN daily counts (`catalogs/pnsn_tremor_cascadia_full.csv`): 2010 first day ≥50 epicentres in the box is 07-25 (S-WA), S-Olympic 08-08..08-16, N-Olympic 08-15..08-28; 2011: 07-24 (S-WA), S-Olympic 08-05..08-15, N-Olympic 08-16..08-30. XG sites BS and LC (10 stations each) were alive only 2010-08-10..09-02, so the "8-site XG" exists for 23 days; three arrays (18/27/28 stations) ran to 2011-09-10.

**F9 — MINOR (download rate).** "4 station-days/s, ~3 h": measured project rates are 1–2.3 station-days/s (P4-0 §1: 36 GiB in ~25 min; addendum A.1: 338 station-days in 2.5 min), and 100-Hz temporaries are ~3× the bytes of 40-Hz permanents. Plan on 6–10 h for the first window; not a blocker.

**F10 — MINOR (sample rates / short-period instruments).** `lfe_picker/scripts/o_cache_month.py` line 80 raises on non-integer decimation to 20 Hz; P4-0 §5 has a documented rational twin that requires an equivalence check before any template product. The Gate-0 HF class veto (`g0_hf_station = ("PGC","B011")`, cut 3.5 calibrated at PGC) must be moved to a WA 100-Hz borehole (B001 or B005) and its WA quiet-day distribution reported before the cut is applied; short-period array sensors are not valid HF-ratio stations.

**F11 — MINOR.** Arm C (P1/P2 picker) adds nothing to a claim (lfe_features is a fingerprint, not a detector); drop it from the gated path.

**F12 — MINOR but easy to get wrong.** §6 "realness score as in lfe_extension": Track E's `p_real` is a gradient-boosted model whose label is a one-to-one match to Bostock (`lfe_extension/results/trackE/RESULTS.md` §0). WA has no such label. WA realness must be the Track A 3b construction — withheld-station stack test on quiet-day detections per family per epoch — reported as an empirical fraction per tier, never the hgb model.

What is right and should stay: array-then-permanent is exactly how every published set was built; the frozen chain and the FA-only tiers are the correct extension recipe; per-day availability not intersected; pre-registration and hashes; "Opus agents launch shell queues and exit".

## Answers

**Q1 / (a) — Arm choice and the autocorrelation statistic.** Arm A first and alone for the first deliverable; Arm B (LFEDetect `from_pretrained`, PyOcto — both installed, `seisbench 0.12.5`) as an *independent seed source and descriptive corroboration* in week 2, on the same station-days; Arm C dropped. Feasibility: yes, days. The statistic, in prose:

- *Data*: the frozen chain — 1–8 Hz Butterworth-4 zero-phase at native rate, decimated to 20 Hz, 3-C ZNE (boreholes rotated from StationXML azimuths), one file per station-day as `cache_one` writes it. Rational-rate stations go through the P4-0 twin and are flagged.
- *Sites*: data-driven merge of stations within 1 km into sites (XG arrays become 8 sites; B005/6/7+LRIV one; B013+B943 one). Members are **not** beamformed; each member keeps its own window, so intra-array delays are irrelevant.
- *Blocks*: 1-hour segments on candidate hours. For 2010/2011, candidate hour = ≥5 PNSN epicentres within 50 km of any active cell of the sub-region (the P4-0b rule). For 2007–2009 see Q3.
- *Windows*: 10 s (200 samples) at 0.5-s hop → ~7,180 windows per hour, cut at the **same absolute time at every station, no moveout model**. Same-source events share their inter-station moveout, so the network correlation peaks at a common lag without knowing the cell (Brown et al. 2008's construction; Bostock et al. 2012 applied it hour by hour). 10 s accommodates the ~5–6 s S moveout spread across 45 km epicentral at 40 km depth plus 2–3 s of S energy.
- *Per station*: for window i, the normalised 3-C mean cc against the whole hour at that station, converted to a lower-half-MAD z over the hour (exactly `DayData.station_z`, with the median/MAD restricted to valid samples — the C-8 fix). Per site: mean of member z, clipped at 8 (`SITE_Z_CLIP`). Network: equal-weight site sum, re-normalised over lag by its own median/lower-half MAD (`net_mad`, as `run_day` does). Exclude |lag| < 10 s.
- *Hit*: net_mad ≥ T AND ≥2 sites with site-z ≥ 3 AND ≥4 sites observable (raise `MIN_SITES` from 3 to 4 for the array). Peaks declustered at 10 s (not 30 s — LFEs within tremor are seconds apart).
- *T*: FA-only, no 8.0 floor. On ≥40 quiet hours (≥20 quiet days × 2 hours, same sites, no PNSN tremor within 100 km and ≥3 days from any tremor day), T = smallest value on a 0.25 grid such that false pairs ≤1 per quiet hour AND zero quiet-hour components of size ≥5. Set the engineering floor at 5.0 so the rule can bind; report where it binds.
- *Pairing → clustering*: nodes = window times (merged at 2 s), edges = hits; connected components; a component is a **seed cluster** if ≥5 members within the hour (or ≥8 across the hour and its two neighbours) and its members span ≥2 distinct 10-minute bins (burst guard).
- *Handoff to growth*: per cluster, `align_stack` (2 iterations, ±1.5 s) per station → stack → 20-s pass-0 template per station cut around the stack's energy onset (the origin is unknown; the moveout is embedded in the stack, which is all growth needs) → `v_engine` growth over the whole window with per-day availability, T from the FA-only rule on the quiet days (no floor), survival = ≥20 independent hits (60 s), ≥3 distinct days, ≤20 % of hits within ±10 min of the seed hour, and a **template-scramble null** in place of the N-moveout null (each station's template cut from a different random tremor window and grown identically; require real hits ≥3× scramble hits) → two to three iterations (12-s templates from S−2 s after the first re-pick) → dedupe by inter-template cc at ≥3 sites and by hit-set Jaccard ≥0.5 → locate → gates.
- *Cost*: a 7,180-window hour over ~160 stations is ~48k template-station-day-equivalents, i.e. ~50 s at the measured 940 tpl-sta-days/s → N-Olympic 2010 (~14 tremor days ≈ 336 hours + 40 quiet) ≈ 5 GPU-h; all windows and sub-regions ≈ 30 GPU-h. Growth: ≤2,000 seed templates × 45 days × 160 stations ≈ 1.4e7 tpl-sta-days ≈ 4 h per pass; three passes plus three null realisations ≈ 1–2 GPU-days for one window. Gates ≈ 4 s per proposal (CPU). LFEDetect ≈ hours over ~10k station-days.

**Q2 — Sub-regions, windows, minimum sets.** Sub-regions: N-Olympic 47.8–48.3 N / −123.7..−122.7 (XG footprint + B001/B003/B005-cluster/B013-cluster); S-Olympic 47.3–47.8; Puget–S.WA 46.2–47.3. Rule for a (sub-region, window) to run: ≥5 quality sites within 60 km hypocentral of ≥80 % of its active cells **and** three withheld permanent sites within 60 km of ≥80 % of the cells. Passing pairs, in order: **N-Olympic 2010-08** (100 %), **Puget 2008-05** (98 %), S-Olympic 2008-05 (98 %) and 2010-08 (91 %), Puget 2007-01 (85 %), N-Olympic 2011-08 (98 %, sealed), S-Olympic 2007/2009/2011 (91–96 %, thin). Not passing: Puget 2009/2010/2011. So 2009-05 is worth running only for S-Olympic and only after the others.

**Q3 — Tremor timing before 2010.** Reframe: Gate 3 is UNTESTABLE inside any single window (F6), so pre-2010 timing is needed only for (i) selecting candidate hours and (ii) selecting quiet days. Sources to try, in order: the Ide (2012) and Wech WTD Cascadia catalogs in the Slow Earthquake Database (coverage not verified by me — check on fetch; the PNSN product itself begins 2009-08 per PNSN's own description, but the on-disk file starts 2010-01-06); the published ETS dates; and, as fallback for hour selection only, the array's own hourly network-median 2–8 Hz energy relative to the window's quiet-day distribution — a coarse tremor-presence indicator, legitimate for *hour* selection because P4-0b's negative was about energy as an *event seed*, not as an hour flag. Quiet days for CAFE/PB in 2007–08 come from months away from the ETS. Gate 3 proper is evaluated once on the extension record ≥2010.

**Q4 — Location.** Per-station iterated stacks; S on √(H1²+H2²) within ±3 s and P on Z within ±2.5 s of the prediction, envelope onset + AIC, accept at peak/noise-rms ≥3 (the g_locate/a5 recipe). For arrays pick each member and take the site median. Crosson 1-D (a Puget Sound model — more appropriate here than on Vancouver Island) plus per-station corrections from catalogued intraslab earthquakes M ≥2, 25–50 km depth, inside the WA box over each array's deployment span (the `a5_eq_corrections.py` recipe with its hard-coded `STAS` list replaced; XG 2009-06..2011-09 and CAFE 2006–2012 have years of events, ZK has 3 months — report n per station). Grid: 2-D Slab2 interface, `grid_lat0` 48.0, `grid_lon0` −123.2, halfwidth 62 km, plus the free-depth 3-D sanity run (`g_common.LAT_REF = 48.6` and the cos(48.6) at `g_gates.py` line 266 are a ~1 % distance error at 48.0 — acceptable, note it). "Located" = ≥5 picked sites within 60 km, azimuthal gap ≤180°, shuffled-pick null ratio <0.5, synthetic floor ≤3 km, leave-one-site-out ≤5 km, free depth within ±10 km of Slab2 and not at a bound. Accuracy is *estimated* from the injection control (known cell), reported as a distribution, never certified from controls.

**Q5 / (b) — Gates, nulls, sealed window, claim rule.** The discovery arms produce proposals (member times on the origin axis + cell; origin time = site S pick − Crosson travel time − correction after location; before location use `offset_search_s = 30`, the tested value, and read `offset_fit_s` back). The withheld sites are in no autocorrelation sum, no growth sum and no stack, for the whole run. Claim ladder, pre-registered: **candidate** = N ≥20, Gate 0 PASS (HF veto at a WA 100-Hz borehole; distinct from every already-accepted WA family by the adopted registering-site rule), Gate 1 PASS at ≥1 withheld site; **gated family** = Gate 1 PASS at **≥2 withheld sites** (both nulls: 200 random-time p99 with ≥+3 dB excess, and 100 moveout-shuffled p95) AND "located" per Q4 AND the whole-chain null condition; **confirmed recurrent family** = re-fires in the sealed 2011-08 window (or in ≥2 later ETS in the extension) with Gate 3 PASS on the long record. Whole-chain null = the entire pipeline (autocorrelation → clusters → growth → iteration → dedupe → gates) re-run ≥3 times on the same station-days with each *site's* day-series circularly shifted by a site-specific whole-day offset (members shift together, so site-coherent noise is preserved and true coherence destroyed); the count of null "gated families" is the family-level false-alarm rate; the real count must exceed 5× the null mean and the null mean must be ≤1. Positive controls: (i) the north engine test (Step 1 below) — the decisive one; (ii) injection of scaled northern per-station 12-s templates (PGC/B011/B926 class, assigned to WA stations of matched distance) into withheld-station and array days at a chosen active cell's moveout, ~40 events on tremor days at three amplitude levels spanning the north's observed registration — verifies the chain finds a planted repeater, that Gate 1 fires at the withheld sites at the planted N, and gives the location-error distribution. Chestler & Creager's derived coordinates: written into the freeze as a *descriptive prediction*; after the gated list is frozen, report the distribution of nearest-Chestler distances against 1,000 draws of the same number of random active cells — never used to select, merge, or locate. Arm B agreement (times ±2 s, cell within 5 km) is reported as corroboration, not a gate.

**Q6 — Registration and extension.** For each gated family: stack every permanent WA station at the array-era hit times (alignment-free, at the located cell's moveout), S-excess vs 200 random-time nulls → per-station registration verdict; 12-s templates from S−2 s in the frozen npz format **from the permanent station's own stack, never transferred array waveforms** (instrument responses differ). Network epochs for WA: W0 2007-01..2009-08 (PB partial: 6 sites in 2007, 16 by mid-2008; HDW, GNW), W1 2009-08..2014 (PB 16 + LRIV + DOSE), W2 2015..2021 (+ WINDI/SKOKO/HERD/OSQM/DOORS/EQUIL/COYL/WYNO as they appear — read exact epochs from StationXML), W3 2021+ (SNB-style losses, check per StationXML). FA-only tiers per epoch exactly as `c_tiers_by_epoch.py`: T_core ≤0.1 quiet false hits per template-day, T_ext ≤1.0 quiet and ≤1.5 post-SSE, measured on ≥20 quiet family-days per epoch, no floor. Completeness by station-set ablation as Track D §1a; realness by the 3b withheld-stack fraction per tier (F12). Download ordering to avoid idling is in (d).

## Prescription

**Step 0 — Freeze v1 before any WA byte is scored (CPU, hours).** Write `lfe_array_discovery/results/freeze_v1.json`: sub-regions, windows (PNSN-corrected), the site-merge rule, the autocorrelation constants above, the FA-only rules (no 8.0 floor), survival clauses, dedupe rule, the location controls, the claim ladder, the withheld sites per sub-region, the sealed 2011-08 set, the injection design, the Chestler comparison protocol, and SHA-256 of every script. Withheld sites, chosen to minimise coverage loss and to *register*: N-Olympic — B013/B943 cluster, HDW, GNW (all within 60 km of ≥80 % of N-Olympic cells; B001 and the B005 cluster stay in the sum because the extension needs them and the arrays supply discovery power); Puget/S-Olympic (2008) — B018, B941, GNW or HDW (whichever is not needed for the ≥5-site rule at ≥80 % of cells; compute it, write it). Acceptance: freeze written and hashed before the first WA GPU pass. Failure signature: any WA scoring pass whose scripts do not match the freeze → discard, re-freeze, re-run.

**Step 1 — North positive control of the autocorrelation engine (GPU ~3–5 h, no download).** Bed: `lfe_catalog_repair/cache20hz` (20 stations, 2010 and 2012 north data on disk; 1,332 day files), withheld VGZ/SNB/B926 as in the Gates suite, hidden families per `lfe_picker/results/holdouts.json` (30 hidden). Run the full Arm-A chain on the 2012 ETS tremor hours exactly as specified for WA (autocorrelation → clusters → growth → iterate → dedupe → locate → Gates 0–2), then score with `g_rediscover.py` against the calibrated ceiling (the frozen template detector itself scores 9/30, 5/10 isolated). Accept: ≥3 of the 10 isolated hidden families rediscovered (centroid ≤5 km, ≥30 % one-to-one recall, ≥50 % purity), spurious-split rate ≤0.1, and the whole-chain site-shift null (3 realisations) yields ≤1 gated "family" per realisation. Failure signature: <2/10 with null ≤1 → the engine cannot do on 15 stations what the plan needs; fix (window length, hop, clustering, floor) on the north *only* and re-run; do not touch WA until it passes. Null ≥3 per realisation → the FA rule is broken; stop.

**Step 2 — Metadata and site table (CPU, 1 h).** From the channel-level StationXML: sample rate, components, sensor type per station per epoch; flag EHZ-only stations (excluded from all sums), rational-rate stations (twin path + equivalence check per P4-0 §5), and short-period sensors (excluded from HF-ratio duty). Build the data-driven site table (1 km). Accept: every station in the download list has a row with fs, n_components, sensor class, site id. Failure signature: XG/ZK are vertical-only → the array-site benefit shrinks to Z-only cc; re-estimate coverage counting only 3-C sites before proceeding.

**Step 3 — Array-era quiet-hour FA and hour selection (GPU 1 h).** On the 2010 quiet days: derive T for the autocorrelation stage and the growth stage per the rules in Q1. Report where the FA rule binds; report the number of quiet-hour components (must be 0 at T). Accept: T derived from measured FA, not a floor. Failure: quiet hours contain ≥5 components at every T ≤ 20 → site-coherent noise dominates; inspect which sites; remove them by the far-tremor/contrast probe (Step 4) and re-derive.

**Step 4 — Array-era site probe (CPU, 1 h; data already on disk).** P4-0's statistic on the array stations: native 2–8 Hz tremor/quiet contrast on 6 N-Olympic tremor days (08-16..08-28) vs 6 quiet days, local−far ≥3 dB using S-WA-only days (07-25, 09-03..07) as "far". Admit stations at ≥4/6 days ≥3 dB and local−far ≥3 dB; report per array how many members pass. Accept: ≥6 XG sites with ≥5 admitted members each. Failure: <4 XG sites admitted → the N-Olympic geometry advantage is gone; report before spending growth compute.

**Step 5 — Discovery on N-Olympic 2010 (GPU ~1–2 days including nulls).** Autocorrelation on candidate hours → clusters → growth → 2–3 iterations → dedupe → locate → Gates 0–2, withheld B013/B943, HDW, GNW never summed; then the whole-chain site-shift null ×3 and the injection control (Step 5b, same day). Accept for continuing to Step 6: ≥5 gated families (Gate 1 at ≥2 withheld sites, located per Q4) with null mean ≤1 and real ≥5× null; injected repeater recovered, gated, and located within 5 km at the middle amplitude. ## Honest priors

Step 1 passes at ≥3/10: 55 %. Given Step 1 passes: ≥5 gated N-Olympic 2010 families with clean nulls: 60 %; ≥10: 40 %; 0–2: 25 %. Unconditional ≥10 gated Washington families (N-Olympic + Puget 2008) by the end of the second window: ~35 %. ≥10 gated families registered on ≥3 permanents and re-firing in 2011: ~25 %. Puget 2008 ≥5 gated: 30 %. Oregon ≥5: 25 %. Extension per-family completeness will be in the north's 0.13–0.2 class or lower — a bound, not a catalog, for most families.

## What would change my mind

Toward "wrong at the root": Step 1 failing at <2/10 after one repair cycle, or Step 3 finding quiet-hour components at every T — the engine or the array noise, not geometry, would then be the ceiling, and no window choice fixes it. Toward "better than I think": Step 1 at ≥6/10 with spurious-split ≤0.05, or Step 5 returning ≥10 gated families whose injected-control location error is ≤3 km — then the 3-km bar in the draft becomes defensible and the Puget 2008 pass should start immediately rather than after Step 7.

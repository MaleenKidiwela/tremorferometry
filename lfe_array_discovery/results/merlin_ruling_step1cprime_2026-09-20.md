# Merlin ruling on Step 1c′ (oracle abandon) and the final specification — 2026-09-20 (verbatim; Fable 5.1 as Merlin)

**Model check**: Fable 5.1 (claude-fable-5-1), running as Merlin. Not downgraded.

## Verdict

C_30 = 0 is neither a bed verdict nor a duration/gate mismatch: it measures that the oracle templates were incoherent stacks, because the 2004–2005 rows of the Bostock export carry whole-second times (100 % integer `sec`, versus 2.2–2.6 % in every other year) and `align_stack` cannot recover sub-second alignment at per-event SNR < 1 — so the oracle protocol I specified (≤40 busiest-hour events, "aligned by align_stack ±1.5 s because the times are quantised") was my second mis-specified control after the 2012 bed. The executor's three supporting measurements do not hold up: the "24 % own-day recall" is self-detection (passing count = seed count in all four families), the "decisive" 400-event control ran at the engineering floor with no T_seed and fires up to 1.4×/quiet-day against a rule of 0.1, and the gate is demonstrably passable per episode (every hidden family has 55–431 sixty-second-independent catalogued events in the 24-day bed; ≥20 is ~10 % recall, which the 2012 truth templates achieved at 46–91 km). The single decision that matters: do not STOP and do not spend GPU on a second bed; run a corrected oracle plus a truth-seeded bootstrap-convergence test (both on disk, ≤4 GPU-h) whose pre-registered outcome selects the frozen or a staged survival order for ONE final blind run — after which the study closes either way.

## Flaws (severity-ordered)

**F1 — MAJOR (executor's diagnosis): the "own-day recall 11–43 %" is self-detection.** `results/step1c_prime/evidence/c_clause_budget.csv` gives n × frac_ALL = 358×0.1117 = 40.0, 83×0.4337 = 36.0, 192×0.1979 = 38.0, 129×0.2791 = 36.0; the seed counts for those families in `results/step1c_prime/oracle_reference_2005.csv` are 40, 36, 37, 36. `evidence/bc_templates.csv` H0008: 27 hits, self_detect 0.675 × 40 seeds = 27 — every hit is a constituent. An N-window average of noise correlates with each constituent at ≈1/√N by construction, which a 12-site network sum lifts to net_mad ≈ 10 — exactly the nm_max 11.8–14.9 in `c_netmad_at_catalogued_events.csv`. Consequence: "templates are correct" and "20–46 hits at scramble ratios up to 17" are unsupported; the only non-circular number is 1–13 outside-seed hits against an FA expectation of up to 4.4 over 44 growth days.

**F2 — MAJOR (executor's diagnosis): the 400-event "decisive control" did not run the frozen chain.** `evidence/e_allevent_stacks.log` shows growth only (no quiet stage). The hits table (scratchpad `allev/hits.parquet`, which I split by day): A0008 has 28 hits on the 20 quiet days (1.4 per template-day vs `FA_PER_TPL_DAY = 0.1`, `w_common.py:144`) and 72 % of all its hits below net_mad 5.5; A0010 1.0/quiet-day; A0015/A0062 24–38/quiet-day. Only A0012 (3 quiet hits, 62 ETS-day independent hits on 11 days, median 5.72) and possibly A0010 would survive a per-template T_seed. "4 of 6 clear the frozen bar outright" is false; the honest count is 1–2 of 6.

**F3 — MAJOR (mine): the oracle relied on an alignment that cannot work on this catalogue.** `lfe_picker/scripts/s_common.py:489–505`: `align_stack` aligns each event to the running mean stack within ±30 samples, two passes, starting from the unaligned mean. With uniform ±0.5 s jitter the coherent gain of a plain stack is |sinc(f·1 s)|: zero at every integer Hz and ≤0.21 anywhere in 1–8 Hz, so the initial reference is noise and the realignment locks onto noise. Measured: `results/step1c_prime/d8_envelope.csv` stack coherence median 1.002 (p10 0.942, p90 1.06; 1 = incoherent) at median 68 events; `d8_chain.csv` 0.985–0.999 in all three chain variants; `evidence/c_stack_vs_N.csv` peak-MAD median 4.5 → 3.95 and S-SNR 2.6 → 1.3 dB from N = 5 to 160 — flat, the incoherent-stack signature, not "tremor made of the same family". I measured coherence ≈1 in my first audit and misread it as physics. Consequence: the frozen chain's ceiling on this bed is unmeasured, not zero; the fallback bar computed from C_30 = 0 is void. The discovery path is NOT affected (pair seeds and growth hits are cc-peak-aligned); only the oracle is.

**F4 — MAJOR (Q1 premise): the frozen gate is passable per episode.** From the catalogue (`lfe_catalog_repair/results/external/bostock/bostock2015_sloweq_FULL_2003-2013.csv`, families keyed as `lfe_picker/scripts/p_common.py:175`): the 30 hidden families have 109–1,109 events in 2005-08-29..09-21 (median 456), on 7–16 active days (median 11), 55–431 sixty-second-independent events (median 226; 40–408 outside the busiest hour, median 212), and 29/30 have ≥3 days with ≥3 independent events outside the busiest hour (bkey 125 has 2). ≥20 independent hits is therefore ~10 % recall; `lfe_picker/results/p3/truth_template_reference.csv` records recall_ref 0.06–0.13 for the 2012 truth templates at 46–91 km, and the scorer's ceiling note says those passed 9/30, 5/10 isolated on a 51-day window. Consequence: "a single episode cannot feed the ≥20-hit gate by construction" is false; option (b) as a standalone fix rests on it.

**F5 — MINOR (chain defect): growth and survival include the FA-calibration days.** `w_common.py:449–454` `bed_days()` returns every cached day of the year = 44 (24 ETS + 20 quiet); `results/step1c_prime/oracle/p0_indep.parquet` has 30 of 472 independent hits on quiet days (1–2 per template, as the FA rule guarantees), and `w_grow.survive` (lines 236–238) counts them in n_indep and n_days. Consequence: two of the "≥3 distinct days" can be quiet-day false alarms. Fix in the final spec: survival counts ETS-window days only; quiet-day hits are reported as the empirical FA.

**F6 — MINOR: the v2 oracle rule and the discovery rule differ**, so v2's "outside the seed window" count was not a ceiling of what the discovery path faces (`scripts/z_oracle.py:97–146`). Moot once the oracle seeds are spread over the window (below), where the near-seed clause does not bind and the frozen rule applies unchanged.

**F7 — MINOR (rule reading): Arm B's failure closes Arm B, not the study.** My "Arm B(jms) <2/10 → STOP the study" was the terminal condition for the case where Arm A had already stopped ("the only path that survives a STOP of Arm A"). Arm A's one repair has not been spent: stages 7–10 never ran; the queue stopped in a calibration stage I mis-specified. Arm B itself was a fair test and stays closed: `results/freeze_armB.json` used the 20 Hz 1–8 Hz jms card on raw data, S-only 3-station association; 95 events at 0.3 with depth median 28.53 km against a prior floor of 28.0, 5 clusters vs 3 in the null, 0 located (`results/ARMB_DONE`).

The bed is usable (catalogued events are present, the loud tail is enriched 11× — 2,553 pairs ≥ 8.0 in 299 tremor hours vs 31 in 40 quiet hours, `results/step1c_prime/ac.parquet`, `T.json`), and the gate is passable per episode (F4). C_30 = 0 measures incoherent oracle templates (F3). Bostock grew families over years because he had years; his families in one episode are as productive as F4 shows. Was my Step-1 design mis-specified again? Yes — the oracle protocol (busiest-hour ≤40 events, align_stack against 1-s-quantised times), which I own. The gate ORDER has not been shown wrong by any evidence on file; whether a 2-event seed can bootstrap through a pass-0 gate is untested and is the actual question of the study.

## Specification — final, the only remaining run

**Step 1 (CPU, ≤1 h) — quantisation A/B and re-alignment.** For 5 hidden families with ≥300 events in the bed, at the shared permanents CN.PGC and PB.B003 (present in `lfe_array_discovery/cache20hz/` for 2005 and `lfe_catalog_repair/cache20hz/` for 2012): plain linear 3-C stack of ≥100 catalogued 2012 events at t_origin + fam_tt (sub-second times), and the same for 2005 events (quantised), through `z_common.stack_coherence`. Then correlate the 2012 stack against each 2005 event window within ±1.0 s at those stations; the cc-peak lag is the corrected 2005 time; restack the 2005 events at the corrected times at the two nearest POLARIS sites. *Why*: proves or kills F3 before anything is rebuilt, and produces the corrected times the oracle needs. *Accept*: 2012 coherence ≥ 2 at ≥1 shared station for ≥3 of 5 families; 2005 quantised coherence ≤ 1.2; lag distribution spread ≥ 0.5 s; corrected-time POLARIS coherence ≥ 2 at ≥2 sites. *Abandon-and-report*: 2012 coherence ≈ 1 too → the stacking machinery or the time base is broken independent of quantisation, and the 2012 reference was itself obtained with incoherent templates — audit `s_common.align_stack` and `fam_tt` before any further step.

**Step 2 (GPU ≈1 h) — corrected oracle ceiling and 2005 reference.** For each of the 30 hidden families: seed with up to 400 catalogued events spread evenly over the 24-day window at the Step-1-corrected times (the engine's own `MAX_STACK` cap, `w_grow.py:88–89`), t_seed/t0/t1 set so the near-seed clause does not apply, then the frozen chain unchanged — per-template quiet-day T_seed, growth, frozen survival with ETS-day-only counting (F5), 2 iterations, dedupe, locate, `w_score_step1`. Write C_30, C_iso, per-family recall_ref/purity_ref against denominators (i)–(iii) to a NEW `oracle_reference_2005_v3.csv`. *Why*: the ceiling the bars are defined against. *Accept*: C_30 ≥ 3; if C_iso < 4 the pre-registered fallback governs (≥ max(5, 0.5·C_30) of 30). *Abandon*: C_30 < 3 with Step-1 coherence verified → the frozen chain cannot pass with the best possible templates → STOP the study, and the write-up says "this chain", not "the archive".

**Step 3 (GPU ≈2 h) — truth-seeded bootstrap-convergence test.** Take the stage-7 seed set exactly as frozen (every ≥2-node component of pairs ≥ T_pair = 8.0, top 2,000 by max net_mad). Mark a component a true seed of hidden family k if ≥2 nodes fall within ±2.5 s of t_origin + median fam_tt − 1 s of two distinct catalogued events of k (D3's matching, `y_step1diag.py` ~550), and report the chance count from a +300 s catalogue shift; let K = number of hidden families with ≥1 true seed. Run the true seeds through (A) the frozen order and (B) a staged order: pass-0 growth at T_seed → continuation filter (≥5 independent hits on ≥2 ETS days AND ≥2× the template-scramble twin — a compute filter, not a claim) → `v_iterate` with use_all and min_indep 5 → growth → iterate → growth → the frozen survival gate with every clause, ETS days only → dedupe → locate → score. Scramble twins go through (B) identically. *Why*: the study's actual question — can a true pair bootstrap to a family here — answered with truth-selected seeds and no blind result. *Accept-A*: rediscovered ≥ max(3, 0.3·K) under the frozen order → no chain change, Step 5 runs as frozen. *Accept-B*: A fails, B rediscovers ≥ max(3, 0.3·K) with at least one isolated family, AND scramble twins yield ≤ 10 % as many survivors → the staged order is the final specification. *Abandon*: neither, or scramble twins ≥ 50 % of true-seed survivors under B (FA amplifier) → STOP the study. Also abandon if chance-matched seeds exceed 30 % of matched seeds (the truth labels are not meaningful).

**Step 4 (spec) — `results/freeze_step1c_prime_v3.json`, new file.** Records: the selected order, ETS-day-only counting, continuation constants, `oracle_reference_2005_v3.csv`, and the unchanged bars (≥3/10 isolated at centroid ≤5 km, recall ≥0.5× and purity ≥0.8× the v3 reference — or the fallback; split ≤0.10; null gated ≤1; null survivors ≤10 % of real; FAIL <2/10 or <0.4·C_iso; FA-broken ≥50 %). Hashes of all scripts. Written before Step 5 starts.

**Step 5 (GPU 6–8 h) — the one blind run.** Stages 7–10 with site-shift null ×3 under v3. Score. PASS → WA_GO conditions of my first audit apply with v3 in place of 1c′; FAIL or FA-broken → STOP Arm A and WA; the study closes (Arm B is closed).

**Step 6 (only after a PASS; download ≈900 station-days) — 2004-07 re-fire.** POLARIS ran 2004-04 → 2005-10 (`results/fdsn_stations_40-50N_2003-2014.txt`: GLBC, LCBC from 2004-03/04; SHVB, JRBC, MGCB, TWBB, TWKB, TSJB, SILB, SSIB all up; TWGB extra); the 2004 episode has 59,659 events over 18 busy days (median 564 per hidden family). Run the 2005 survivors' templates over 2004-07-01..07-31 with a 2004 quiet-day T; a family re-fires if ≥20 independent hits on ≥3 days with scramble ≥3. Report the re-fire fraction as a confirmation statistic, never as a pass condition. 2007-01 is worthless (3,147 events; only MGCB, TWBB, PPSB left).

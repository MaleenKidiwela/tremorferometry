# Track E — per-event realness score `p_real`

Generated by `scripts/e_results.py` from the output files. Model `hgb`, 2012 training year, 2010 held-out validation year.

## 0. What p_real is

`p_real` is the **calibrated probability that a detection is matched one-to-one (±2 s) to a Bostock (2015) event of its OWN family**, given per-event features only. That is the only realness label that exists: Bostock's catalog is the reference, and it covers the 2010 and 2012 ETS episodes. Two consequences are carried through the whole report:

* Bostock is incomplete, so a real LFE he missed is labelled 0 in training. The measured likelihood ratio is therefore an **under**estimate of the true real/false separation, and `p_real` is conservative in the high tail.
* The training prior is the 2012 ETS-day detection population (**π = 0.395**). For a population with a different realness prior — inter-ETS above all — the level must be re-based; `p_real_prior` does that with independently measured priors and is **reported, never validated**.

Labels are never features. Context (tremor_flag, quiet_flag, season, epoch) is never a feature either: with Bostock having no inter-ETS events, a context feature would let the model answer 'inter-ETS ⇒ not real' by construction and destroy the very use case.

## 1. Features (18)

| group | features | where they come from |
|---|---|---|
| independent-site breadth | `n_sites_above` (site z ≥ 3), `frac_sites_above`, `z_site_med`, `z_site_min`, `z_ex_top` | a **per-site re-run of the frozen matched filter** (`scripts/e_persite.py`): the mergeB within-site weighted-mean cc series of every site, scored in units of THAT site's own family-day MAD |
| independent confirmation | `conf_z`, `conf_ze`, `ze_site_med` | the **2 sites with the smallest template-weight share** for that family — the ones that contributed least to triggering: their own cc z, and the S-window (4 s) over pre-arrival (4 s) energy excess at the predicted moveout, in units of that site's own daily distribution |
| burst clustering | `n_same10`, `n_same60`, `n_other5`, `burst_z` | same-family detections within ±10 / ±60 min, OTHER-family detections within ±5 min, and the excess of `n_same60` over the Poisson expectation of that family-day's own rate. Computed on the WHOLE record at once, so no detection loses neighbours at a month boundary |
| physics | `net_mad`, `net_cc`, `rel_amp`, `hfr_rel`, `n_sites_obs`, `wsh_pgc` | Track C columns. `hfr_rel` = log10 HF ratio **relative to its own month's median**, because the PGC BH→HH change moves the absolute ratio (Track C README caveat) and an absolute `hfr` would make the score epoch-dependent by instrument, not by physics |

Monotone constraints (gradient boosting): `net_mad` +, `n_sites_above` +, `frac_sites_above` +, `conf_z` +, `conf_ze` +, `burst_z` +, `hfr_rel` −.

**Self-check on the per-site pass**: the weighted sum of the recomputed per-site series at every stored detection index reproduces Track C's stored `net_cc` to **≤ 9e-8** (float32 epsilon) in every month and both referee years — the decomposition is the production network sum, not a re-measurement.

## 3. VALIDATION on 2010 — the verdict

* **Discrimination transfers.** 2012 grouped-CV AUC 0.894 → **2010 AUC 0.855** on a network that is three sites smaller (9 sites in 2012, 6 in 2010).
* **The pre-registered calibration bar FAILS.** Largest deviation in a bin with ≥ 30 events: **0.261** (bar ±0.10). Every bin deviates in the SAME direction — `p_real` over-predicts throughout.
* **It fails in LEVEL, not in SHAPE.** One free scalar — a log-odds anchor fitted on 2010 — brings the largest deviation to **0.060**, inside the bar in every bin. The anchor is -0.926 in log-odds; the population-prior difference alone accounts for -0.356 of it, so about 62% of the shift is a genuine **network-strength** effect: the same feature vector is less likely to be a real LFE on a 6-site network than on a 9-site one.
* **Operationally, precision at the delivered cuts is measured directly on 2010**: p ≥ 0.5 → 0.587 (5,102 events, recall 0.807); **p ≥ 0.8 → 0.743** (2,630, recall 0.526); p ≥ 0.9 → 0.837 (1,344, recall 0.303).

**Verdict: the calibration bar is NOT MET as pre-registered.** What is delivered is a score whose ORDERING is validated on a held-out year and whose absolute level is bracketed: `p_real` is exact on a 9-site ETS network and over-predicts on a 6-site one by a measured -0.93 in log-odds. `p_real_cons` applies that 2010 anchor and is the conservative reading. Production epochs carry 7–9 sites, i.e. between the two.

Three artifact hypotheses were tested and none explains it:

1. **A feature that leans on winter.** The per-feature seasonal medians (`seasonal_feature_shift.csv`) show the two largest shifts are `n_sites_obs` (+1 site in DJF, +0.47 sd — winter simply has more stations up) and `wsh_pgc` (-0.46 sd), while every feature normalised by the day's OWN background (`z_site_med`, `conf_z`, `z_site_min`, `frac_sites_above`) is LOWER in winter — the same direction as Track D's moveout-shuffled test. Resolving it needs a seasonally unbiased statistic that neither track currently has.

### 8d. A data-quality finding handed back to Track C

The per-site pass re-derives each site's own normalised cc, which cannot exceed 1. On **18 station-days** — 2014-11-18, 2014-12-06, 2014-12-27, 2017-02-04, 2018-03-28, 2018-03-30, … — one station (mostly UW.LRIV before it was dropped on 2019-05-30, also CN.SNB, CN.VGZ, CN.NLLB) has a near-zero-energy interval that slips past the energy floor and its cc blows up to ~1e11. **486 detections** carry such a site; **59 of them are in catalog v1, all flagged `cat_core` AND `cat_ext`**, some with `cc` > 1 (e.g. 2.34) and `net_mad` up to 136. Track C's own stored `net_cc` carries the identical blow-up, so this is not a discrepancy with production — it is a floor that is too permissive. Track E flags these rows (`persite_bad`) and leaves them UNSCORED. They are listed in `results/trackE/bad_persite_detections.csv` for Track C/D.

## 10. Honest limits

1. **The calibration bar is not met** (§3). Ordering transfers; absolute level is network-dependent and is delivered as a bracket (`p_real` / `p_real_cons`).
2. **`p_real` answers 'is this a Bostock-matchable own-family event', not 'is this a real LFE'.** The two differ wherever Bostock is incomplete, which is everywhere outside his ETS windows — that is exactly the inter-ETS regime the score is wanted for. `p_real_prior` bridges the gap with Track A's measured priors and cannot itself be validated.
3. **Quiet-day detections are training negatives with ~5–22 % label noise** (step 3b). Real inter-ETS repeats are therefore pushed DOWN by the training signal; the inter-ETS retained fractions in §7 are lower bounds on what a perfectly labelled model would keep.
4. **`hfr` after 2017-08-10 is instrument-shifted** (Track C caveat). It enters only as a within-month relative quantity, and it is the least important feature (§4), so the exposure is small — but it is not zero.
5. **The burst features are computed on the detection population, which contains false alarms.** A family-day whose false alarms cluster will earn burst credit. The moveout-shuffled evidence in Track D §1d says inter-ETS clustering is partly non-family-specific.

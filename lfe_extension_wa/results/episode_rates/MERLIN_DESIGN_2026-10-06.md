## Design (one for WA and SVI)
1. Exposure + episodes (tremor only, fixed before counting): per family-day n_trem30 (WA has post/tremor_family_day.parquet; SVI must build from PNSN + Bostock hypocentres). Regional episode = run of days with ≥ 10 % of families exposed, gaps ≤ 3 d bridged, ≥ 5 d; MAJOR if family-exposure-days ≥ 0.25 × N_fam × 14 (NW ≈ 460, SVI ≈ 400). Pre-2009-08-06 WA = WECC windows. Per-family window = exposure days ± 2 d. Acceptance: every regional entry of tremor_episodes.csv is in the major list, else revise thresholds before counting.
2. Per (family, episode) table, same columns both regions: N at 0.75 (WA minus time-reversed, SVI minus scrambled), N_scr, N at 0.5/0.85/0.9, ΣP, X = Σ n_trem30, D exposure days, active days, onset/offset lags, live-carrier days, in-sample flag, station-change flag (±45 d). SVI: no extension day in-sample (state it).
3. Consistency, k-free, within one station set: primary multinomial share test (expected N = family share × total of families within 40 km, ≥ 5 neighbours), χ²/df + parametric bootstrap p, BH q as column; secondary Poisson dispersion vs ρ_f × X. "Exposure-explained" if secondary < 2 while primary > 3. No test when ΣN < 20 (still listed).
4. Artefact vs physical: residual correlation vs log-distance and carrier-Jaccard (permutation p); per (family, episode) labels "spatially coherent" / "station-coincident" (pre-registered rules). SVI adds fixed-S13 ratio per episode (> 2σ → "set-affected"). WA fixed-10 subset re-run (B001, B003–B007, B013, B017, B018, GNW) only if station-coincident steps show across eras.
5. Cross-era bridging as columns only (WA corrected k; SVI S13 counts). SVI: PGC changes 2015-01-12 and 2017-08-10 (detectability stepped up) — potential step.
6. Pre-registered sanity checks: (a) build/test ratio on out-of-sample months 0.85–1.15; (b) control ≤ 2 % of data in every major episode; (c) Spearman(log regional N, log regional X) ≥ 0.5 across major episodes within an era, else detectability dominates → pause step 3.

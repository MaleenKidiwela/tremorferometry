## B018 (PB.B018, 46.98, -123.02) — Phase-1 done, awaiting densify confirm
- Borehole EHZ 100 Hz, **active 2006→now**, ~9 km from CPW (same site, different instrument). Fills CPW's post-2022 gap.
- 764,959 candidates → **24,518 families** (17× CPW! borehole is quieter → more candidates/clusters), but **only 3 SNR≥10**.
- **KEY LESSON — template SNR is SENSOR-DEPENDENT; the SNR≥10 floor is wrong for borehole.** B018 SNR dist is
  COMPRESSED (median 3.2, 90th 4.5, max 11) vs CPW surface (median 3.6, 90th **19**, max 39). Borehole LFE templates
  are less impulsive (more in-band pre-pulse energy) → peak/pre-pulse-RMS SNR sits on a lower scale. Families are
  REAL (same 3-member/3-year repeater criteria). **Fix: adapt the floor per sensor** — B018 SNR≥6 (302 pool) ≈ CPW
  SNR≥10 (286 pool) → coverage-selected **56 families**, 11/12 az sectors, all rings, south to 46.15°N (best southern
  coverage yet). So: don't hard-code SNR≥10; choose the floor from the station's own SNR distribution (e.g. ~top 1%,
  or 90th-percentile-based). Low template SNR does NOT preclude dv/v — the dv/v uses daily-stack coda, not template SNR.

## Lessons learned today
1. **Metadata-overlay QC is essential and catches bad-SENSOR eras, not just rate/sensor STEPS.** CPW's ROCK1 era
   would look like a dramatic real velocity history without it. Always run `plot_dvv_metadata.py` per station.
   Same-type sensor *swaps* (different serials) can still wreck a record if a unit is faulty — per-era won't save it.
2. **Check station lifetime before promising "to current."** CPW died 2022-07; verify FDSN epoch end-dates up front
   (`get_stations` end_date) so "1996-current" isn't a false expectation. PB borehole (B-codes) are the active
   replacements at many decommissioned UW short-period sites.
3. **Generalized the downloader** → `scripts/download_station.py --network --station --start --end --client`
   (was HDW-hardcoded). For PB borehole use `--client EARTHSCOPE`; channel pref EHZ>HHZ>BHZ>SHZ picks the borehole EHZ.
4. **Borehole + surface co-located pairs** (B018 over CPW) can stitch a longer clean record — surface L-4C clean
   eras + borehole active era — if cross-instrument referencing is handled (per-era / overlap calibration).
5. Delete raw waveforms after processing (re-downloadable); keep detections/stacks/dv/v. NFS has TBs free, but tidy.

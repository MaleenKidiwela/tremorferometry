## Grade definitions (the gates)
- **GOLD** = `coda_sigma > station fake-MAX` (beats every reversed-template fake at that station).
- **TRUSTED** = `coda_sigma > fake-95th` AND `daynight_cc > 0.3`.
- **FAIL** = `coda_sigma < fake-median`.
- **UNDETERMINED** = everything in between.
(`coda_sigma` = coherence of the +2..+4 s coda stack in σ above a random-time null. `daynight_cc` =
day-vs-night coda correlation = source-independence.)

## ⚠ Critical caveats (do not skip when using these)
1. **GOLD/TRUSTED means "real coherent LFE", NOT "deep".** T1 separates real repeating families from
   noise; it does not certify depth. Even high-σ GOLD families can be shallow site-carriers.
2. **Depth/shallow split = the separate T2a layer** (`results/family_verdicts/exp_t2a_<STA>_fixed.csv`,
   stations B018/B927/B941). `cls` = SHALLOW-COUPLED (site-carrier) / DECOUPLED (deep-or-noise) /
   ANOMALOUS. T1 σ is **orthogonal** to shallow-coupling → can't σ-weight out site-carriers; the deep
   inversion input must be T2a-filtered. Only ~1/4 stations have a usable ambient monitor for T2a.
3. **Deep velocity CHANGE = an upper bound (~0.02%)**, not a positive detection.

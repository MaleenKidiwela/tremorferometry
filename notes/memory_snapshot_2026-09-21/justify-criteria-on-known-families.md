**Why:** the day of failed north controls came from clauses we added (survival gates, quiet-day FA thresholds, coincidence rules, site merges) that Bostock never used; the same habit crept into the Washington screen with fixed numbers (0.1/day, 3×, 8 km).
**How to apply:** (1) the discovery chain is the published recipe, nothing added; (2) any post-hoc screen is allowed only if it passes ≥85 % of the 58 blind-rediscovered Bostock families and rejects most of the north noise population (results/SCREEN_VALIDATION_NORTH.md is the template for this test); (3) prefer data-driven forms (shuffle/permutation p-values, distributions measured on true families) over fixed constants; (4) report results with and without each clause. Quiet-hour comparisons remain legitimate as a DIAGNOSTIC (tremor-vs-quiet pair ratio: north 11×, WA all-station 0.9×). See [[reproduce-known-answer-first]].

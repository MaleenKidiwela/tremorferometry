Two findings from the B935 detection-level cleaning experiment (2026-06-11), Fable-audited (logic-supervisor).

**1. Detection cleaning by coda-vs-time-mean is CIRCULAR — do NOT use it to "sharpen" dv/v.**
The cleaning kept detections whose 2-4s coda correlated (>0.20) with the family ALL-TIME-MEAN coda; but the dv/v reference is ALSO the all-time mean (`ref=Rf.mean(0)` in dvv_roll30cal.py). So it discards the detections least like the time-mean, then measures each day's deviation from the time-mean — pre-removing the stretch signal it then measures. Result: scatter AND seasonal amplitude fell for ALL families, MOST for FAIL (the circularity control firing correctly). cc_max→0.98 is a direct readout of the selection criterion, NOT a quality gain (my compare script's "GOLD should gain cc_max" success line was itself the confound). Falling dvv_std on a 30-cal-day rolling product is doubly misleading (adjacent points ~97% shared; not an honest per-day noise estimate). **Why:** coherence-to-mean selection ≠ noise removal. **How to apply:** never select detections on coda-vs-reference before measuring stretch; the real cleaning lever is the DETECTOR REBUILD (3-component + network coincidence), not post-hoc selection.

**2. The trust-battery rollout ([[trust-battery-rollout-2026-06-11]]) ran T1 ONLY — and T2a is a SITE filter, NOT a depth certificate (Fable-corrected 2026-06-11).**
T1 (coda coherence + day/night independence vs reversed fakes) certifies a REAL coherent LFE, NOT deep signal. Coherence ≠ depth — a coherent SHALLOW site-carrier passes T1 too (B018: high-σ GOLD families c554/c891/c285/c41 are shallow-coupled). **T2a (regress family 2-4s dv/v on the station Z-autocorrelation dv/v, Theil-Sen slope; scripts/exp_b935_t2a_slope.py, exp_t2a_B018.py)** measures only whether a family's dv/v TIME SERIES tracks the shallow signal = a CONTAMINATION/SITE filter. It does NOT certify deep KERNEL (geometry: does the coda sample the deep source) NOR deep CHANGE (temporal). Do NOT read slope≈⅓ as "deep-certified" — that conflates the kernel's geometric ⅓ receiver-lobe with a temporal-tracking slope. ⚠ T2a IMPLEMENTATION BUGS to fix before use (Fable): (C1) CIs in data/exp_t2a_B018_slope.csv are computed on the RAW slope but slope_des is reported — mismatched, don't quote; (C2) CIs ~5× too tight — n≈3600 daily pts but data are 30-day rolling (~120 indep) → need ≥30-day BLOCK BOOTSTRAP; (C3) ~⅓ of B018 GOLD have NEGATIVE slope (impossible for a mixing share) = noise-around-zero. B935 T2a uninformative (ACF monitor dead: cc median 0.39).
T2a-FIXED RESULT (B018, scripts/exp_t2a_fixed.py, data/exp_t2a_B018_fixed.csv): of 39 GOLD families, 21 SHALLOW-COUPLED (candidate site-carriers, slope CI>0), 15 DECOUPLED (deep-or-noise), 3 ANOMALOUS (neg slope). 8 CONFIDENT site-carriers (R²≥0.4) incl. high-σ c554/c891/c1036/c285. **CRITICAL for inversion: T1 σ-score is ORTHOGONAL to shallow-coupling — Spearman(σ,slope)=−0.08 p=0.61; shallow-coupled median σ 22.7 ≈ decoupled 23.6. So you CANNOT σ-weight out site-carriers; the inversion input MUST be T2a-filtered (remove/down-weight SHALLOW-COUPLED), not just trust-ranked.**
SHALLOW-MONITOR SCARCITY (2026-06-12, kept-station test B941/B927 via scripts/test_kept_stations_shallow.sh): the template-free ambient ACF (the clean shallow monitor, scripts/autocorr_dvv.py) is USABLE at only 1 of 4 stations measured — B018 cc median 0.94 (usable); B941 0.52 (weak); B927 0.41 (dead); B935 0.39 (dead). Family common-mode does NOT predict ACF quality (B941 cm 0.64 & B927 cm 0.50 both looked promising but have weak/dead ACFs) — the common-mode proxy OVERpredicts shallow usability. Good single-station ambient ACF appears to be the EXCEPTION at these boreholes (B018 unusually well-coupled). NOT regional/predictable: B941 (lat 47.10) is a same-latitude NEIGHBOR of B018 (47.05) yet weak — ACF quality is STATION-IDIOSYNCRATIC, no predictor (region, LFE-richness, common-mode all fail); must compute ACF per station (needs waveforms) to find usable ones. CONSEQUENCE: both the SHALLOW MAP and the T2a depth-filter need a coherent ambient monitor, which most stations lack → clean depth/shallow discrimination only at B018-class stations; can't apply T2a network-wide as hoped. T2a at weak-ACF stations is unreliable (coupling there is likely shared seasonal, not clean shallow). Open: network-wide fraction with usable ACF unknown (only 4 measured; most waveforms deleted).

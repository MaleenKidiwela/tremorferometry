### Lessons this run (non-PB / surface)
- **"Surface broadband ⇒ high SNR floor 8-14" did NOT hold for COR or COLT.** Both gave borehole-like compressed SNR (median ~3.2). The high-SNR surface signature (B019 median 6.6) is about IMPULSIVE near-surface short-periods; a quiet GSN vault broadband (COR) and an HF-weighted accelerometer (COLT) both produce LOW-SNR emergent deep-LFE templates. **Always compute the floor from the station's own SNR distribution — never assume by sensor class.** COR floor 6, COLT floor 4.
- **download_station.py extended** (backward-compatible): added `--location` (e.g. `10`, or `blank` for ''; note argparse eats `--`, use `blank`) and `--channel`/`--channel-pref` (e.g. `EN?` accelerometer) so non-`?HZ`-velocity and multi-location-code stations can be targeted. **plot_dvv_metadata.py** got `--channel` (default `*HZ`; pass `EN?` for accelerometer-only sites) — else FDSN 204s on stations with no HZ channel.
- **Metadata epochs ≠ archived data** (COR `10 HHZ`): probe per-year actual `get_waveforms` availability before committing a channel.
- COLT is **one-sided westward/downdip** (mean az 271°, dist 48-121 km — inland east of the band, like B941/B201). COR is two-sided, on-band (dist 4-102 km). COLT's coverage is intrinsically sparse (accelerometer detects few families) — flagged.
- **FLAG for human:** COLT being accelerometer-only is a methodological caveat — the dv/v is internally valid but not directly comparable in absolute amplitude/noise to the velocity-sensor stations; worth a human decision on whether to include COLT in the cross-station tomography or treat it as lower-tier. No data-quality red flag otherwise (clean, flat, cc 0.979).
